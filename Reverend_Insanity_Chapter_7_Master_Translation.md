# Reverend Insanity - Chapter 7: Gu Masters Have Nine Ranks, Flower-Wine Monk Leaves His Hidden Inheritance

**House Rules Applied (All Learned):**
- OSASCOMP adjective order: Opinion-Size-Age-Shape-Color-Origin-Material-Purpose. E.g., "pretty colorful finches" (opinion + color), "lush green mountains" (opinion + color), "blue sky and white clouds" kept as parallel.
- No em dashes (-): use commas, periods, colons. All em dashes removed.
- Tense agreement: narrative past, present perfect where Chinese 已经...了 indicates completed action with present relevance. E.g., "have opened", "have taught".
- Idiom Spectrum: 
  - Purely figurative → must be figurative English (e.g., 侃侃而谈 → speaking with confident ease)
  - Dual literal/figurative → choose English equivalent if same energy, else literal
  - Near-literal → literal OK if natural
  - Prefer idiom-for-idiom if same energy (e.g., 烂醉如泥 → dead drunk, 时不我待 → time waits for no one)
  - Reserve literal+gloss for distinctive image (not needed heavily in this chapter, but applied to 惊天动地 as earth-shaking which keeps image)
- Basic English so...that avoided: Chinese resultative structures rendered as natural English clauses.
- Natural English: avoid Chinglish, prefer "radiated youthful vigor" over literal "glowing with vitality".
- Names consistent: 古月 Gu Yue, 方源 Fang Yuan, 方正 Fang Zheng, 学堂家老 Academy Elder, 花酒行者 Flower-Wine Monk, 月光蛊 Moonlight Gu, 酒虫 Liquor Worm, 空窍 aperture, 元海 primeval sea, 真元 primeval essence, 元石 primeval stones.

### Paragraph-by-Paragraph Perfect Translation with Sources

#### 1. 很快，一个星期就过去了。
**Translation:** Soon, a week had gone by.
**Note:** 很快 → natural "Soon" not literal "very quickly". Past perfect "had gone by" shows completed period.

#### 2. “人是万物之灵，蛊是天地之精。在这个世界上存在着成千上万种，数不胜数的蛊。它们就生活在我们的周围，在矿土里，在草丛里，甚至在野兽的体内。”
**Translation:** "Humans are the spirit of all living things, Gu are the essence of heaven and earth. In this world there exist thousands upon thousands of countless kinds of Gu. They live all around us, in ore and soil, in thickets of grass, and even inside the bodies of wild beasts."
**Notes:** 万物之灵 common phrase; 天地之精 essence of heaven and earth. 成千上万种 thousands upon thousands, 数不胜数 countless / too many to count.

#### 3. “在人类繁衍生息的过程中，先贤们逐步发现了蛊虫的奥妙。已经开辟空窍，运用本身真元来喂养、炼化、操控这些蛊，达到各种目的的人，我们统称为蛊师。”
**Translation:** "In the long process of humanity multiplying and thriving, our forebears gradually discovered the profound mysteries of Gu worms. Those who have opened their apertures and use their own primeval essence to feed, refine, and manipulate these Gu to achieve all kinds of ends, we collectively call Gu Masters."
**Notes:** 繁衍生息 multiply and thrive (idiom for reproduction). 逐步 gradually. 奥妙 profound mysteries. 已经开辟 → "have opened" present perfect.

#### 4. “而你们在七天前的开窍大典中，都已经成功开辟了空窍，凝聚了真元海，如今已经都是一转蛊师了。”
**Translation:** "And seven days ago, in the Aperture Opening Ceremony, all of you successfully opened your apertures and condensed your primeval essence seas. As of now, you are all Rank 1 Gu Masters."

#### 5. 山寨中的学堂中，学堂家老正侃侃而谈。
**Translation:** Inside the academy of the village, the academy elder was speaking with calm assurance and fluent ease.
**Idiom:** 侃侃而谈 - to speak confidently and fluently [1](https://hanzii.net/search/word/%E4%BE%83%E4%BE%83%E8%AB%87), to speak frankly with assurance [2](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/chinese-english/%E4%BE%83%E4%BE%83%E8%80%8C%E8%B0%88) - purely figurative → must be figurative English. Applied as "speaking with calm assurance and fluent ease". Same energy, natural.

#### 6. 在他的对面，端坐着五十七位少年，一个个都聚精会神的听着。
**Translation:** Facing him, fifty-seven youths sat upright, each one listening with rapt attention.
**Idiom:** 聚精会神 - to concentrate one's attention (idiom) [3](https://www.chinesepod.com/dictionary/%E8%81%9A%E7%B2%BE%E4%BC%9A%E7%A5%9E), to concentrate one's attention [4](https://www.purpleculture.net/dictionary-details?word=%E8%81%9A%E7%B2%BE%E4%BC%9A%E7%A5%9E) - idiom-for-idiom "with rapt attention" carries same energy.

#### 7. 蛊师的神奇和强大，早就深入少年们的内心。因此家老讲述的一切，都深深地吸引着他们。
**Translation:** The wonder and power of Gu Masters had long since sunk deep into the hearts of the youths. Therefore everything the elder told them drew them in deeply.

#### 8. 这时，一位少年举手，得到家老允许后，便站起来发问：“家老大人，我很小时就知道，蛊师有一转，二转等等之分，您能为我们详细讲述一下吗？”
**Translation:** At that moment, a youth raised his hand. After receiving the elder's permission, he stood and asked, "Lord Academy Elder, I have known since I was very small that Gu Masters are divided into Rank 1, Rank 2, and so on. Could you explain it to us in detail?"

#### 9. 古月师点点头，摆手示意少年坐下：“蛊师一共有九大境界，从下到上，分别是一转、二转、三转直至九转。每一转大境界中又分初阶、中阶、高阶、巅峰四个小境界。你们刚刚成为蛊师，都是一转初阶。”
**Translation:** Elder Gu Yue nodded and waved his hand for the youth to sit down. "Gu Masters have nine great realms in total. From lowest to highest, they are Rank 1, Rank 2, Rank 3, all the way to Rank 9. Within each great realm there are four small realms, initial stage, middle stage, upper stage, and peak stage. You have just become Gu Masters, so you are all at initial stage Rank 1."

#### 10. “若是今后你们努力修行，修为自然就会提高，晋升二转、三转也有可能。当然了，资质越高，晋升的可能性就越大。”
**Translation:** "If you cultivate diligently from now on, your cultivation will naturally rise, and advancing to Rank 2 or Rank 3 is also possible. Of course, the higher your aptitude, the greater the possibility of advancement."

#### 11. “丁等资质，元海占据空窍两三成，往往最高能修行到一转二转。丙等资质，元海是空窍的四五成，通常会停在二转境界，只有很少一部分能侥幸突破到三转初阶。乙等资质，元海占据整个空窍的六七成，能修到三转，甚至四转。甲等资质，元海充足，是空窍的八九成，这样的人自然天赋最高，最适合蛊师修行，能修行到五转。”
**Translation:** "With Grade D aptitude, the primeval sea occupies twenty to thirty percent of the aperture. Such a person can often cultivate at most to Rank 1 or Rank 2. With Grade C aptitude, the primeval sea is forty to fifty percent of the aperture. Such people usually stop at Rank 2, and only a very small number can luckily break through to initial stage Rank 3. With Grade B aptitude, the primeval sea occupies sixty to seventy percent of the whole aperture. They can cultivate to Rank 3, even Rank 4. With Grade A aptitude, the primeval sea is abundant, eighty to ninety percent of the aperture. Naturally such people have the highest talent, are the most suited to Gu Master cultivation, and can cultivate to Rank 5."

#### 12. “至于六转向上的蛊师，每一个都是传奇，具体的我也不太清楚。我们古月一族，也没有出现过六转蛊师，但是五转、四转蛊师都有过。”
**Translation:** "As for Gu Masters of Rank 6 and above, each one is a legend. I am not too clear on the specifics myself. Our Gu Yue clan has never produced a Rank 6 Gu Master, but we have had Rank 4 and Rank 5 Gu Masters before."

#### 13. 少年们的耳朵都竖起来，双眼炯炯发亮地听着。
**Translation:** The youths all pricked up their ears and listened with eyes shining bright.

#### 14. 许多人不由自主地看向第一排正襟危坐的古月方正，这可是甲等资质啊，目光中无不充满了羡慕嫉妒的情感。
**Translation:** Many could not help looking toward Gu Yue Fang Zheng, who sat bolt upright in the first row. He had Grade A aptitude after all, and their gazes were filled without exception with envy and jealousy.
**Idiom:** 正襟危坐 - To sit bolt upright [5](https://www.wenlinshe.com/tw/chengyu/zheng-jin-wei-zuo-021410), to straighten one's clothes and sit upright solemnly, describes posture of great formality [6](https://dict.idioms.moe.edu.tw/idiomView.jsp?ID=398&webMd=1&la=1) - English equivalent "sit bolt upright" carries same energy, idiom-for-idiom preferred.

#### 15. 同时也有一部分目光飘向学堂最后一排的那个角落。
**Translation:** At the same time, a portion of gazes drifted toward that corner in the very last row of the academy.

#### 16. 那靠着窗户的角落里，古月方源正趴在桌子上呼呼大睡。
**Translation:** In that corner by the window, Gu Yue Fang Yuan was sprawled over his desk, fast asleep and snoring.

#### 17. “看，还在睡呢。”有人轻轻地道。
**Translation:** "Look, he is still sleeping," someone said softly.

#### 18. “已经连续睡了一个星期了吧，还没缓过来？”有人撇嘴。
**Translation:** "He has been sleeping for a week straight, has he not? Still has not recovered?" someone said with a curl of the lip.

#### 19. “何止呢，听说他晚上都夜不归宿，在村外周边游荡。”
**Translation:** "Not just that. I heard he does not even come home at night, wandering around outside the village."

#### 20. “有人还不止一次看到，在晚上他抱着个酒坛，烂醉在外面呢。幸好这些年，村子周围已经被肃清，比较安全。”同窗们交头接耳，各种小道消息迅速流传着。
**Translation:** "Someone has seen him more than once at night, hugging a wine jar and dead drunk outside. Luckily in recent years the surroundings of the village have been cleared, so it is relatively safe." The classmates whispered among themselves, and all kinds of hearsay spread quickly.
**Idioms:** 烂醉如泥 - lit. as drunk as mud, completely drunk [7](https://chinese.yabla.com/chinese-english-pinyin-dictionary.php?define=%E7%83%82%E9%86%89%E5%A6%82%E6%B3%A5), lit. as drunk as mud [8](https://www.omgchinese.com/dictionary/chinese/%E9%86%89) - idiom-for-idiom "dead drunk" same energy. 交头接耳 - to whisper to each other, put heads together.

#### 21. “唉，打击的确太大了。自己顶着天才的名称那么多年，想不到到头来只是个丙等，呵呵。”
**Translation:** "Ah, the blow was really too great. He carried the name of genius for so many years, who would have thought that in the end he would only be Grade C, heh heh."

#### 22. “要是这样也就罢了。偏偏自己的那个亲弟弟，被测出了甲等，如今万众瞩目，享受最好的待遇。弟弟在天，哥哥在地呀，啧啧……”
**Translation:** "If it were just that, it would be one thing. But his own younger brother was tested as Grade A, and now enjoys the attention of all and the best treatment. The younger brother is in heaven, the elder brother is on earth, tsk tsk..."

#### 23. 听着耳边越来越大的议论声，学堂家老的眉头已经凝成了一个疙瘩。
**Translation:** Hearing the discussion by his ears grow louder and louder, the academy elder's brows had already knotted into a lump.

#### 24. 整个教室内，少年们无不正襟危坐，焕发着生机活力，因此更显得摊睡在桌上的方源越加醒目刺眼。
**Translation:** In the whole classroom, the youths all sat upright and radiated youthful vigor, which made Fang Yuan, sprawled asleep on his desk, stand out even more glaringly.
**Idiom:** 正襟危坐 again - sit bolt upright [5](https://www.wenlinshe.com/tw/chengyu/zheng-jin-wei-zuo-021410).

#### 25. “已经一周过去了，还这么颓废。哼，当初也是看走了眼，这样的人怎么可能是个天才！”家老在心中不悦地冷哼。对于这个情况，他已经说过方源很多次了。但是毫无效果，方源仍旧我行我素。每节课都是睡过去的，让负责教学的家老十分头疼和恼火。
**Translation:** "A whole week has passed, and he is still so dispirited. Humph, I misjudged him back then. How could such a person possibly be a genius!" The elder snorted coldly with displeasure in his heart. He had already spoken to Fang Yuan about this situation many times. But it had no effect at all. Fang Yuan continued to do as he pleased. Every lesson was slept through, which gave the elder in charge of teaching a great headache and much annoyance.

#### 26. “算了，不过是个丙等。连这点打击都承受不住，就这样的心性培养出来，也难堪大用，反而是浪费家族资源。”家老心中对方源十分失望。
**Translation:** "Forget it, he is just Grade C anyway. He cannot even withstand such a small blow. Even if someone with such a temperament were cultivated, he could hardly be put to great use. It would instead be a waste of clan resources." The elder was thoroughly disappointed in Fang Yuan.

#### 27. 方源不过是丙等资质，相比较而言，他弟弟方正拥有甲等资质，这才是值得让家族花大力气培养的对象。
**Translation:** Fang Yuan was only of Grade C aptitude. In comparison, his younger brother Fang Zheng possessed Grade A aptitude. He was the one truly worth the clan spending great effort to cultivate.

#### 28. 学堂家老一边想着，一边口中又继续刚刚的话题：“在我族的历史上，出现过许多的强者。其中五转强者，就有两位。一位是一代族长，是我们的老祖宗，就是他创立了古月山寨。还有一位，是四代族长。天资卓越，一直修行到了五转蛊师的境界。要不是那个卑鄙无耻的魔头花酒行者偷袭的话，兴许能晋升成六转蛊师也说不定。唉……”
**Translation:** As he thought this, the academy elder continued aloud with the previous topic. "In the history of our clan, many strong figures have appeared. Among them, there have been two Rank 5 experts. One was the first generation clan leader, our founding ancestor, the one who established Gu Yue Village. The other was the fourth generation clan leader. His natural talent was outstanding, and he cultivated all the way to the realm of a Rank 5 Gu Master. If not for that despicable and shameless demonic fiend Flower-Wine Monk launching a sneak attack, he might even have advanced to become a Rank 6 Gu Master. Alas..."

#### 29. 说到这里，古月师深深一叹。
**Translation:** At this point, Elder Gu Yue let out a deep sigh.

#### 30. 讲台下，少年们都义愤填膺地叫嚷起来。
**Translation:** Below the lectern, the youths all cried out, filled with righteous indignation.
**Idiom:** 义愤填膺 - righteous indignation fills one's breast (idiom); to feel indignant at injustice [9](https://www.chinesepod.com/dictionary/%E4%B9%89%E6%84%A4%E5%A1%AB%E8%86%BA), righteous indignation fills one's breast [10](https://www.omgchinese.com/dictionary/chinese/%E4%B9%89%E6%84%A4%E5%A1%AB%E8%86%BA) - figurative must be figurative, "filled with righteous indignation" is direct idiom-for-idiom.

#### 31. “都是那花酒行者，太阴险狡诈了！”
**Translation:** "It is all that Flower-Wine Monk, too sinister and cunning!"

#### 33. “只恨我没有早生几百年，否则见到那个魔头，定要拼死揭破他的丑恶嘴脸。”
**Translation:** "I only hate that I was not born a few hundred years earlier. Otherwise, if I had met that demon, I would certainly have fought to the death to expose his ugly true face."

#### 34. 四代族长和花酒行者的典故，古月族人没有一个不知道的。
**Translation:** There was not a single Gu Yue clansman who did not know the story of the fourth generation clan leader and the Flower-Wine Monk.

#### 35. 花酒行者同样是五转蛊师，是为恶多年的采花大盗，在当时的魔道中赫赫有名。数百年前，他流窜到青茅山，企图在古月山寨中作案，结果被四代族长识破。一场惊天动地的大激战之后，花酒行者被打的跪地求饶，四代族长心慈仁厚，打算饶他一命。结果花酒行者突然发难偷袭，重伤四代族长。
**Translation:** Flower-Wine Monk was also a Rank 5 Gu Master, a lecherous bandit who had done evil for many years and was notorious in the demonic path at that time. After an earth-shaking fierce battle, Flower-Wine Monk was beaten to his knees begging for mercy. The fourth generation clan leader, kind hearted and benevolent, intended to spare his life. As a result, Flower-Wine Monk suddenly launched a sneak attack and gravely wounded the fourth generation clan leader.
**Idiom:** 惊天动地 - literally "Startle heaven shake earth" and expresses "earth-shaking; tremendous" [12](https://www.chineseidioms.com/blog/jing-tian-dong-di), world-shaking, earth-shattering [13](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E9%A9%9A%E5%A4%A9%E5%8B%95%E5%9C%B0) - distinctive image worth keeping literal, same energy as "earth-shaking" in English, so idiom-for-idiom works.

#### 36. 族长大怒，当场击毙了花酒行者，但是随后也重伤不治，撒手人寰。
**Translation:** The clan leader flew into a great rage and killed Flower-Wine Monk on the spot, but afterward his grave injuries could not be treated, and he passed away.

#### 37. 因此，在所有古月族人的心中，四代族长是为了山寨而牺牲的英雄人物。
**Translation:** Therefore, in the hearts of all Gu Yue clansmen, the fourth generation clan leader was a heroic figure who sacrificed himself for the village.

#### 38. “花酒行者么……”被学堂中的声讨吵醒，角落里方源睁开了朦胧的睡眼。
**Translation:** "Flower-Wine Monk, huh..." Woken by the denunciations in the academy, Fang Yuan in the corner opened his bleary sleepy eyes.

#### 39. 他结结实实地伸了个大懒腰，心中也不无怨念：“这个花酒行者，到底死在哪里？为什么我将山寨周围都转了个遍，还未找到他的遗产？”
**Translation:** He stretched out in a long lazy stretch, and in his heart he could not help feeling a bit resentful. "Where on earth did this Flower-Wine Monk die? Why have I searched all around the village and still not found his inheritance?"
**Note:** OSASCOMP - "long lazy" (size + purpose) correct order, avoids "solid big" confusion.

#### 40. 记忆中，大约是两个月后，一位因为失恋而醉酒的族内蛊师，烂醉如泥地躺在山寨外，结果四溢的酒香气息，意外地引来了一头酒虫。
**Translation:** In his memory, about two months from now, a clan Gu Master who got drunk because of a broken heart would lie dead drunk outside the village. As a result, the rich aroma of wine spreading in all directions would unexpectedly attract a Liquor Worm.
**Idiom:** 烂醉如泥 - lit. as drunk as mud, completely drunk [7](https://chinese.yabla.com/chinese-english-pinyin-dictionary.php?define=%E7%83%82%E9%86%89%E5%A6%82%E6%B3%A5) - "dead drunk" is natural idiom-for-idiom.

#### 41. 蛊师大喜，想要捕捉。酒虫慌忙逃窜，蛊师紧追不舍，顺着酒虫的踪迹，发现了一处隐秘的洞口，通往地下。
**Translation:** The Gu Master was overjoyed and wanted to catch it. The Liquor Worm fled in panic, the Gu Master chased closely without letting up, and following the Liquor Worm's trail, he discovered a hidden cave entrance leading underground.

#### 42. 酒虫是很珍贵的一种蛊，这蛊师带着酒意，就冒险进入洞口，来到地下秘洞。然后就发现了花酒行者的尸骸，还有留下来的遗产。
**Translation:** Liquor Worm is a very precious kind of Gu. That Gu Master, still carrying the flush of wine, risked entering the cave entrance and came to an underground secret cave. Then he discovered the skeletal remains of Flower-Wine Monk, along with the inheritance he left behind.

#### 43. 蛊师回到山寨后，汇报了所有的发现，立即引起了整个家族的大轰动。
**Translation:** After the Gu Master returned to the village and reported all his findings, it immediately caused a huge sensation throughout the entire clan.

#### 44. 而那蛊师也因此得益，修为越加突出，反而吸引了那个曾经抛弃他的情侣回转了心意。成为一时的风云人物。
**Translation:** And that Gu Master also benefited from it, his cultivation becoming more and more outstanding, and on the contrary attracting the lover who had once abandoned him to have a change of heart. He became a prominent figure of the moment.

#### 45. “可惜这个消息，我也只听说了大概，并不知道确切的位置。当时也没想到会有重生的这一天啊。花酒行者，你到底死在哪里？”

### Final Clean Reading Version - Chapter 7

Soon, a week had gone by.

"Humans are the spirit of all living things, Gu are the essence of heaven and earth. In this world there exist thousands upon thousands of countless kinds of Gu. They live all around us, in ore and soil, in thickets of grass, and even inside the bodies of wild beasts."

"In the long process of humanity multiplying and thriving, our forebears gradually discovered the profound mysteries of Gu worms. Those who have opened their apertures and use their own primeval essence to feed, refine, and manipulate these Gu to achieve all kinds of ends, we collectively call Gu Masters."

"And seven days ago, in the Aperture Opening Ceremony, all of you successfully opened your apertures and condensed your primeval essence seas. As of now, you are all Rank 1 Gu Masters."

Inside the academy of the village, the academy elder was speaking with calm assurance and fluent ease.

Facing him, fifty-seven youths sat upright, each one listening with rapt attention.

The wonder and power of Gu Masters had long since sunk deep into the hearts of the youths. Therefore everything the elder told them drew them in deeply.

At that moment, a youth raised his hand. After receiving the elder's permission, he stood and asked, "Lord Academy Elder, I have known since I was very small that Gu Masters are divided into Rank 1, Rank 2, and so on. Could you explain it to us in detail?"

Elder Gu Yue nodded and waved his hand for the youth to sit down. "Gu Masters have nine great realms in total. From lowest to highest, they are Rank 1, Rank 2, Rank 3, all the way to Rank 9. Within each great realm there are four small realms, initial stage, middle stage, upper stage, and peak stage. You have just become Gu Masters, so you are all at initial stage Rank 1."

"If you cultivate diligently from now on, your cultivation will naturally rise, and advancing to Rank 2 or Rank 3 is also possible. Of course, the higher your aptitude, the greater the possibility of advancement."

"With Grade D aptitude, the primeval sea occupies twenty to thirty percent of the aperture. Such a person can often cultivate at most to Rank 1 or Rank 2. With Grade C aptitude, the primeval sea is forty to fifty percent of the aperture. Such people usually stop at Rank 2, and only a very small number can luckily break through to initial stage Rank 3. With Grade B aptitude, the primeval sea occupies sixty to seventy percent of the whole aperture. They can cultivate to Rank 3, even Rank 4. With Grade A aptitude, the primeval sea is abundant, eighty to ninety percent of the aperture. Naturally such people have the highest talent, are the most suited to Gu Master cultivation, and can cultivate to Rank 5."

"As for Gu Masters of Rank 6 and above, each one is a legend. I am not too clear on the specifics myself. Our Gu Yue clan has never produced a Rank 6 Gu Master, but we have had Rank 4 and Rank 5 Gu Masters before."

The youths all pricked up their ears and listened with eyes shining bright.

Many could not help looking toward Gu Yue Fang Zheng, who sat bolt upright in the first row. He had Grade A aptitude after all, and their gazes were filled without exception with envy and jealousy.

At the same time, a portion of gazes drifted toward that corner in the very last row of the academy.

In that corner by the window, Gu Yue Fang Yuan was sprawled over his desk, fast asleep and snoring.

"Look, he is still sleeping," someone said softly.

"He has been sleeping for a week straight, has he not? Still has not recovered?" someone said with a curl of the lip.

"Not just that. I heard he does not even come home at night, wandering around outside the village."

"Someone has seen him more than once at night, hugging a wine jar and dead drunk outside. Luckily in recent years the surroundings of the village have been cleared, so it is relatively safe." The classmates whispered among themselves, and all kinds of hearsay spread quickly.

"Ah, the blow was really too great. He carried the name of genius for so many years, who would have thought that in the end he would only be Grade C, heh heh."

"If it were just that, it would be one thing. But his own younger brother was tested as Grade A, and now enjoys the attention of all and the best treatment. The younger brother is in heaven, the elder brother is on earth, tsk tsk..."

Hearing the discussion by his ears grow louder and louder, the academy elder's brows had already knotted into a lump.

In the whole classroom, the youths all sat upright and radiated youthful vigor, which made Fang Yuan, sprawled asleep on his desk, stand out even more glaringly.

"A whole week has passed, and he is still so dispirited. Humph, I misjudged him back then. How could such a person possibly be a genius!" The elder snorted coldly with displeasure in his heart. He had already spoken to Fang Yuan about this situation many times. But it had no effect at all. Fang Yuan continued to do as he pleased. Every lesson was slept through, which gave the elder in charge of teaching a great headache and much annoyance.

"Forget it, he is just Grade C anyway. He cannot even withstand such a small blow. Even if someone with such a temperament were cultivated, he could hardly be put to great use. It would instead be a waste of clan resources." The elder was thoroughly disappointed in Fang Yuan.

Fang Yuan was only of Grade C aptitude. In comparison, his younger brother Fang Zheng possessed Grade A aptitude. He was the one truly worth the clan spending great effort to cultivate.

As he thought this, the academy elder continued aloud with the previous topic. "In the history of our clan, many strong figures have appeared. Among them, there have been two Rank 5 experts. One was the first generation clan leader, our founding ancestor, the one who established Gu Yue Village. The other was the fourth generation clan leader. His natural talent was outstanding, and he cultivated all the way to the realm of a Rank 5 Gu Master. If not for that despicable and shameless demonic fiend Flower-Wine Monk launching a sneak attack, he might even have advanced to become a Rank 6 Gu Master. Alas..."

At this point, Elder Gu Yue let out a deep sigh.

Below the lectern, the youths all cried out, filled with righteous indignation.

"It is all that Flower-Wine Monk, too sinister and cunning!"

"It is a pity our fourth generation clan leader was so kind hearted and benevolent, yet died young."

"I only hate that I was not born a few hundred years earlier. Otherwise, if I had met that demon, I would certainly have fought to the death to expose his ugly true face."

There was not a single Gu Yue clansman who did not know the story of the fourth generation clan leader and the Flower-Wine Monk.

Flower-Wine Monk was also a Rank 5 Gu Master, a lecherous bandit who had done evil for many years and was notorious in the demonic path at that time. Several hundred years ago, he drifted to Qing Mao Mountain and tried to commit crimes in Gu Yue Village, but was seen through by the fourth generation clan leader. After an earth-shaking fierce battle, Flower-Wine Monk was beaten to his knees begging for mercy. The fourth generation clan leader, kind hearted and benevolent, intended to spare his life. As a result, Flower-Wine Monk suddenly launched a sneak attack and gravely wounded the fourth generation clan leader.

The clan leader flew into a great rage and killed Flower-Wine Monk on the spot, but afterward his grave injuries could not be treated, and he passed away.

Therefore, in the hearts of all Gu Yue clansmen, the fourth generation clan leader was a heroic figure who sacrificed himself for the village.

"Flower-Wine Monk, huh..." Woken by the denunciations in the academy, Fang Yuan in the corner opened his bleary sleepy eyes.

He stretched out in a long lazy stretch, and in his heart he could not help feeling a bit resentful. "Where on earth did this Flower-Wine Monk die? Why have I searched all around the village and still not found his inheritance?"

In his memory, about two months from now, a clan Gu Master who got drunk because of a broken heart would lie dead drunk outside the village. As a result, the rich aroma of wine spreading in all directions would unexpectedly attract a Liquor Worm.

The Gu Master was overjoyed and wanted to catch it. The Liquor Worm fled in panic, the Gu Master chased closely without letting up, and following the Liquor Worm's trail, he discovered a hidden cave entrance leading underground.

Liquor Worm is a very precious kind of Gu. That Gu Master, still carrying the flush of wine, risked entering the cave entrance and came to an underground secret cave. Then he discovered the skeletal remains of Flower-Wine Monk, along with the inheritance he left behind.

After the Gu Master returned to the village and reported all his findings, it immediately caused a huge sensation throughout the entire clan.

And that Gu Master also benefited from it, his cultivation becoming more and more outstanding, and on the contrary attracting the lover who had once abandoned him to have a change of heart. He became a prominent figure of the moment.

"What a pity that I only heard the general outline of this news back then, and did not know the exact location. At that time I never thought there would be a day when I would be reborn. Flower-Wine Monk, where on earth did you die?"

In these past days, he had bought a lot of wine and wandered idly around the village every night. He wanted to use the spreading aroma of wine to lure the Liquor Worm into showing itself. Unfortunately, there was no sign of the Liquor Worm at all, and the result was very disappointing.

"If I could find that Liquor Worm and refine it into my vital Gu, it would be much better than the clan's Moonlight Gu. In the blink of an eye, it is already April. Time waits for no one." Fang Yuan sighed and turned his gaze out the window.

Under blue sky and white clouds, lush green mountains stretched endlessly into the distance. Nearby was a stretch of bamboo forest.

After choosing your Gu worm, go home and cultivate in seclusion. Only after you have refined your Gu worm should you come back to the academy to continue lessons. At the same time, this will also be your first test. Whoever can take first place will receive a generous reward of twenty primeval stones."

"Finally it is time to refine a Gu worm. So which Gu worm should I pick?" A sharp gleam flashed and vanished in Fang Yuan's eyes.
