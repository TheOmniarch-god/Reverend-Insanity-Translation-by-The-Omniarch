🏔️ The Mountain — 青茅山

青茅山 — Green Thatch Mountain. Vast, tranquil, nourished by spring rain without cease. Not one peak but a range: Gu Yue Village on southern slope, Bai Village northwest, Xiong Village northeast — three-way balance of power. Spirit spring in underground cavern produces primeval stones, foundation of all three villages. Spear bamboo unique to mountain, straight as spearhead. Mountain wind whips blood-soaked garments like war banner. In 3D: 14 vantage points from Qing Mao Peak to Southern Border panorama.

📖 First seen: Chapter 1 p16 春雨绵绵 · Region: Southern Border 南疆 · 3D: x5.5 z-2.8

🏮 Gu Yue Village — 古月山寨

古月 = Ancient Moon — clan surname, also evokes ancient, mysterious. Several thousand stilt houses (not ten thousand, but several thousand nonetheless — approved line), draped as brilliant ribbon of light at night, giving vast mountain range distinct sense of human habitation. Heart: grand pavilion where Aperture Opening Ceremony held. Factions: Mo (莫系) and Chi (赤系) — rival elders Mo Chen and Chi Lian, grandsons Mo Bei and Chi Cheng as heirs. Fang lineage lives under aunt/uncle after parents' death. Through 500-year demon lord eyes: a cage, but sturdy cage is safety. 3D pin: x0.9 z1.1.

📖 Ch1 p18 灯火数千 · Population: Several thousand households · Clan Leader: Fourth Generation, Rank 4

🌀 The Rebirth — 春秋蝉 · Five Hundred Years

Fang Yuan, demonic overlord of 500 years, refines Spring Autumn Cicada (Rank 7 Mystical, time-path) at cost of ten million lives. Besieged by Righteous Path on summit — "We have long since laid an inescapable dragnet here. This time, you shall be beheaded!" — he self-detonates: 轰的一声自爆. Spring Autumn Cicada carries will back to 15-year-old self on Qing Mao Mountain, 500 years ago. Verse: "As the verse left his lips, memories of past life on Earth surged — He had once been a student of Huaxia on Earth". Huaxia² appears after Spring Autumn Cicada¹ in reading order — smart superscript.

📖 Ch1 p1-p11 · Gu: Spring Autumn Cicada 春秋蝉 Rank 7 · Origin: Huaxia 华夏 Earth

🔮 Aperture & Talent — 开窍 · 真元 · 资质

At 15, youths open aperture — 空窍, empty orifice containing primeval sea 真元, crystal membrane. Talent graded by sea percentage: Gu Zhen Ren writes 两三成 (two-three tenths) = twenty to thirty-nine percent, not %. Forty-four percent Grade C limit (Fang Yuan), sixty+ Grade B (Mo Bei 66%, Chi Cheng announced B), eighty+ Grade A (Fang Zheng). Primeval stones 元石 — condensed essence of heaven and earth, currency + cultivation resource, like gold standard. No % symbol in lore — preserve as words. First to Ninth Revolution system detailed Ch7. Click character → Aperture tab shows sea visualization: copper-green 44%, gold 84%, silver 70%+ for elders.

📜 Culture — 叩头 · 祭祀 · 魔道

叩头 — kowtow, forehead meets wood with soft thuds, palms flat, upper body erect, ancestral hall ritual before spirit tablets. Fang Yuan's perfunctory kowtow — irony of demon lord bowing to mortals. 魔道/正道 — Demonic Path: accumulate no good karma, only murder and arson, spurned by heaven, enemies of whole world, still wild following whim. Righteous Path: revered sects, order and law. Not good vs evil — two cultivation philosophies. Author's preface: "This is a book with warped values 三观不正".
