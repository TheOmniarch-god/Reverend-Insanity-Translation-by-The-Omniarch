# Live synopsis comparison: the seven supplied Chinese lines

Checked the live homepage at https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/ on14 October2026. This is a fresh check, not a repetition of the earlier benchmark. Original HTML, extracted synopsis and capture metadata are saved beside this report. No website or manuscript edits made.

## Overall verdict

The live version is substantially faithful. The clearest source-alignment issue is structural: it moves the final demonic-overlord line before the Gu list, drops the explicit addition in还有, and therefore changes the original closing emphasis. The strongest English-clarity issue is the unexplained calque “Three views.” Most other differences are optional stylistic expansions, not mistranslations.

## 1. 人是万物之灵，蛊是天地真精。

**Live:** Man is the most intelligent of all living things; Gu are the true essence of heaven and earth.

**Verdict: retain.** This preserves the previously accepted working rendering. “True essence” accounts for真精. The semicolon appropriately joins the parallel assertions. Do not reopen the accepted first clause merely to produce a new variation.

## 2. 三观不正，魔头重生。

**Live:** Three views warped; the demon lord is reborn.

**Verdict: intelligibility query, not a reversed meaning.** “Warped” conveys the negative judgement of不正. However, “Three views” is a literal cultural shorthand that English readers may not decode. In this modern usage,三观 means worldview, outlook on life and values, not three arbitrarily selected opinions or the Buddhist technical sense. [5](https://www.zdic.net/hans/%E4%B8%89%E8%A7%82)

**Explicit candidate:** Worldview, outlook on life, values: all warped. The demon lord is reborn.

This spells out the three elements at the cost of the original compact rhythm. Keeping “Three views warped” with a note is also defensible if the cultural shorthand is intentional. Do not automatically rewrite this as “his three views”: the source does not explicitly restrict the opening judgement to the protagonist rather than the work’s outlook. “Demon lord” is a stylised rendering of魔头, not evidence of a formal title. “Demon” is a leaner option, not a required correction.

## 3. 昔日旧梦，同名新作。

**Live:** An old dream of yesteryear, a new work bearing the same name.

**Verdict: faithful.** “Old” and “yesteryear” echo the Chinese overlap between昔日 and旧 rather than inventing an error. “Bearing the same name” is correct. The seven lines alone do not establish what earlier work/name is intended, so no bibliography should be invented.

**Optional tightening:** An old dream, a new work bearing the same name.

Retaining the live version is equally acceptable if its nostalgic register is preferred.

## 4. 一个穿越者不断重生的故事。

**Live:** This is the story of a transmigrator continuously reborn through time.

**Verdict: defensible contextual expansion, with a closer alternative.** “Through time” is not explicit in this line, although the story’s time-reversal mechanism supplies context. “Continuously” can suggest an uninterrupted process; “again and again” more clearly expresses repeated rebirths. This is precision and cadence, not proof that the live sentence is false.

**Candidate:** The story of a transmigrator reborn again and again.

Keep transmigration and subsequent rebirth distinct; do not replace both with a single generic reincarnation claim.

## 5. 一个养蛊、炼蛊、用蛊的奇特世界。

**Live:** A wondrous, bizarre world of nurturing, refining, and using Gu.

**Verdict: the three processes are correctly distinguished.** Two adjectives expand奇特: “wondrous” supplies admiration, “bizarre” supplies strangeness. Neither warrants labelling the sentence an outright mistranslation, but the combination is more embellished than necessary.

**Candidate:** A strange world of nurturing, refining, and using Gu.

“Peculiar” or “extraordinary” are tonal alternatives. “Refining” should remain distinct from nurturing and using.

## 6. 春秋蝉、月光蛊、酒虫、一气金光虫、青丝蛊、希望蛊……

**Live:** Spring Autumn Cicada, Moonlight Gu, Liquor Worm, One Qi Golden Light Worm, Black Hair Gu, Hope Gu...

**Verdict: retain the names, restore this line’s position before the final character flourish.** The six names are present and in source order. 青丝 has an established literary sense of black hair, so “Black Hair Gu” must not be mechanically corrected to blue/green silk merely from individual character meanings. The dictionary also records other senses, so dictionary possibility alone is not whole-novel certification of a Gu’s mechanics. [3](https://www.zdic.net/hans/%E9%9D%92%E4%B8%9D)

“One Qi Golden Light Worm” is a transparent literal construction; hyphenation is a terminology/style decision, not a newly established error. No name migration proposed here.

## 7. 还有一个纵情纵横的绝世大魔头！

**Live, currently before the Gu list:** A peerless demonic overlord who acts as he pleases and roams the world unfettered!

**Verdict: substantially defensible, but restore the connective and ending position.** 纵情 supports acting to one’s heart’s content, so “acts as he pleases” is not inherently a mistranslation. [1](http://www.zdic.net/c/5/152/338001.htm)

“Roams the world unfettered” foregrounds the freedom in纵横. A more forceful phrase could foreground sweeping or ranging unchecked, but that would be a tonal choice, not a compulsory factual correction. “Peerless demonic overlord” is heightened genre diction for绝世大魔头; it should not be treated as proof of an institutional office.

**Minimal candidate:** And a peerless demonic overlord who acts as he pleases and roams the world unfettered!

The initial “And” restores the additive force of还有. Returning this line to the end restores the source’s climax on the protagonist rather than on the Gu catalogue.
