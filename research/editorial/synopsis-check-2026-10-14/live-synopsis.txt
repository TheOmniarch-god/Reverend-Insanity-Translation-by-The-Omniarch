Synopsis
Man is the most intelligent of all living things; Gu are the true essence of heaven and earth.
Three views warped; the demon lord is reborn.
An old dream of yesteryear, a new work bearing the same name.
This is the story of a transmigrator continuously reborn through time.
A wondrous, bizarre world of nurturing, refining, and using Gu.
A peerless demonic overlord who acts as he pleases and roams the world unfettered!
Spring Autumn Cicada, Moonlight Gu, Liquor Worm, One Qi Golden Light Worm, Black Hair Gu, Hope Gu...
Say not that mighty mountains stand unyielding as iron;
Today, I stride forward and cross them anew.
Facing peril, I fashion my body from the essence of Heaven and Earth;
My heart, as ever, gazes at the Heavens.
