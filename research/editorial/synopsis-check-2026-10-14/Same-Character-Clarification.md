# Same-character clarification following the translator’s objection

The earlier recommendation to add “And” alone is superseded. “And a peerless demonic overlord” can read as introducing a second character after “the demon lord,” especially because the designation changes. Chinese还有一个 does not itself establish a distinct second person here. English discourse clarity should preserve the referent.

Preferred held candidate:

> And the peerless demon lord himself, acting as he pleases and roaming the world unfettered!

The definite article, repeated designation and “himself” explicitly reconnect the final flourish to the character introduced earlier. “Himself” is a clarifying addition, not a separately expressed Chinese lexical item. Restoring the final line to the end remains recommended. No live-site edit or user approval to implement is implied. The prior audit’s minimal and clarity versions remain historical proposals, not current final copy.
