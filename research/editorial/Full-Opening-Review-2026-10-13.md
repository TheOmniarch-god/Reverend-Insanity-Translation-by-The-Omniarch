## Verdict

**The opening has substantially improved, but this snapshot is not ready for an unconditional final sign-off.** The important earlier restorations are present. Several remaining corrections are small and definite; a smaller set concerns source fidelity rather than grammatical polish. There is no justification for another wholesale rewrite of the chapters.

### Classification

- **Precision:** a smaller but supported fidelity adjustment, not a plot-breaking error.

## 2. Immediate English/integration corrections

### E01. Chapter 3 p26: incompatible concessive constructions

[Live anchor](https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/chapter-3#p26)

**Snapshot:**

> Even if Shen Cui were however beautiful, so what?

**Chinese:**

> 就算是沈翠长得再漂亮又如何

“Even if” and “however beautiful” are competing constructions here. Preserve the source’s concession, not both English openings.

**Minimal replacement:**

> However beautiful Shen Cui might be, so what?

This does not change her beauty into the reason for his rejection. The following absence of love and loyalty remains the reason.

The newly approved “If I cast him aside, so be it” and the following “Heh” are now present. The body correctly says **piss off**, not “kindly piss off”.

**Separate, lower-priority choice:** this paragraph now uses first-person possessives but several past-tense assessments: “he was”, “she was”, “they were”. If the whole passage is intended as his immediate direct thought, present-tense assessments would be more natural. This is not permission to recast the source’s entire interior narration again. Make the grammatical construction above first, then decide tense within the approved presentation.

### E02. Chapter 5 p23: narration has been inserted inside the thought quotation

[Live anchor](https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/chapter-5#p23)

**Snapshot ending:**

> …Twenty-four… twenty-five… twenty-six… twenty-seven, Fang Yuan counted silently in his mind.”

The silent-counting cue has been restored, but the closing quotation mark comes **after** the narratorial tag. This makes the tag part of what he is thinking.

**Chinese:**

> “二十四、二十五、二十六……二十七。”方源心中默默地数着

**Minimal repair, retaining the quotation style:**

> …Twenty-four… twenty-five… twenty-six… twenty-seven,” Fang Yuan counted silently in his mind.

Or split the source’s threshold reflection from the count:

> “Fewer than ten steps means no aptitude. Ten to twenty is Grade D; twenty to thirty, Grade C; thirty to forty, Grade B; forty to fifty, Grade A. So far, I have taken twenty-three steps.”
>
> “Twenty-four… twenty-five… twenty-six… twenty-seven,” Fang Yuan counted silently in his mind.

The tag is source-supported. Correct its punctuation rather than removing it again.

### E03. Chapter 8 p3: a comma splice accompanies the size-bound error

[Live anchor](https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/chapter-8#p3)

**Snapshot:**

> They varied in size, the largest was no bigger than a clay pot, and the smallest was no larger than a fist.

The first comma joins independent clauses. The last size comparison also reverses the source’s bound, detailed under M01 below. Fix both in one local change:

> They varied in size: the largest was no bigger than an earthenware cooking pot, and the smallest was no smaller than a fist.

“Clay pot” may remain if preferred; “cooking pot” more specifically reflects 砂锅. The consequential repair is **no smaller**, not the pot wording.

### E04. Chapter 7 p50: the landscape replacement was not fully integrated

[Live anchor](https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/chapter-7#p50)

**Snapshot:**

> Tender shoots had just opened, yellow-green new growth, where plumaged finches perched among the branches from time to time.

The inserted “yellow-green new growth” sits awkwardly between “opened” and a “where” clause. The Chinese gives separate observations:

> 不远处的树林，已经泛起了新绿。抽出的嫩芽，黄绿一片。不时有漂亮的彩雀儿，落到枝干上。

**Conservative local rendering:**

> Not far off, the woods had come into new leaf. The tender buds formed a spread of yellow-green. From time to time, pretty, colourful little birds alighted on the branches.

“Colourful” preserves 彩; “plumaged” does not communicate that colour. “Little birds” avoids unnecessarily committing to a precise bird family. The essential point is to preserve new growth, its colour, and the birds as clear observations, not to impose this exact cadence.

## 3. Source-supported meaning and precision corrections

### M01. Chapter 8 p3: the lower bound is reversed

**Chinese:**

> 格子有大有小，大的超不过砂锅，小的小不过拳头。

The upper bound is a cooking pot; the lower bound is a fist. **小不过拳头** describes not being smaller than a fist. The English **“the smallest was no larger than a fist”** points the inequality the other way.

**Correct to:**

> …the smallest was no smaller than a fist.

This is a substantive local translation correction, not an attempt to remodel the fictional room.

### M02. Chapter 7 p11: D-grade’s usual ceiling has become an absolute ceiling

[Live anchor](https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/chapter-7#p11)

**Chinese:**

> 往往最高能修行到一转二转。

**Snapshot:**

> …such people can at most cultivate to First Revolution or Second Revolution.

往往 qualifies the claim. The English drops that qualification and reads like an exceptionless ceiling.

**Minimal repair:**

> …such people usually reach no higher than First Revolution or Second Revolution.

Do not change the grade bands as part of this repair. The issue is **usually**, not whether one personally finds the cultivation system plausible.

### M03. Chapter 2 p24: “legally” is an unsupported addition

[Live anchor](https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/chapter-2#p24)

**Chinese:**

> 舅父舅母就依着抚养的名义，堂而皇之地侵占了双亲的遗产

**Snapshot:**

> …his uncle and aunt had openly and legally usurped their parents’ inheritance…

堂而皇之 supports acting openly or with an appearance of legitimacy. It does not establish that the appropriation was lawful under the clan’s rules. “Legally” supplies a substantive legal judgement.

**Minimal repair:**

> Under the pretext of raising them, his uncle and aunt had openly usurped their parents’ inheritance…

This removes the added legal assertion without inventing an alternative verdict that it was formally illegal.

### M04. Chapter 1 p20: invented motive at the moment of advance

[Live anchor](https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/chapter-1#p20)

**Chinese:**

> 群雄逼迫而来，恰在这时，轰的一声，方源悍然自爆。

**Snapshot:**

> The righteous host surged forward, greed and panic driving them into a frenzy.

The advance is in the source. This sentence’s causal explanation involving **greed, panic, and frenzy** is not. Earlier apprehension does not license inventing this precise motivation.

**Local replacement:**

> The righteous host closed in. Just then, with a thunderous blast, Fang Yuan brazenly detonated himself.

This preserves the advance, sudden timing, sound, and deliberate self-detonation without assigning an unquoted collective motive.

### M05. Chapter 1 p3: “innocents” adds a moral classification

[Live anchor](https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/chapter-1#p3)

**Chinese:**

> 杀了千万人的性命

**Snapshot:**

> …you slaughtered ten million innocents!

The source accusation gives the lives/people and number; it does not explicitly classify the victims as innocent.

**Minimal repair:**

> …you slaughtered ten million people!

Keep the number distinct from Chapter 2’s hundreds of thousands. That numerical discrepancy is a separate source issue, not a reason to alter either figure here.

### M06. Chapter 2 p4: the possible third use is omitted

[Live anchor](https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/chapter-2#p4)

**Chinese:**

> 有的蛊用一次或者两三次，就会彻底消散。

**Snapshot:**

> Some Gu would completely dissipate after being used once or twice.

The source includes once, or two or three times. “Once or twice” omits the third-use possibility.

**Minimal repair:**

> Some Gu would completely dissipate after being used once, twice, or three times.

This is a precision correction, not a major plot problem.

### M07. Chapter 1 p6: added psychological colouring

[Live anchor](https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/chapter-1#p6)

**Chinese sequence:**

> 有的在咆哮，有的在冷笑，有的双眼眯起闪着警惕的光，有的捂着伤口恐惧地望着。

**Snapshot includes:**

> …their composure fractured… bared their teeth… brittle arrogance… venomous suspicion…

The Chinese supplies roaring, cold laughter, narrowed/watchful eyes, and fear while clutching wounds. The additional psychology and physical detail are interpretive embellishments. They should not be described as literal source restoration.

**Source-tight alternative for the list:**

> Some roared, some sneered, some watched with narrowed, wary eyes, and others clutched their wounds and stared at him in fear.

This is a fidelity adjustment to an otherwise readable passage. It does not imply that elevated diction elsewhere must be removed.

### M08. Chapter 1 p35: frequency is strengthened

[Live anchor](https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/chapter-1#p35)

**Chinese:**

> 而经验表明，往往从小就过目不忘，或者力气大如成人等等天赋异禀的人，都有优秀的修行资质。

**Snapshot:**

> …almost always possessed superior cultivation talent.

“Almost always” is stronger than the ordinary frequency of 往往. For a conservative rendering, use **often**. This matters because the scene concerns a fallible inference from visible precocity to aptitude.

**Minimal repair:** “often possessed superior cultivation talent.”

### M09. Chapter 8 p9: restore “through it” without restoring “obscured”

[Live anchor](https://ri.theomniarch.com.ng/chapter-8#p9)

**Chinese:**

> 方源放在手中，能透过它，看到自己被遮掩的掌纹。

**Snapshot:**

> …Fang Yuan could see the lines of his palm beneath.

The revised English avoids “obscured”, but no longer explicitly says he sees the palm lines **through the Gu**. Earlier translucence imagery makes the intention inferable; the direct physical relation is still worth preserving.

**Minimal repair:**

> Holding it in his hand, Fang Yuan could see the lines of his palm through it.

This is also a correction to my earlier suggested wording, which omitted “through it”. The source supports optical transparency, not a logical impossibility between covering and transparency.

**Verse query, not approved for emendation:** the EPUB reads:

> Say not that mighty mountains stand unyielding as iron;

The first, second, and fourth English lines preserve the broad mountain/recommencement/heavenward orientation. This pass does not establish that the entire verse is a uniquely correct translation.

## 5. Source problems that remain separate from translation defects

### Chronology: Chapter 7–8

The one-week/seven-days anchors, “a mere month”, and Fang Zheng’s ten-plus-day adjustment coexist in the Chinese. The public English retains that tension. It is not resolved by grammar editing.

**Decision needed:** retain the source with an internal continuity flag, or approve a documented harmonisation after checking the adjoining sequence. No silent number replacement is recommended here.

### Casualties: Chapter 1 versus Chapter 2

千万 versus 数十万 is a genuine numerical discrepancy between an antagonist’s accusation and Fang Yuan’s private reflection. Neither identical factual scope nor deliberate hyperbole has been established beyond doubt. Preserve the perspectives; do not harmonise the figures by assumption.

### Aperture height versus volume

Chapter 6 relates 44% to sea height; Chapter 10 expressly describes volume. A spherical cavity makes a strict mathematical identification problematic. That imprecision originates in the source. Do not calculate a new height and insert it into Chapter 6 as an unannounced repair.

### Chapter 8 p19: an awkward base-text reading

The supplied EPUB has **有沒有其他手段** in simplified form **有没有其他手段** within the clause about his C-grade aptitude and chances. The English “lacking alternative means” adopts the contextually expected negative sense. This is a plausible local correction of a rough base reading, not evidence that the English must be changed into a question. Record the reading internally if maintaining a textual-emendation ledger.

The Chapter 22 38% inconsistency remains outside this eight-chapter publication scope. It should stay in its separate emendation record, not be used to alter the opening’s grade definitions.
