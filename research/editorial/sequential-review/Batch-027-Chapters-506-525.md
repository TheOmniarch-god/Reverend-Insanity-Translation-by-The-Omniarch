## 2. Content summary (key events checked against the numbered text: 35 terms, all found in the cited chapters)

- 506–509: The 黑盟 army advances on 草府. 黑楼兰 holds a war council. 方源 extracts compensation after an assassination attempt. 孙湿寒 offers a strategy. 东方余亮 prepares against 常山阴 with 三心合魂 and three 奴道 advisers. 方源 sends the wolf force into battle without the customary single combat, and 唐妙鸣 is captured. 方源 completes the 逆炼 of 爆脑蛊 and obtains 空念, with a 盗天 inheritance pointing to 落魄谷.
- 510–513: 汪疆 kills 唐家 elders. 水魔 and 风魔 fight. Elite-troop battles follow. 三心合魂 meets 黑楼兰's 暗漩, which is countered by 云涡. 边丝轩 strikes with 叠影蛊, but the target is 水像蛊, and 浩激流 is revealed as the decoy. 东方余亮 uses 七星灯 and a star-thought cloud. 黑楼兰's self-destructive 暗漩 kills the three 奴道 advisers.
- 514–517: 东方 retreats. 方源's wolves break the first defence line. 焦雷土豆蛊 ambush. 战功榜 and wartime debt. 潜魂兽衣蛊 is bought. 功倍蛊 is purchased on credit. 全力以赴蛊 is raised to four-level.
- 518–521: 唐幽 is killed by 潘平 with 单刀蛊. 东方余亮 surrenders and withdraws through a 光门. In the 马家 camp, 费才 and 赵怜云 run the shoe ruse, and 费才 is promoted to 奴仆长.
- 522–525: 功倍蛊 purchase on credit. 马家 vs 宋家 (成虎 vs 苏毅). 罗家 surrenders. 努尔图 defeats 江暴牙. 古家 surrenders after 太白云生 uses 山如故. 刘家 vs 黑家 (欧阳碧桑; 浩激流 vs 裴燕飞).
