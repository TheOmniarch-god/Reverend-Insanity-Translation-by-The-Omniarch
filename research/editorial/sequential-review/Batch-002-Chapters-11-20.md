### Consequence for the Chapter 8 week proposal, SR-002

The additional chapters support the connected short chronology. They do not make the entire novel checked. Retain the candidate month → week and ten-plus days → first few days in the held proposal ledger. Continue testing it against later references rather than applying it now.

## 2. Seven search days are not automatically seven elapsed calendar days

Several descriptions now coexist:

- Ch12 p1: **一周多的时间**, more than a week spent trying to attract the Liquor Worm.
- Ch12 p33: **连续买了七天的酒水**, seven consecutive days of wine purchases.
- Ch13 p29: **前七天的探索**, the preceding seven days of exploration.
- Ch16 p55: **连续七天都找不到**, seven consecutive days without finding the legacy.
- Ch13 p26 and Ch14 p51: a three-day, three-night rain interval.

**Decision:** do not change every seven to ten. Purchase days, active search days, and elapsed time since awakening are different quantities. Seven days of activity followed by interruption can coexist with more than a week of elapsed time. The last phrasing is a compressed retrospective and should be watched, but it does not by itself prove that the rain interval must be removed.

For the eventual translation, preserve the scope of each claim: buying wine, searching, or time passing. Do not smooth all three into a single undifferentiated duration.

## 3. A month in the previous life is not the disputed month in Chapter 8

**Ch13 p9:**

> 在前世的一个月后，一位因失恋而醉酒的蛊师，烂醉如泥地躺在山寨外，结果四溢的酒香气息，意外地引来了一头酒虫。

This introduces the discovery made by another Gu Master in Fang Yuan's previous life. It belongs to a different course of events. Its precise reference point needs to remain visible as an open interpretive question; the sentence does not give an absolute date.

**Decision:** retain the month and the previous-life marker. Do not change it to a week merely to harmonise the opening chapters. Do not date the other discoverer's success in the present timeline, since Fang Yuan has already intervened.

Working English temporal phrase:

> In his previous life, a month later, …

That preserves the relative formulation without inventing “one month after the ceremony” or a calendar date.

## 4. The historical recording needs its own chronology

Ch13 pp1–7 recount the clan's received story from more than three hundred years earlier. Ch15 shows the recorded confrontation; Ch16 interprets it. These are not interchangeable evidence types.

### Keep the early false account

The public story makes the fourth-generation leader the honourable victor. The recording reverses those roles. That discrepancy is an intentional revelation, not an opening passage that should be rewritten to agree with later knowledge.

### Separate threats from elapsed events

The seven-day poison deadline in Ch15 p11 and Ch16 p2 belongs to the historical confrontation. The leader is killed during the encounter instead. Do not add seven days to the present chronology, or assume seven days actually passed in the recording.

The weekly demand for thirty jin of petals and three thousand stones in Ch15 p32 is a proposed tribute arrangement. It is not evidence that a payment was made or that another week passed.

### Replays do not create new historical events

Ch16 pp4–5 identifies the second and third playbacks. Record one confrontation, replayed, rather than three separate confrontations.

### Preserve the remaining uncertainty

Fang Yuan's deductions about the first discoverer's motives and the legacy's history are sometimes expressly conjectural. His conclusion that the cave has been completely searched must not become an editorial guarantee that no later discovery is possible. The approximate three-century language does not supply a precise death year.

## 6. New discrepancy SR-012: twelve stones spent, then six recalled

**Ch18 p84:**

> 我耗费了足足两天两夜，每天只休息两个小时，耗费十二块元石，也不过只炼化到十五分之一的进度。

The passage says two days and nights of work, two hours of rest per day, twelve stones consumed, and only one-fifteenth refinement achieved.

**Ch20 p29:**

> 这些天，方源炼化酒虫，前前后后足足消耗了六块元石。但是这一晚，他根本就没有耗费元石，仅仅只是空窍中的青铜真元海见了底。

The retrospective now gives six stones spent refining the Liquor Worm across these days. The larger specified subperiod cannot fit inside the smaller cumulative total on the same accounting basis.

### Editorial action

Register a linked numerical conflict, not two independent stylistic edits. Seek subsequent balances, recaps, and another source witness before choosing which number to replace.

A **working candidate**, if the twelve-stone expenditure survives those checks, is:

> Over these past days, refining the Liquor Worm had cost him at least twelve primeval stones.

“At least” deliberately avoids manufacturing a precise total when the first night's separate expenditure is not fully itemised. It is an editorial qualification, not a literal translation of the six-stone sentence.

The competing repair is to change the earlier twelve to six if later accounting supports that direction. **Neither is selected for publication.** Do not simultaneously change both figures or hide the discrepancy under a vague “many stones”.

### A further scope caution

Ch20's “this evening” contrast follows a scene in which refinement was already underway. Read its immediate emphasis as the assisted completion without further stone expenditure; do not automatically conclude that no stones were used at any earlier point that night. This temporal scope remains attached to SR-012 for later checking.

## 8. Preserve these apparent reversals

### More than a month predicted, then rapid completion

Ch17 p69 says refinement within a month is impossible under the conditions then faced, and immediately names suppression by stronger Gu as a means of assistance. Ch19–20 supplies precisely such an intervention, through the Sixth Revolution Cicada.

**Keep both.** Do not shorten the forecast to make it resemble the outcome. The change in circumstances produces the reversal.

### The Cicada thought absent, then discovered

The opening belief that the Cicada had not returned is revised in Ch19 pp30–36. Its extreme weakness explains why Fang Yuan had not detected it. Preserve the mistaken belief at the earlier viewpoint; do not insert advance knowledge into it.

### Planned vital Gu, then a different actual vital Gu

Fang Yuan intends to make the Liquor Worm his vital Gu. Ch19 pp50–60 explains that the Cicada's surviving bond already occupies that role in this life. Preserve intention versus discovery. Do not rewrite the plan as though Fang Yuan knew all along.

### Thirty years of preparation within five hundred years of experience

Ch19 p31 specifies thirty years of preparation for refining the Cicada; p34 specifies the five-hundred-year reversal. A project duration can sit inside a longer life-history period. These do not require equalisation, and neither statement establishes an exact birthday.

### Short intervals are nested, not competing

The Liquor Worm's final refinement takes moments. The Moonlight Gu process takes less than five minutes, and the academy scene follows a quarter-hour transition. Do not combine these into a single five-minute deadline for the entire sequence.
