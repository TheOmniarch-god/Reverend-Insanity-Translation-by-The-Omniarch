## 2. Content summary

- 446–457: The 葛家 encounter with the wolf packs. The Gao family alliance with Chang Shanyin; wolf-king tactics; the defence of the 葛家 camp and the death of the Gao patriarch in 458.
- 458: 葛家老族长 dies. Chang Shanyin takes the Night Wolf King with 驭狼蛊 and 狼烟蛊. Mo Tu learns of the failure and plans a third wind-wolf attack. The wolf force reaches 三万五.
- 459–460: The 月牙湖 journey. The lake site, the 鬼王 and 红玉散人 party, the 花海三仙 and the 地灵 of the 琅琊福地.
- 461: The 三尊说 and Ma Hongyun's past passage through the 石林 into the 琅琊福地.
- 462–463: Fang Yuan trades with the 地灵 for the 星门蛊 and related Gu, and learns about 天元宝皇莲 and the 十二云阁.
- 464–465: The five 仙 are captured and forced to craft 星门蛊. Fang Yuan uses 涸泽蛊 and 推杯换盏蛊 to send the 通天蛊 to the fox immortal. He returns through the 星门.
