## 2. Chapter notes

**Ch406 (第一节：凤九歌).** Feng Jin Huang's humiliation is re-lived through rage at Fang Yuan's foot-on-face memory. Bai Qing's worry and Feng Jiu Ge's "it's good for her" argument. Feng Jiu Ge's letter, carried by a Qing Bird Gu (青鸟蛊), demands compensation. The Supreme Elder is furious but cannot destroy the letter. Crane Feng Yang defends Fang Zheng as the leading elite disciple, a cover story the elders accept for now.

**Ch407 (第二节：仙鹤门的图谋).** The Crane Gate plan. Crane Feng Yang's claim that Fang Yuan is an in-house disciple, and his plan to use Fang Zheng as a lure. Important lore: the Bloodline Gu inheritance list. The nine Blood Sea True Inheritances are listed (血颅蛊, 血手印蛊, 血气蛊, 血汗蛊, 经血蛊, 血影蛊, 血战蛊, 戾血龙蝠, 血神子). Crane Feng Yang's statement that the sixth earthquake is "three months away" (407p22) is in outside time, resolved below. 407p58: the Supreme Elder orders Crane Feng Yang to hand over a Su Gu (素蛊) as compensation.

**Ch408 (第三节：方源苏醒).** Fang Yuan wakes after seven days of sleep and reports soul damage. Fox Immortal Fortune's realm and Soul-Shaking Palace are described. Fang Yuan's five-hundred-year previous life is recalled: he and nearly ten devil immortals attacked this fortune, winning narrowly with only himself and Song Zhong left. The sixth earthquake is "one year and three months" away (408p50), and Fang Yuan notes the 5× time flow. Fortune size is six million mu (408p72). The "four Hong Kongs" comparison is at 408p79 (see SR-193).

**Ch409 (第四节：执掌福地).** Inventory: about 860,000 Level 1 Gu, 130,000 Level 2 Gu, 5,000 Level 3 Gu, about 60 Level 4 Gu, one Level 5 Fox Hide Gu. Fox herds total about 4.7 million. Earthquake history: meteor fire (2nd), flood (4th), and Mei Lan Lightning Shadow (5th). The Lightning Shadow killed the Fox Immortal. The "one year three months = three months outside" relation is explicit at 409p22.

**Ch410 (第五节：荡魂山胆石).** Seventy-eight Cyan Lotus Yuan Stones and a half. Fixed Immortal Travel is fed one stone, which is enough for six years. Gall Stones (胆石) give Gall Courage Gu (胆识蛊) after shattering. The Ancestor Ren myth (人祖传) of Taiyang Mang is introduced: Ancestor Ren goes through the Gate of Life and Death (生死门) via three barriers: Soul-Shaking Mountain, Fallen Valley, and Turbulent River. He reaches the Underworld, where Fairness Gu (公平蛊) tells him that death is destiny. Fang Yuan's soul is now about five times stronger than before (410p43).

**Ch411 (第六节：未来大计).** Taiyang Mang's afterlife (Lethe Lake and "Peace Wine") and Fairness Gu's lecture on destiny. Ancestor Ren fails to bring his son back. Fang Yuan plans to walk the Slave path, backed by Power path strength, and to refine the Second Aperture using a Divine Wander Gu (神游蛊) made from four famous wines. Fang Yuan says he has been reborn three times (411p75). The Second Aperture embryo is a forty-year intermediate.

**Ch412 (第七节：我喜欢你的恐惧).** Yan Yong wakes after sleeping. Stone People sleep most of their lives. The Stone People elders refuse the labour order and the Granite Stone elder is killed, then the wave of Stone People fighting back is wiped out. Yan Yong is spared: "because you are afraid, and I enjoy your fear."

**Ch413 (第八节：一场戏).** Yan Yong returns with a false story of a tyrant. The Stone People revolt, and the eight tribes unite under him. Yan Yong's orders to attack the Soul-Shaking Mountain are presented as the dying White Stone Elder's secret. Fang Yuan lets the Stone People collect Gall Stones on the mountain, then gives a canal deadline of three months (fortune time). He takes the Ghost Gu (葬魂蟾) to collect soul fragments and raises his soul to six times normal.

**Ch414 (第九节：上位者的良知).** Yan Yong's guilt. He leads the Stone People on the canal project under Fang Yuan's direction. The White Stone Elder is said to be 998 years old. Fang Yuan's view that rules and morality are tools is explicit. Seven months remain before the sixth earthquake (414p73).

**Ch415 (第十节：为了明天！).** Labour deaths and memorial stones. Old Stone People question the order and are shamed by youth gangs. Fang Yuan's Estrus Gu (发情蛊) is used to repopulate the fox herds. Canal completed: north water flows into east fire, forming lakes. Clouds remain over the east fortune.

**Ch416 (第十一节：渡地灾（上）).** Sixth earthquake. Mud Crab (泥沼蟹) emerges from a light-beam portal. The crab's shell is heavy, it breeds crab armies by spraying mud, and its fox counter-attacks fail. Fang Yuan has six times normal soul. Yuan Stones are spent to teleport the crab (78 total before the earthquake).

**Ch417 (第十二节：渡地灾（下）).** Mei Lan Lightning Shadow attacks Fang Yuan but is driven off by Soul-Shaking Mountain. Fang Yuan orders the north abandoned (about 1,000,000 mu). The Lightning Shadow is caught in the sealed north and expelled to Central Continent. The Mud Crab dies on the mountain's slope. Fang Yuan's reflection: "Give me more power."

**Ch418 (第十三节：有失有得).** Aftermath. Fortune losses are reported. Fang Yuan concludes the crab's death is a gain, not a loss. Three letters arrive through the rift: a Qing Bird Gu from Feng Jiu Ge (challenge for Feng Jin Huang), a flying-sword letter gu from Jian Yisheng (challenge), and a paper crane from Crane Feng Yang (Fang Zheng is alive, Crane Gate wants to trade). Fang Yuan calls Fang Zheng in.

**Ch419 (第十四节：方正的痛苦).** Fang Zheng's pain: brother, enemy, Crane Gate tool. Tian He Shangren's orders. Crane Feng Yang's plan to use Fang Zheng as the expendable piece. A rift shows the fortune and Fang Zheng attempts a message; Crane Feng Yang notices Fang Yuan abandoning more land.

**Ch420 (第十五节：兄弟相见).** Jian Yisheng grabs the north land and attacks the Lightning Shadow outside. Crane Gate teleports Fang Zheng into the fortune. The brothers' confrontation. Fang Yuan reveals he wants trade, not allegiance, and keeps the Crane Gate identity as useful cover. Fang Yuan plans to seek Tong Tian Gu in Langya Fortune later.

**Ch421 (第十六节：石人族大发展).** Crane Feng Yang reads the trade list. Fang Yuan wants 5 million Yuan Stones, five Spring Egg Gu, one Golden and three Purple Crystal Sheli Gu, Feather-Transformation Wine (羽化酒), and many low-level Gu. Crane Feng Yang concludes Fang Yuan is preparing to refine, and withholds components. Spring Egg Gu are planted and three Yuan Springs erupt. Stone People population grows tenfold in a day, passes 10,000 by the next morning, and reaches 300,000 by the third evening.

**Ch422 (第十七节：幽魂魔尊).** Silver Cup Gu refinement (159 Level 3 Gu). Fang Yuan's soul reaches 100 times normal (百人魂). Ghost Soul Venerable (幽魂魔尊), the Soul Gu founder, is introduced with notes on Soul-Shaking Mountain and Fallen Valley. Fang Yuan refines Golden Cup and Silver Cup Gu into four-level Golden Cup Silver Cup Gu (金杯银盏蛊). Stone People split into three tribes. Fang Yuan enslaves Yan Yong and other elders with Slave Gu.

**Ch423 (第十八节：方源你这个小王八蛋！).** Crane Feng Yang's fury. Trade continues: Stone People for Cave Earth Gu. Crane Feng Yang trims the component list to test whether Fang Yuan hides a special formula. Fang Yuan refuses reductions. Crane Feng Yang reverse-engineers the Gu and finds an ordinary formula, then curses Fang Yuan.

**Ch424 (第十九节：我们要屠仙！).** Cave Earth Gu opens west. Stone People are tricked into it and taken to Crane Gate. Sixty thousand young Stone People are sold for a net of about 1.6 million Yuan Stones after costs. Nine-Eye Wine Worm retired at Fourth Level peak. Fang Yuan's three-year plan. A month later the Mud-Harmony Gu problem emerges.

**Ch425 (第二十节：仙蛊和稀泥).** Soul-Shaking Mountain erodes into mud. The Mud-Harmony Immortal Gu (六转和稀泥仙蛊) is the cause (one-use, Sixth Level). Fang Yuan considers three options: Land-Transformation Immortal Gu (化石蛊, Sun Cu, West), East Mountain Rebirth (东山再起, Sea Market Fortune, East), and Universe-Constancy Immortal Gu (江山如故, Tai Bai Yun Sheng, North). He chooses the North. Fang Yuan refines Pushing-Cup Gu (推杯换盏蛊) and leaves via Fixed Immortal Travel.

## 3. Resolved and locally settled points

- **Outside versus fortune time (407p22 vs 408p50).** Crane Feng Yang's "three months" is outside time. Fang Yuan's "one year and three months" is fortune time. 409p22 states the five-to-one ratio explicitly. Locally resolved, no edit needed.
- **Fang Yuan in Crane Gate cover.** Crane Feng Yang's pretence (407, 419, 423) is consistent with 420, where Fang Yuan keeps the membership as cover but refuses allegiance. No conflict.
- **Mei Lan Lightning Shadow survival.** It is expelled to the Central Continent (417, 420). Not dead. Its later fate is outside this batch.
