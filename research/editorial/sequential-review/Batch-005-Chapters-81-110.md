## 1. Main chronology findings

### School year, elapsed year and private tuition must be separated

Chapter 109 locates the next awakening ceremony in March. Together with Chapter 7's April lesson, seven days after awakening, this strengthens the spring-entry calendar. It does not support moving the original entry to January just to rescue Chapter 72's half-year claim.

Several new passages describe an academy year, almost a full year, or a full year of extortion. Some may be ordinary school-year shorthand. They should not all be mechanically replaced. The stronger independent problem is Chapter 84's claim that Fang Zheng has been privately taught for a year:

> 哼，拥有五百年战斗经验的方源，又岂是被一个四转蛊师教导一年的方正所能媲美的？

Those lessons began after the Jia inquiry in Chapter 58. At graduation, the later Jade Skin gift and the June tusk scheme are each recalled as roughly half a year earlier. The preferred held wording for private tuition is **a little over half a year**, with **several months** available if later dates require less precision.

Chapter 99's Cicada passage also distinguishes a seasonal cycle of spring and autumn from a measured twelve-month recovery period. Do not invent a 365-day minimum for its partial recovery.

### Age fifteen versus sixteen: a local, repairable mismatch

In Chapter 90, the aunt says Fang Yuan is already sixteen. In the same chapter Fang Yuan says he will be sixteen after New Year. Chapter 86 still calls him fifteen; the later inheritance scene calls both twins sixteen.

**Working correction:** change the aunt's statement to **He will be sixteen after the New Year.** This preserves the sequence without inventing a birthday or imposing a Western age convention.

### The new caravan contradiction is not the butterfly effect itself

Chapter 39 distinguished the caravan's usual April arrival from that year's late May arrival. Chapter 109 now describes summer as the usual arrival season:

> 知道商队进驻山寨后，方源在心中轻轻一叹：“又改变了。关于前世的记忆里，这支商队应该是在夏季才到。按照往年的惯例，也是夏季的时候商队到达这里。但是如今，却是提前了两三个月份，在春季就到了。并且规模更大。”

Its current March arrival is explicitly attributed to changes caused by Fang Yuan. Preserve that. The issue is the conflicting description of the ordinary historical schedule.

The held clarification distinguishes **usually April** from **that year's prior-life summer arrival**. The two-or-three-month advance should then compare March with the remembered summer arrival, not with April. Later caravan evidence may revise this proposal.

## 5. Resources and mechanics: what now reconciles

### Breakthrough does not change aptitude

The ordinary 55% peak-essence threshold is explicitly qualified by exceptions. Fang Yuan has 44%, and succeeds through prolonged replenishment while maintaining pressure on the aperture wall. The text supplies the mechanism. Do not change 44 to 55 or erase the threshold.

### Inventory is consistent

**7 Gu→6 after White Jade fusion→7 after inheriting Nine-Leaf Vitality Grass.** OneLittle Light then dies in failed Moonglow refinement. A replacement is implied before the successful three-input recipe, but its purchase price is not supplied. Successful Moonglow fusion leaves five permanentGu, apart from produced leafGu inventory.

Chapter 110 has not yet given Fang Yuan a secondLiquor Worm. It is still an auction prospect.

### Leaf production and sale are not the same account

Each leaf requires 20 essence points to regrow, so nine require 180 points of cumulative expenditure. A 44-point reserve supports two before replenishment, not nine stored at once. This is throughput, not a 180% aperture.

Nine leaves at 50 retail give 450 gross. The text reports more than 400 in earnings, but does not itemise production cost or Jiang Ya's margin. Chapter 107 distinguishes the clan's 55 retail price from Fang Yuan's 50 retail price. Do not assume the agent hands over exactly 450.

White Jade consumes eight liang of jade every 20 days, compared with Jade Skin's two liang every 10 days. That doubles average jade consumption, not quadruples it. Pigfeeding ceases, but the narrator still says the combined market feeding cost rises. Self-sourced jade makes cash expenditure a separate question.

### Cash and assets

The pre-inheritance cash low is explicitly **2.5 stones**. The uncle's 1000/2000/2500 settlement offers are refused. Inherited property includes over 10 mu of farmland, eight servants, three bamboo houses, a wine-shop and Nine-Leaf Vitality Grass. Its cash valuation is not given. The uncle has already withdrawn the shop's current-month receipts; those cannot be credited to Fang Yuan.

The displayed Liquor Worm price is 500 against a stated normal 580. The 80 difference is real, but sealed bids determine the actual price. No purchase or bid outcome is claimed throughChapter 110.

- Jiao San is initially presumed dead, then observed moving, then killed. Preserve that sequence.
- PermanentWhite Boar strength survives consumption of theGu in fusion. White Jade does not itself grant new strength.

- TwoLittle Light recipe inputs do not stack two amplification bonuses before fusion.
- Qing Shu's death is recalled from the former life. The proposed five-year right to use a higherLiquor Worm is an unaccepted conditional offer.
