## 2. Content summary

- 466–469: The 星门 route and the 星萤蛊 supply. Fang Yuan trades with the 宝黄天 through 通天蛊, sells residual Gu formulas, and returns through the star-gate. He prepares 推杯换盏蛊 and buys 舍利蛊. 砚石老人 and 白凝冰 are introduced. The 琅琊地灵 is baited with 神游蛊.
- 470–473: The 严家 (严天寂, 严翠儿) and the 水魔浩激流 episode. The 严家 summons Chang Shanyin; the betrayal of the 严家 elders by Fang Yuan's wolves; the fall of the 严家 camp; the surrender.
- 474–477: Growth after victory. The 狐仙福地 pasture, 星屑草 and 星萤虫 on the cloud. Fang Yuan reaches five-level peak through 紫晶舍利蛊; the 胆识蛊 harvest; the 敛息蛊; the 葛家 spoils and 太上家老 status.
- 478–481: The 贝家, 郑家 and 裴家 alliance; Fang Yuan's night raids; 裴燕飞; 贝草川's ambush and retreat.
- 482–485: The 裴家 battle; the wolf retreat; the 汪家 response; Fang Yuan's raid on the 水狼 nests and his plan to deal with the 琅琊地灵 for the second 空窍 Gu.
