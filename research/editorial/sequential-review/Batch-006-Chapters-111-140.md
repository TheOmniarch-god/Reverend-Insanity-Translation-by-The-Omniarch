## 1. Main chronological finding: the remembered destruction date conflicts

Chapter 126, during the January alliance sequence, recalls destruction two years later:

> 但方源正需要这种排斥，他身上的秘密越来越多了，他需要形单影只。同时，这些人也没有结交的价值。记忆里，青茅山三大山寨虽然从狼潮中艰难地保存下来，但是两年后那场事故后统统灭亡，从此青茅山化成一片冰寂死地。

Chapter 135 gives a different anchor:

> 在方源的前世，狼潮过去之后的三年后，白凝冰的修为不可避免地达到了四转境界。终于让他的空窍无法承受真元的重负，轰然自爆。

These are accounts of the **same previous life**, not a comparison between the old and reborn branches. The wolf tide is still ahead during the alliance sequence, and Chapter 124 forecasts at least a year of it. “Two years later” and “three years after the wolf tide” therefore cannot simply be interchanged.

**SR-053, held:** the least committal interim wording is “After the wolf tide had passed,” deleting the disputed three-year interval from 135 rather than inventing a replacement date. That is a proposal only. The original figures remain in the register, and the whole-novel evidence must determine which numerical account, if either, should ultimately stand.

The future investigation is a separate clock: Jia Fu says Tie Xue Leng is expected **the year after next** in Chapter 113. Jiang Fan’s 200-plus-year future history, the future discovery of recipes, and Ren Zu’s overnight vigil are also kept outside the present-life calendar.

## 2. A correction to my earlier diagnosis, plus a separate seasonal query

**SR-047 is withdrawn.** Batch 005 treated the usual April arrival and the usual summer arrival as a firm conflict. That was too categorical. The text itself calls July “early autumn” in 116; a Gregorian month-to-season assumption cannot settle the earlier passage.

> 七月，初秋。

This does **not** mean the calendar is now consistent. A new, internal tension appears later: Chapter 131 reaches late July, Chapter 132 advances eight or nine days, and Chapter 139 explicitly calls the resulting scene summer:

> 如此，天气渐渐炎热，到了七月末。虽说情况仍旧不容乐观，但是在三大家族不断地合作之下，情势已经得到控制。

> 八九天之后。

> 在这炎热的夏季，狭窄的山道上，洁白的冰霜却蔓延开来，覆盖泥土，吞没树木。周围了空气中的温度骤然下降。

**SR-054, held:** “in this hot weather” is a minimal candidate for the last phrase. It preserves the heat without committing to a disputed season. The direction remains provisional. Different yearly weather is possible, and no exact lunar or Gregorian calendar has been established. This new query does not reinstate the withdrawn April/summer diagnosis.
