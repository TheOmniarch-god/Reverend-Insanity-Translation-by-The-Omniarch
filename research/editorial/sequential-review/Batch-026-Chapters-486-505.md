## 2. Content summary (key events checked against the numbered text: 27 terms; citations corrected where needed)

- 486–487: Fang Yuan watches 琅琊地灵 make the second 空窍 Gu. He receives 第二空窍蛊 and 神游蛊 and trades for 驭狼蛊 formulas and a 和稀泥仙蛊.
- 488–490: The 马家 under 马英杰 takes 暖沼谷 from 费家; 费清 and his son 费才 flee. Fang Yuan learns about 八十八角真阳楼 from the 地灵 and forms the second 空窍 in his own body.
- 491–493: Fang Yuan buys 狼皇 and 异兽 wolves in the 宝黄天. 砚石老人 counters him in the market (492) and uses 天机蛊 at heavy cost (492). Fang Yuan's 仙元石 run low; he trains toward five-level in both 空窍.
- 494–495: A standoff between 仲家 and 柴家 at a lake ends with Fang Yuan's wolves scattering both. He returns to 葛家 and finds 灰白石板.
- 496–497: 地丘 map hidden in a grey tablet; the 葱谷 wolf reserve; the 玉田 great meeting; the 墨狮狂 and 毒蛇郎君 fight; the 刘家 and 黑家 clash.
- 498–500: 墨狮狂 fights 黑绣衣. Fang Yuan's wolves arrive; 黑楼兰 allies with him; 龟玉狼皮蛊 is given; the 黑家 alliance is sealed with 毒誓蛊.
- 501–502: The wolf count rises past fifty thousand; the 赵家 father decides to leave for 马家 after his young daughter persuades him (502; she is named 赵怜云 in 503).
- 503–505: 东方余亮 plans against 常山阴. 边丝轩 attacks with 爆脑蛊 on a 鱼翅狼 and fails. Fang Yuan sees the trap in time.
