## 2. Content summary (key events checked against the numbered text)

- 546–549: 方源 travels with 蓝田蛞蝓蛊 caravans and reflects on 王庭福地. 546p46 has a speaker say the 福地 refuses 蛊仙 entry and hope to gain a 运道 传承 inside the 真阳楼. 547 describes a grotesque many-headed, many-mouthed creature with 人首马身 features. 548 covers 幽火龙蟒 and its wild 炎道 Gu. 549 counts the 天青万狼王 group, which grows from two to three.
- 550–553: After 潘平 and 朱宰, the gathered 蛊师 bow to 方源 as 狼王 (550p15). 方源 rides a 天青万狼王 (550p18). 潘平 tries to get 方源 to join him in breaking the wolf line (550p65). The 星蛊 set is described, including a 偏门 attack-amplifier (550p85). 地丘 传承 is traced to a 灰白石板 fake (552p9–22). 552 lays out the 寝宫 system: 圣宫 with three other earthly 寝宫 and one heavenly 寝宫 (552p34–35), and the history of the ten 九转 蛊师 (552p36). 553 describes the 圣宫 layout (八层, 八百余丈), the 八大正宫 and 八十八角真阳楼 as a future structure (553p10–67).
- 554–557: 石武 is given 泉蛋蛊 for a 丁字号 元泉, and the 红炎谷 元泉 are drying out (554p45–80). 飞手雪 and a snow monster destroy the 泉蛋蛊 (555p18–29). 方源 notices that 地丘 has no small tower, which is a clue to the 传承 (555p72). 黑楼兰 is blocked at the 54th gate and recruits 方源 to enter 真阳楼 (556p5–86). 方源 clears the 水蛇狮 gate with 刺猬鱼 and gets a top rating (557p98–108).
- 558–561: 方源 trades for 天元宝王莲 with four 泉蛋蛊 (558p45–55). He passes the 来客止步碑 and handles the 晶壁 (559p17–96). 琉璃楼主令 is unlocked (560p28–55). The 中枢室 shows 地丘 as the leak in the sand model (560p73–95). 561 explains that the 小塔楼 forms the 三气 (天, 地, 人) and that the 地丘 传承 is a 炼蛊 method. 方源 solves the 密语 (561p78–93).
- 562–565: The 黑楼兰 faction struggles with the 金白虎虚像 at gate 78 and opens 真阳楼 for a fee (562p48–75). 方源 starts the 炼蛊 at 地丘 with 小光蛊 and 光栅蛊 (563p18–34). 太白云生 relives his choice of the third 传承 from the old beggar (563p69–93). The 第二层 霞光 weakens and the 第一层 collapses, then reforms (564p1–44). 方源 uses 花洒 手法 and 臭屁蛊, and the 极乐雪蝠群 comes in (564p55–565p89). 565 has 耶律桑 and 常飚 enter 真阳楼, 潘平 and 常极右 clash, and 唐妙鸣 is named as a possible replacement for the 狼王 (565p17–82).

## 4. Cross-links and systemic notes

- SR-299 (攻倍蛊 vs 功倍蛊) is lowered from 0.75 to 0.6 because 550p85 uses 攻倍蛊 as a generic category. The 功倍 name still dominates the corpus. Decide at whole-novel reconciliation with SR-316 in view.
- SR-289 (王庭福地 entry rule, 528p53 vs 524p47): 546p46 is a further datum (SR-309). Hold.
- SR-336 (常极右's parentage): 530 has 常山阴 as 义父 and the adoption; 565p62 calls 常极右 his 亲身儿子; 565p67 calls 常飚 the real father. The conflict should be settled in whole-novel reconciliation, not by a local fix alone.
- SR-332 (炎道 vs 火道 for the 耶律 Gu): three uses of 炎道 against one of 火道. Confidence is moderate because the difference could be deliberate.
- 565p62 and 530 (SR-336) are the only identity conflicts in the batch.
- Systemic: gates are counted in 关 and storeys in 层 (555p85, 556p20). 565p47 uses 层 for gates (SR-335). 565p17 uses 王庭之主 for the office (SR-334). The one 光 for 关 slip is at 556p20 (SR-322).
