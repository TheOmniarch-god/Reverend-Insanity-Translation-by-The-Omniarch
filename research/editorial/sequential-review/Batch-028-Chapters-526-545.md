## 2. Content summary (key events checked against the numbered text)

- 526–529: 边丝轩 fights 欧阳碧桑, who uses 修罗尸蛊 and then 修罗变 and gets the better of her. 墨狮狂 joins in. 刘家 searches for 方源 (526p82). After the first battle, the 黑家 hall is subdued and wounded, and a 黑家 speaker accuses 方源 of holding a 五转功倍蛊 on credit (527p46). A 销魂福地 council has 黑柏 and 黑城 discuss support for 黑楼兰, and 狐仙 has 上百 异兽狼 to sell (528p55). The 马家 army moves through the marsh. 赵怜云 resists a betrothal to 魏家 (529p64). 常飚 writes to 方源 (529p82).
- 530–533: 方源 steps on 孙湿寒's head (530p74). 常极右 is among the 常家 figures present (530p2). 草兵 fights 贝草川 while 方源 plans to use 狼嚎蛊 and 功倍蛊 once he shows himself (533p24, 533p27). 狼群 is sent against the 刘家 central tent (532p41).
- 534–537: 三头六臂 (刘文武, 欧阳碧桑, 墨狮狂 fused) kills 高扬, 朱宰 and 潘平, and 太白云生 revives them with 人如故. The 刘家 camp breaks as units withdraw. 黑楼兰 captures 刘文武 and the others, and the 刘家 indemnity is set (535p19–22). 常家 surrenders to 黑家 and is handed to 方源 for disposal (535p55). 大决战 opens between 黑家 and 马家 (536p65–90). 雪松子, the 大雪山 sponsor of 马家, sends three 雪人 commanders and the 丁字战部 (537p37–44).
- 538–541: 潘平 duels 费生成 and 浩激流 duels 成虎 (538p39–66). 常飚 is sent into battle while still weak (538p84–89). 方源 reveals his five-level peak (539p84–91), then uses 四臂地王 (541p46–49). 狼群 is set against the 马家 beast swarms (马尊, 江暴牙, 杨破缨), which are split and broken (540p2–41). 方源 kills 成虎 (541p38–63), 杨破缨 (541p64–78) and 邬夜 (541p86–94), and 成龙 falls later (542p63–82).
- 542–545: 方源 kills 马尊 after the 马家 龙马精神 fails to hold him (542p98). 江暴牙 flees, 马尚峰 dies to 潘平's 单刀蛊 (543p44), and 马家 is defeated. 费才 and 赵怜云 rescue 马英杰 (544p1–11). 马英杰 takes over 马家 and retreats to 暖沼谷. 方源 plans to seize 江山如故 (544p63–87). 黑家 poisons the 暖沼谷 元泉 (545p60). 费才 is renamed 费鸿运 (545p58).
