## D. Spelling consistency (British vs American)

Mixed usage across chapters.
- British forms (majority): centre (3), centres, coloured, colours, favoured, grey (2), harbour, harbouring, honourably, honoured, lustre, metres (2), realised (2), realising, rumour (2), rumours (2), savouring, splendour, utilised, vigour (2).
- American forms (minority): emphasized (ch3 p18), harbored (ch2 p26), utilizing, vigorously.

Recommendation: standardise on British spelling (the translation already leans that way), changing "emphasized", "harbored", "utilizing" and "vigorously" (vigorously is correct as-is in both).
