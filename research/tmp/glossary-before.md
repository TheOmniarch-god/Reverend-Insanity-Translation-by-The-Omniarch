# Glossary: Flower Wine terms (Reverend Insanity / 蛊真人)

Status: working glossary for rendering decisions. These are terminology choices, not approvals of any proposal in proposal-ledger.json. Nothing here changes a source reading.

## Entries

| Chinese | English (working) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 花酒行者 | Flower Wine Wanderer | Name of the character. "Wanderer" keeps the neutral sense of 行者 (a practitioner on the path). "Vagabond" was rejected for its disreputable tone. "Peregrine" was rejected as too elevated. "Monk" is the established fan-wiki form, but 行者 does not carry a religious role here. |
| 花酒大人 | Lord Flower Wine | Frightened honorific from clan members and captives. Keep the deference distinct from the name. Anchor: 15p24. Also 花酒行者大人 at 15p14. |
| 花酒 (alone) | Flower Wine | Check each use before translating. In chapter 156 the title 照影秘方，花酒迷离 appears to be wordplay, not the name. |
| 遗产 | inheritance | Estate or property left by the dead, not only parents'. Includes his own goods: 7p39 (他的遗产), 7p42 (留下来的遗产), and his recorded words "我的遗产都留给你" at 46p32. |
| 遗藏 | hidden cache / legacy (OPEN) | The cache left by the dead, the object of the plot. Used in the chapter 7 title (花酒留遗藏) and about 22 paragraphs with 花酒行者遗藏. Choice pending: "legacy" or "hidden cache". |

## Open decisions

1. **遗藏**: "legacy" or "hidden cache"? "Legacy" carries both the estate and the cache sense. "Hidden cache" is less ambiguous in plot passages.
2. **Fan-established "Flower Wine Monk"**: keep it anywhere in the text (for example, the official Webnovel rendering, if it differs), or use only "Flower Wine Wanderer"?

## Anchor passages

- 7p35: described as a 五转 蛊师 and 采花大盗; ambushed the fourth 族长.
- 7p39: 他的遗产 (inheritance).
- 7p42: 留下来的遗产 (inheritance).
- 15p24: 花酒大人 (honorific from frightened speakers).
- 15p27–32: the banquet, the poison attempt, and the reversal; 奴隶蛊 held by the 花酒行者.
- 46p32: the recording; "我的遗产都留给你".
- 163p10: appearance (bald, pink clothes, alone, preys on women).
- Chapter 7 title (第七章 蛊师有九转，花酒留遗藏): confirmed on Hetushu page 5345246.

## Source inconsistency (context only)

7p39 and 7p42 use 遗产 for the same goods that later passages call 遗藏. Do not change either word; record as context.
