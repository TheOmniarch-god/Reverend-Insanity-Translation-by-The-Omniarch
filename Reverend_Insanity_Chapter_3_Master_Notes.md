# Chapter 3 — Translation and Reading Notes

## Source and title

Source: OEBPS/Text/chapter11.html in the supplied Chinese EPUB. Raw text: Reverend_Insanity_Chapter_3_Raw_Chinese.txt.

第三节：请一边玩蛋去 — Chapter 3: Kindly Piss Off.

请 gives the dismissal a superficially polite form; 玩蛋 is deliberately coarse. The approved idiomatic title is “Kindly Piss Off.” Preserve its echo in the closing line rather than describing literal ball-playing.

## What the chapter establishes

- Fang Yuan's experienced will contrasts with the fatigue of his uncultivated body.
- Shen Cui is his personal maidservant and his aunt and uncle's informant. Her mother, Granny Shen, manages the household and enjoys their aunt's trust.
- The brothers receive unequal treatment. Fang Yuan recognizes its intended effect: resentment and estrangement between them.
- Fang Zheng's position behind his brother becomes a literal shadow on his face, then a metaphor for the influence he fears he cannot escape.
- Fang Yuan believes he could change these relationships through coercion and manipulation, but chooses not to expend his attention on them unless they obstruct his path. This is a reading of his stated reasoning, not an added philosophical doctrine.

## Locked terminology and important distinctions

- 开窍大典: Aperture Opening Ceremony.
- 魔道巨擘: demonic overlord (approved idiomatic rendering). 巨擘 means an eminent or towering figure, not 霸王 and not a formal cultivation rank.
- 外人: outsider, not stranger; the issue is lack of familial affection, not lack of acquaintance or blood relationship.
- 舍了也就舍了: Discard him, and so be it.
- 少女: girl or young woman; no exact age is given. Do not infer adulthood from her occupation.
- 绿衫: green tunic (approved); 长袖长裤 explicitly specifies long sleeves and long trousers. No modern tailoring is implied.
- 袖口: cuffs / sleeve openings. Buttons are not implied.
- 管家: household manager. Household does not automatically mean a landed estate.
- 肥肿的老妈子: fat, bloated old woman; the surrounding text establishes her domestic-service role.
- 小小的丫头片子: dismissive reference to a young girl. User retained “a mere slip of a maid” after discussing its old-fashioned English associations.
- 你退下罢: You can go. Same Chinese and approved English as Fang Yuan's dismissal of Fang Zheng in Chapter 2.

## Physical staging

Shen Cui helps with washing the face and cleaning the teeth, then dressing and fastening clothing. Fang Zheng arrives while she smooths creases in the clothes over Fang Yuan's chest. There is no bathing or bare-chest description here.

Shen Cui's black hair is explicit in this chapter. Fang Yuan's hair color is not specified in Chapters 1–3. Later verification: Chapter 8 explicitly describes his disheveled black hair; do not insert that later detail into Chapter 3.

## Fidelity decisions

- Line 4 retains fatigue and recurring sleepiness without extra oppressive or medical imagery.
- Line 8 specifies fresh moist air, earth, trees, and wildflowers—not invented mist, pine woods, or mountain blossoms.
- Line 23 preserves tentative desire, rather than making Fang Zheng wholly consumed by longing.
- Line 25 emphasizes that it was Fang Yuan who requested a servant for his brother.
- Line 44: “By now, the horizon glowed with the light of dawn, and Fang Yuan’s shadow fell across his face.” 晨光 is morning/dawn light; the following sentence explicitly describes the rising sun.
- Line 47 progresses from chest pressure to difficult breathing to the word “suffocation” occurring to him. Do not add throat constriction or the direct thought “I can't breathe.”
- Line 61 uses a dismissive rhetorical question, not genuine deliberation: “Her, in my harem? She’s not worthy.”
- Onomatopoeia should be incorporated into action where appropriate, rather than rendered as standalone comic-book sound effects.
