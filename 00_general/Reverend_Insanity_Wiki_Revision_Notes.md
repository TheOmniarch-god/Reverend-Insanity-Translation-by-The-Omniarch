# Wiki revision audit

Prepared 24 September 2026. Main deliverable: `Reverend_Insanity_Wiki_Updated.wikitext` (identical copy in `.txt`). This is a revised draft, not a live wiki edit or a claim of Wikipedia publication readiness.

## Substantive corrections

1. **Three Temples:** Removed the erroneous quotation. The current Webnovel page says “With his Three Fundamental Views* unrighteous, the demon is reborn.” Its footnote explains worldview, values and philosophy of life. The EPUB synopsis differs and contains the mountain stanza. 三观不正 also occurs in the author's preface. Our earlier discussion that located the expression in the preface was correct for that EPUB, but should not be generalized into a claim that no synopsis contains it.
2. **Ban rationale:** Removed the alleged official quotation about violence, cruelty and extreme individualism. No regulator quotation about this specific novel was established by the supplied sources. The SCMP content retrieved discusses industry-wide censorship; access to full text may be limited. The full Lacuna article discusses the crackdown and Lord of the Mysteries, not Reverend Insanity. The Guardian article concerns Communist Party officials reading banned political books, not this novel. These sources cannot be attached to title-specific claims as if they establish them.
3. **Suspension:** Retained the reported 2019 suspension as explicitly citation-needed, rather than falsely claiming that a general censorship article proves it. The Inkstone interview supports that the work was stopped and records the author's response, but does not establish the precise 2019 date, an official legal classification, the purported regulatory quotation, or removal from every domestic platform. Current no-resumption claims also need a current title-specific source.
4. **Dates:** Replaced 22 June 2012 with the independently supported year 2012. Removed the unverified 2019–2021 English publication span. A reliable source for exact dates can restore them later.
5. **English edition:** Added actual translator/editor credit: Atlas Studios. Distinguished the availability of the English edition from completion of the narrative. Platform status labels alone do not establish that the story has an ending.
6. **Volume titles:** Retained the user's agreed English renderings, explicitly labeled as translations used in this draft, not established publisher-issued English titles.
7. **Counts:** Verified directly against the supplied EPUB's nested NCX contents: 199 + 206 + 244 + 372 + 945 + 368 = 2,334. Cumulative ranges: 1–199; 200–405; 406–649; 650–1021; 1022–1966; 1967–2334. The final printed heading in Volume 4 is 373 despite 372 entries; Volume 5 ends with section 951 despite 945 entries. Do not confuse printed section numbers with entry counts. JSON audit is saved as `Reverend_Insanity_EPUB_Audit.json`.
8. **Venerables:** Corrected the assertion that only ten exist across the whole story: ten are historical predecessors of Fang Yuan. Removed the incorrect identification of Star Constellation as the first orthodox Venerable and added Primordial Origin as the first Rank 9 human and Heavenly Court founder. Removed the incorrect chronological claim about Red Lotus being the fifth Demon Venerable.
9. **Final battle:** Replaced a simple three-person alliance with the actual distinction: Fang Yuan and Giant Sun attack together; Spectral Soul is also involved but is not straightforwardly their ally. Checked the final chapter directly.
10. **Characters:** Corrected Hei Lou Lan's gender; removed unsupported “most frequently appearing” ranking. Avoided unverified Fang Zheng adoption claims. Replaced an asserted marriage between Ma Hong Yun and Zhao Lian Yun with the supported romantic relationship. Removed first-life/second-life conflations around Ma Hong Yun, excessive Ying Wu Xie detail, and subjective claims about the warmth or significance of Tai Bai Yun Sheng's relationship.
11. **Fairy Zi Wei:** Her Spirit Affinity Pavilion origin is actually supported by the Chinese, Volume 5 Chapter 31. It was retained, not incorrectly removed on the assumption that Heavenly Court membership excludes prior sect membership.
12. **Setting:** Removed “exactly one ability” for every Gu; that overstates a universal rule. Clarified that the ten historical Venerables do not impose a permanent count of Rank 9 cultivators. Removed the suggestion that all Venerables belong to the pole opposite Heavenly Court.
13. **Reception:** Replaced unsupported fame claims with attributed assessment by Qi Lu. The Guangming article includes criticism as well as praise; the revision includes both. It distinguishes author and character values and emphasizes the Ren Zu allegories. This is recommendation-series coverage, not evidence of a competitive award or specified numerical rank.
14. **Poetry:** Removed an uncited literary-identification claim as external analysis. Retained the actual EPUB stanza opening and the existence of differing synopses, without importing our unresolved poetic adaptation as a published authoritative quotation.
15. **Adaptation:** Anime-Planet supports 2018–2020, 96 chapters and cancellation. It does not establish that funding difficulties related to censorship caused cancellation. That causal claim was removed. The fan remake was omitted pending a separate check of provenance, authorization, dates and relevance.
16. **Cover and links:** The named cover image's actual file provenance/licence was not checked; its infobox placement is left for verification. Removed unverified “official website” and YouTube promotion. Did not label an inaccessible page as banned simply because it is inaccessible.

## The Omniarch paragraph and conflict of interest

The long promotional passage has been replaced with a short, attributed description based on the live About page. No independent coverage was found or established during this check for the project's significance. The paragraph is marked in an HTML comment as optional for a private/project wiki, not a presumption of due weight on Wikipedia.

If posting to Wikipedia, a person connected to the project should disclose that connection and propose the addition for review rather than treating the project's own About page as evidence of notability. A self-published source can explain what a project says about itself; it cannot independently establish that earlier translators were machine-assisted, rushed or inferior.

Do not insert the newly drafted About-page wording as a quotation from the live website until it is actually published there.

- Review fan-translation coverage for due weight, external links, copyright and conflict of interest.
