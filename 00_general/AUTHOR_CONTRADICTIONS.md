# Author Contradictions / Progress Notes — Gu Zhen Ren

## Chapter 1 vs Chapter 9 — 万家灯火 vs 数千规模

- **Ch1 Chinese:** 青茅山却不黑暗，从山腰至山脚，闪着许多莹莹的微光...虽称不上万家灯火，却也有数千的规模。 [acgo.cn/discuss/rest/53074] — Explicitly NOT ten thousand, but several thousand households.
- **Ch9 Chinese:** 而在他的背后，是明媚辉煌的万家灯火。 — Myriad lights, ten thousand homes lights idiom.
- **Contradiction:** Ch1 says village is several thousand households, Ch9 says ten thousand homes lights behind. Is author exaggerating idiom or inconsistency? 万家灯火 idiom from Bai Juyi 灯火万家城四畔星河一道水中央 [baike 5684092] means many homes lights, not literal count. So not literal contradiction but poetic exaggeration. Our fix: translate as "myriad lights of home" not literal ten thousand to avoid number clash, keep literary.

## Chapter 9 — Grey vs Heavy Darkness

- P2 灰色 veil, P69 黑暗重重, P60 浓郁的夜色铺盖下来. Author uses grey for overcast rainy look then heavy darkness for night. Grey is okay for rainy looking clouds because later we see rainy weather approaching (mountain rain coming). Heavy darkness interesting word — author maybe intentionally uses wrong words but good for atmosphere. Keep grey + heavy darkness both.

## Chapter 9 — 黑色 vs Darkness

- P2 沉重的黑色 — literal heavy black, but black can mean absence of light (漆黑的夜晚) [baike 68106]. Author uses black as darkness void, not paint colour. So darkness translation justified.

Keep adding for all chapters.
