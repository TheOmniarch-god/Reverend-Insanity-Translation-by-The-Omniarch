## 1. 寿蛊 Shou Gu

- **Chinese:** 寿蛊
- **Literal:** Longevity Gu / Lifespan Gu
- **True meaning:** Mortal Gu Rank 1-5, adds 15y, 80y, 100y, 1000y lifespan, no side effects, production controlled by Heaven's Will to kill venerables, strongest hard currency among Gu Immortals
- **Epub sample:** 寿蛊只是凡蛊。/ 要想延寿，还是寿蛊第一。/ 寿蛊是蛊仙交易中，最强劲的硬通货
- **English placeholder:** **Lifespan Gu** (NOT Longevity Gu, to avoid confusion with 长生)
- **Usage:** Ch2+ all chapters where lifespan extension discussed, e.g., eighty-year Lifespan Gu
- **Example:** 八十年寿蛊 → eighty-year Lifespan Gu

## 2. 长生 Chang Sheng

- **Chinese:** 长生
- **Literal:** Long life
- **Daoist classical:** Daodejing 长生久视之道 = way of long life and lasting vision = transcending mortality
- **Xianxia genre standard:** Defined as Immortality — Ultimate Goal of cultivator, 长生系统 = pursuit of immortality
- **Baopuzi 318 CE:** State of extended life prerequisite to becoming xian (immortal)
- **RI hierarchy:** 寻得长生的人，想要更进一步，获得永生。 = Those who found long life want further eternal life — so 长生 < 永生
  - 长生 = immortality (Gu Immortal, long but finite, killable, needs Lifespan Gu) — 10k-year turtle has longevity but not eternal life, venerables have longevity
- **True meaning in RI register:** **Immortality / Longevity / Long Life** — becoming immortal (xian), transcending mortal 100y, but still killable, finite
- **English placeholder:** **immortality** (for genre register) OR **longevity / long life** (for literal) — DECISION: Use **immortality** for RI register to match xianxia standard, as user locked
- **Places mentioned:**
  - Ch2 raw 145: 心中唯有长生大道 → Great Dao of Immortality / Eternal Life
  - Ch5 raw 113: 这就是长生的希望！！ → hope of immortality (aperture opening first hope)
  - General: 哪一个能得长生？/ 长生即逆天
- **Example:** 这就是长生的希望 → This was the hope of immortality!

## 3. 长生大道 Chang Sheng Da Dao

- **Chinese:** 长生大道
- **Literal:** Great Dao of Long Life
- **True meaning:** Great Way of Long Life / Great Dao of Immortality — ultimate path of transcending mortality, becoming xian, in RI early shorthand for eternal life pursuit
- **Epub count:** 2
  - 心中唯有长生大道 (Ch2)
  - 但凡长生大道，都是超脱之道 (Any Great Dao of Long Life is transcendent dao)
- **English placeholder:** **Great Dao of Immortality** (strict faithful to 长生 in xianxia register) OR **Great Dao of Eternal Life** (ultimate gloss linking to 永生 goal Vol 6 魔尊永生)
- **Decision:** Use **Great Dao of Eternal Life** for Fang Yuan's ultimate vow to link to final goal, but note Chinese is 长生大道 = immortality in register
- **Usage:** Ch2 P32, Ch2 raw
- **Example:** 心中唯有长生大道 → in his heart remained only the Great Dao of Eternal Life / Immortality

## 4. 永生 Yong Sheng

- **Chinese:** 永生
- **Literal:** Eternal life
- **True meaning:** **Complete invincibility + infinite lifespan** — nothing (time, space, beings, chaos, heavenly dao, River of Time) can threaten existence. Greater than longevity. Even venerables only have longevity, not eternal life. Ch2190: 永生远大于长生
- **Epub count:** 26
  - 方源矢志永生 — Fang Yuan vows eternal life
  - 寻得长生的人，想要更进一步，获得永生 — those who found long life want further eternal life
  - 我的追求……永生……究竟是否存在呢？ — does eternal life exist?
- **English placeholder:** **eternal life**
- **Places:** Vol 6 title 魔尊永生 = Demon Venerable, Eternal Life, Fang Yuan's single absolute goal
- **Example:** 矢志永生 → vows eternal life

## 5. 永生之蛊 Yong Sheng Zhi Gu

- **Chinese:** 永生之蛊 (永生蛊 0 in epub, always 永生之蛊)
- **Literal:** Eternal Life Gu
- **True meaning:** Legendary Rank 10 Gu, source of rumor in Legends of Ren Zu, requires person as catalyst, ultimate Gu that would grant eternal life + invincibility, doesn't exist yet, only rumor
- **Epub count:** 5

  - 第二只就是永生蛊。这个传闻的源头来自于《人祖传》。
  - 我们要炼出永生蛊，正需要一个人作为药引。
- **English placeholder:** **Eternal Life Gu**
- **Example:** 传说中的永生之蛊 → legendary Eternal Life Gu

## 6. 蛊仙 Gu Xian

- **Chinese:** 蛊仙
- **Literal:** Gu Immortal
- **Etymology:** 蛊 = true essence of heaven and earth (novel), 仙 = 亻+山 = person in mountains attaining dao, ascended, transcendent, celestial, fairy, wizard, true person
- **True meaning:** **Ascended / Celestial Gu Master** — Rank 6+, has immortal aperture (blessed land/grotto-heaven), dao marks, immortal essence, but lifespan finite, needs Lifespan Gu, NOT truly immortal in lifespan, can be killed
- **Epub count:** 18,895 — core term
- **English placeholder:** **Gu Immortal** (keep for familiarity, 18k mentions, readers know) but understand true meaning = ascended celestial, not immortal in lifespan
- **Example:** 七转蛊仙 → Rank 7 Gu Immortal

## 8. 长生天 Chang Sheng Tian

- **Chinese:** 长生天
- **True meaning:** Longevity Heaven — faction in Northern Plains, created by Giant Sun Immortal Venerable, grotto-heaven, not concept of longevity but name
- **English placeholder:** **Longevity Heaven** (NOT Eternal Life Heaven, because Chinese is 长生天)

## Hierarchy Summary (Author's own differentiation)

**寿蛊 Lifespan Gu (adds years, mortal Gu, hard currency) → 长生 immortality / longevity (long but finite, Gu Immortal, killable, turtle 10k y, venerables) → 永生 eternal life (invincible + infinite, Rank 10 Eternal Life Gu, ultimate goal, even venerables don't have)**

**Formula from epub:** 寻得长生的人，想要更进一步，获得永生。 = Those who found immortality/longevity want further eternal life.
