# Preface: Either Towards Success or Towards Destruction

In the past few months I have lived through pain in both body and mind, and I have been struggling with it.

Life is like a balance scale, with reality on one end and the inner self on the other. For me the reality side grew heavier and heavier until the scale tipped, and that is where the pain came from.

People walk a road for so long that they lose themselves in the scenery along the way and often forget why they set out in the first place.

That is what happened. In the midst of that pain I felt waves of bewilderment.

Since my last book was completed, I have been on a break, trying to find my direction and recover the reason I set out in the first place.

That was about five or six years ago. I had already been reading webnovels for a while. The first was A Journey to the Ethereal, then Blasphemy, Zhu Xian, Mad God, House of Beast Tamers, Boiling Beast Blood, Mount Shu, I Alone Am Immortal, and so on.

I had read a great many novels, but a knot of frustration built up in my chest. The protagonists I saw were almost always blessed with heaven defying fortune and were upright and noble in character. The villains were mostly foolish, deranged and ugly, their titles resounding, yet they were all paper tigers, impressive to look at until they met the protagonist, and then they wilted.

So I began to think that I wanted to see a truly great villain.

This villain would be uncompromising and solitary, proud, dark and decisive in killing, never weak in the knees. He would never hide his own evil, and he would have no false goodness at all.

At a glance you could tell he was utterly evil, his demonic aura stark and his killing intent filling the air. What he was best at was trampling rules and slaughtering the famed righteous sects.

Alone, he would be an enemy to the whole world, sowing terror among all living things. He would be a great master of the demonic path, sometimes arrogant and domineering, sometimes sinister and cunning, sometimes cold and merciless, standing proudly at the summit and looking down on the world with disdain, while every brave soul who dared to challenge him came to a miserable end.

He was cruel in his methods and perhaps even dabbled with Gu worms. In short, you could tell at a glance that he was no decent person at all.

So what should his name be... Super Demon King? Black Mountain Old Demon? Green Robe Ancestor?

No, no, no.

After thinking it over, I thought, why not call him Gu Zhen Ren?

It just so happened that I needed to create an account on Qidian at the time, so I thought, okay, and that ID was born.

Some TV dramas always do it like this: the villain defeats the protagonist and is just one final lethal strike away from killing him. Then the villain starts rambling on and on, while the protagonist secretly gathers his strength for one last gamble, and every time he turns the tables and wins a complete victory.

If the situation is reversed and the villain is the one at a disadvantage, it goes like this. The protagonist unleashes his power and the villain is beaten to the ground. Facing the protagonist's sword, the villain suddenly bursts into tears, wailing and swearing to mend his ways. The protagonist, who is kindhearted and benevolent, hesitates. At that moment the villain launches a desperate counterattack, but it fails. The protagonist, who sees every detail with perfect clarity, completely subdues him. His heart hardens at the same time. He shouts, "Despicable, shameless, vile," and finally kills the villain.

There are also villains who kidnap the protagonist's family and friends, and almost never succeed in using them as leverage. Even with a huge advantage, they somehow get defeated by the protagonist for all sorts of unavoidable reasons and in baffling ways.

It was about six years ago that I created the ID Gu Zhen Ren, because I had a wish in my heart to see a truly evil archvillain. He would have his own way of doing things, tolerated by no one in this world, roaming free and unrestrained and disdaining any explanation. He would be decisive in killing, never trapped by feelings, and never weak in the knees even at the sight of a beautiful woman.

His dreaded name made the whole world tremble, and at the mere mention of him people paled.

Those who understood him admired him, revered him and loved him. Those who hated him gritted their teeth and used every means they had, only to make him much stronger.

He walked his own path, solitude wrapped around him like a cloak, and his cold eyes could see through every falsehood.

When faced with criticism and rejection, he scoffed with disdain. When faced with hollow praise and flattery, he sneered in silence.

He could endure solitude. He could even enjoy it.

If he was not walking towards success, then he was walking towards destruction.

He was a warrior, making a lone pilgrimage through darkness, cutting through thorns and brambles all the way. No matter what stood in his way, nothing could stop him. If a god stood in his way, he would kill the god. If a Buddha stood in his way, he would kill the Buddha!

This dream had always been buried deep in my heart. During these years of writing, I thought several times about starting to write it, but nothing came of it in the end. I did not quite dare. I felt my skill was not yet good enough.

And so, so many years passed. I went through some things, and compared with when I first started writing, much of it now is a case of things remaining but people having changed.

The past was somewhat unbearable to look back on, and this dream was also hidden away in the dust of time, gradually forgotten by me. Even when I did remember it now and then, I felt I was not prepared enough and still lacked the ability to write such a cathartic story.

But these past few days I stopped walking forward and looked back, and I suddenly understood something. Life is not cooking. When you cook, you usually have the oil, salt, soy sauce and vinegar prepared beforehand, and only then do you start.

Life does not wait for you to be ready. Even when you think everything is ready, do you not still lack a wisp of east wind?

Life is always rushed. Many things happen suddenly with little to no preparation.

Fate is unpredictable and always changing. No one can tell what will happen tomorrow.

So I made a decision. Against the backdrop of the prophesied end of the world in 2012 drawing near, I would write a new book.

This book would be called Gu Zhen Ren.

No other title could suit it better.

This is a cause planted six years ago, bearing fruit in the present.

Giving the book the same name has no other meaning. It is not narcissism or arrogance, just a way to fulfill a dream. To fulfill that original dream and to recover that feeling that once moved me.

There is no doubt that this is a book that will invite controversy. In the words of the LongKong crowd, this is what you would call having warped values.

Yes, I will say it plainly here. This is a book with warped values.

Because the protagonist of this book is a great villain who will stop at nothing to achieve his ends and never gives up, supremely evil. While the villains in this book are mostly great, glorious and upright young heroes, or reputable and honorable orthodox sects.

I know this will disappoint many people, but please try to understand. Those who abandon their principles the moment they see a beautiful woman, do they still have the bearing of a peerless demon king or a great boss of the demonic path?

That is just being lovesick.

That is right. This book will kill women, and beautiful women at that. It will kill them in great numbers and without restraint.

And it will never hold back. No matter what kind of existence it is, as long as it is an enemy. No matter how powerful he is, it will fight him. No matter how beautiful she is, it will kill her all the same.

This book is very evil. Read with caution.

Reading is, after all, a hobby, something done for pleasure.

You read to have fun. If you are truly not happy, if you do not enjoy it, then do not read it. Just switch to another book. There are a great many good books on Qidian, and I will recommend one or two as I write.

If you cannot continue, if you feel unhappy, if you feel your outlook on life, your values and your morals are being trampled, then you have my respectful send off.

Those who want to read it are naturally welcome.

The above is the preface to this book, and also a declaration.

# Chapter 1: Though the Flesh Perish, the Demonic Heart Knows No Regret

“Fang Yuan! Hand over the Spring Autumn Cicada without struggle, and I shall grant you a quick death!”

“Old Demon Fang, harbour no delusions of defiance! Today, the revered sects of the Righteous Path have united for one purpose: to trample your demonic lair into dust. We have long since laid an inescapable dragnet here. This time, you shall be beheaded!”

“Fang Yuan, you damned devil! To refine that cursed Spring Autumn Cicada, you slaughtered tens of millions of innocents! You have committed towering atrocities, unpardonable and beyond all count!”

“Monster! Three hundred years ago, you violated me and robbed me of my chastity. You killed my entire family and slaughtered all my kin. From that moment on, I’ve wanted nothing more than to devour your flesh and drink your blood! Today, I’ll make you wish you were dead!”

* * *

Fang Yuan stood cloaked in a tattered emerald robe, his hair wild and unkempt, his body bathed in blood, as his eyes swept over the surrounding host. The mountain wind whipped his blood-soaked garments, fluttering and snapping like a war banner. Fresh blood gushed out from the hundreds of wounds across his body. In the mere moment he had stood there, a large pool of blood had already gathered at his feet. Besieged on every flank by bitter foes, every path to life had long been severed. The trap had snapped shut; on this day, death was absolute.

Fang Yuan perceived his doom with perfect clarity. Yet even as the shadow of death loomed before him, he remained expressionless, his demeanor unmoved. His gaze was dark and deep, like an ancient well, bottomless as ever. The righteous host besieging him comprised distinguished sect leaders and young heroes renowned far and wide. Yet as they surrounded Fang Yuan, their composure fractured: some bared their teeth in savage roars, some sneered with brittle arrogance, some narrowed their eyes with venomous suspicion, while others clutched bleeding wounds, looking upon him with lingering dread.

None dared to make a move, all feared Fang Yuan's final counterattack before death. Thus they confronted each other tensely for six hours, until the sun set in the west, and the afterglow lit the evening clouds by the mountainside, momentarily resplendent as fire. Fang Yuan, who had stood as motionless as a statue, slowly turned around. That solitary motion sent a convulsion through the host; the multitude recoiled in unison, retreating a full pace in panic. At this moment, the grey-white mountain rocks beneath Fang Yuan’s feet had long been dyed dark red with fresh blood. His face, pale from excessive blood loss, was reflected by the sunset glow, suddenly taking on an alluring, delicate radiance.

Gazing upon the green mountains and the setting sun, Fang Yuan let slip a soft, low chuckle:

“Green mountains beneath the setting sun,
Autumn moon and spring breeze.
In the morning one's hair is like black silk,
By dusk it turns to snow.
Right and wrong, success and failure,
All vanish in a turn of the head.”

[^1]: 朝如青丝暮成雪 ,  from Li Bai's poetry on the swift passage of time.
[^2]: 是非成败转头空 ,  from Yang Shen's Lin Jiang Xian (Immortal by the River), famously used in Romance of the Three Kingdoms opening.

As the verse left his lips, memories of his past life on Earth surged unbidden before his eyes. He had originally been a Huaxia student on Earth, cast into this foreign world through a serendipitous twist of fortune. Three hundred years of drifting, tossed like flotsam upon tempestuous tides; two hundred years of unbridled dominion, carving his will across the world, more than five centuries, yet in the retrospect of memory, gone in the span of an eyeblink.

Countless memories buried deep within the bedrock of his soul awakened, playing across his mind with breathtaking, indelible clarity.

“In the end, I failed after all,” Fang Yuan sighed in his heart. A trace of wistfulness stirred, yet not the faintest whisper of regret.

This outcome was nothing he had not foreseen; the moment he made his choice centuries ago, he was already mentally prepared. For what was the Demonic Path? It was to accumulate no good karma, only murder and arson; spurned by heaven and reviled by earth, making enemies with the whole world, and still running wild, following the whim of one's own heart to the very end.

“If this newly refined Spring Autumn Cicada works, I will still be a demon in my next life!”

Thinking like this, Fang Yuan could not help laughing out loud.

“Old Demon, what are you laughing at?!”

“Everyone be careful, the demon is at death’s door and wants to counterattack!”

“Quickly hand over the Spring Autumn Cicada!!”

The righteous host surged forward, greed and panic driving them into frenzy. And in that very breath. With a thunderous blast, Fang Yuan brazenly self-detonated.

* * *

Spring rain fell continuously, silently nourishing Qing Mao Mountain. Night was already deep, and threads of cool wind blew through the fine rain. Qing Mao Mountain was not dark, however; from the mountainside down to the foothills, faint, shimmering lights glowed, as if draping a brilliant ribbon of radiance across the slope. These lights came from the stilt houses, not ten thousand households, but several thousand nonetheless. This was Gu Yue Village, nestled upon Qing Mao Mountain, giving the vast, tranquil mountain range a distinct sense of human habitation. At the very heart of Gu Yue Village stood a grand pavilion. Right now a solemn sacrificial ceremony was being held, so it was brightly lit and resplendent.

“Ancestors bless us! May this Aperture Opening Ceremony produce many youths of excellent aptitude to bring fresh blood and hope to our clan!”

The Gu Yue Clan Leader appeared middle-aged, his temples touched with frost. Wearing plain white ceremonial robes, he knelt upon the amber floorboards, his upper body erect, hands pressed together, closing both eyes to pray sincerely. He faced a tall, black-lacquered altar table with three tiers, enshrining the spirit tablets of the ancestors. On both sides of the tablets stood red-copper incense burners, thin plumes of fragrant smoke coiling upward. Behind him, dozen or so figures knelt in matching white sacrificial robes. These were the clan elders and decision-makers, each wielding power over various affairs of the clan.

After concluding his prayers, the Clan Leader was the first to bend down, palms flat against the wooden floorboards, kowtowing. His forehead met the floor with soft, rhythmic thuds. The elders behind him, their expressions solemn, followed suit in silence. For a time, the ancestral hall was filled only with the gentle cadence of foreheads knocking against the floor. When the ceremony concluded, everyone slowly rose from the floor and quietly filed out of the hallowed hall. In the corridor, the elders breathed a quiet sigh of relief, the tense atmosphere easing noticeably.

Murmurs of conversation soon began to rise:

“Time truly flies. In the blink of an eye, another full year has gone by.”

“The previous Aperture Opening Ceremony feels like it happened just yesterday; the memory is still vivid.”

“Tomorrow is the annual Aperture Opening Ceremony. I wonder what kind of fresh blood our clan will produce this year?”

“Sigh, I only hope a youth with Grade A aptitude emerges. It has been three years since our Gu Yue clan had such a genius.”

“Indeed. Both Bai Village and Xiong Village have seen remarkable talents emerge over recent years. In particular, Bai Ning Bing of the Bai clan, his natural talent is truly terrifying.”

Whoever mentioned the name Bai Ning Bing, a shadow of worry involuntarily crossed the faces of the clan elders. This youth’s talent was extraordinary. In just two short years, he had already cultivated to Rank 3 Gu Master, standing unrivaled among the younger generation. Even the older generation felt immense pressure from this rising star. Given time, he would undoubtedly become the pillar of Bai Village, at least a powerhouse standing on his own. No one ever doubted this.

“However, among the youths participating in this year’s Aperture Opening Ceremony, it is not as though we have no hope.”

“Quite right. A genius youth has appeared in the Fang lineage. He could speak at three months and walk at four. By age five, he could compose Tang poetry and Song lyrics, exceptionally intelligent and overflowing with talent. It is just a pity his parents died early, and he is currently being raised by his aunt and uncle.”

“Mm, he possesses precocious wisdom and grand ambitions. In recent years, I have heard of the poems he composed, Bring in the Wine, Ode to the Plum Blossom, and Jiang Cheng Zi. He is truly a genius!”

The Gu Yue Clan Leader was the last to exit the ancestral hall. As he slowly closed the door, the murmurs of the elders reached his ears. He immediately knew that the elders were discussing a youth named Gu Yue Fang Yuan. As the leader of the clan, he naturally paid close attention to outstanding juniors, and Gu Yue Fang Yuan was the most prominent and dazzling among them. Experience had shown that those gifted with photographic memories or strength rivaling an adult from childhood almost always possessed superior cultivation talent.

“If this child tests with Grade A aptitude and is properly nurtured, he may well be able to contend with Bai Ning Bing. Even if he only has Grade B aptitude, he will certainly become a stalwart pillar and a banner for the Gu Yue clan in the future. Yet with such precocious intellect, Grade B is unlikely, it is overwhelmingly probable that he has Grade A.”

With this thought, the corners of the Clan Leader’s lips lifted slightly into a smile. He cleared his throat with a dry cough and addressed the elders: “Everyone, the hour is late. For the sake of tomorrow’s Aperture Opening Ceremony, tonight please be sure to rest well and conserve your energy.” Hearing this, the elders paused slightly. In the glances they exchanged, a subtle trace of vigilance was concealed. The Clan Leader’s words were polite and restrained, but everyone understood their deeper meaning. Every year, in order to recruit talented juniors into their own factions, the elders fought tooth and nail until their faces flushed red and heads bled.

It was indeed time to rest and gather strength, so they could contend fiercely tomorrow. Especially for that Gu Yue Fang Yuan, the probability of him possessing Grade A aptitude was exceptionally high. Moreover, both his parents were deceased, leaving him and his brother as the only surviving orphans of the Fang lineage. If one could bring him into their own faction and groom him well, it could guarantee a century of prosperity for their line!

“However, let me make the ugly words clear first. Compete openly and honorably; underhanded schemes that harm clan unity will not be tolerated. Elders, bear this firmly in mind!” the Clan Leader warned sternly.

“We wouldn’t dare, we wouldn’t dare.”

“We shall definitely keep it well in mind.”

“Then we shall take our leave. Lord Clan Leader, please do not trouble yourself to see us off.”

Harbouring their own calculations, the elders dispersed one by one. Before long, the long corridor grew quiet and desolate. Spring rain and slanted wind blew through the window; the Clan Leader stepped lightly towards the window frame. Instantly, a mouthful of fresh, moist mountain air filled his chest, refreshing to the soul. This was the third floor of the attic; the Clan Leader looked out, and more than half of Gu Yue Village could be seen in a single glance. Though it was late at night, lights still burned in most homes in the village, quite unlike usual.

Tomorrow was the Aperture Opening Ceremony, touching upon everyone’s personal interests. An excited and nervous atmosphere covered the clansmen’s hearts, naturally making many sleep restlessly.

“This is the future hope of our clan,” with dots of light reflected in his eyes, the Clan Leader sighed softly.

* * *

At this very moment, another pair of clear eyes quietly gazed upon those twinkling lights in the deep of the night, brimming with complex emotions.

“Gu Yue Village… this is five hundred years ago?! The Spring Autumn Cicada really had effect…”

Fang Yuan’s gaze was dark and profound as he stood beside the window, letting the wind and rain beat against his body. The effect of the Spring Autumn Cicada was to reverse time. Ranked seventh among the Ten Great Mystical Gu, it was naturally no ordinary existence.

In short: rebirth.

“Reborn using the Spring Autumn Cicada… back to five hundred years ago!”

Fang Yuan reached out his hand, staring intently at his young, tender, and somewhat pale palm. Then he slowly clenched it into a fist, feeling the vivid reality with all his strength. He heard the gentle pitter-patter of drizzle tapping against the window lattice. He closed his eyes slowly, opening them only after a long moment, letting out a wistful sigh:

“Five hundred years of experience… it truly feels like a dream.”

Yet he knew with absolute certainty: this was no dream.

# Chapter 2: Reversing Time, Five Hundred Years of Enlightenment
## 第二节：逆光阴五百年觉悟

In legends, there exists a River of Time in this world, supporting the flow of all existence. And by utilizing the power of the Spring Autumn Cicada, one can travel upstream against the current, returning to the past. Regarding this rumor, the world was divided in opinion. Many did not believe it, while some half-believed and half-doubted. Almost no one was truly certain. Because every time the Spring Autumn Cicada was used, one had to pay with their life, sacrificing their entire body and all of their cultivation base as the driving power.

This price was truly too steep, and what made it even harder to accept was that even after paying with one’s life, the outcome remained unknown. Even if someone obtained the Spring Autumn Cicada, they would never dare use it casually on a whim. What if the rumor was false, nothing more than a hoax? If Fang Yuan had not been driven to a dead end, he would not have used it so readily. However, now, Fang Yuan believed completely and thoroughly. Because the ironclad truth lay before his eyes, beyond all dispute. He had indeed been reborn!

“It is only a pity for such a good Gu. Back then, it took monumental effort, the slaughter of hundreds of thousands of people, stirring the wrath of heaven and the resentment of men, enduring endless tribulations before I finally refined it…” Fang Yuan sighed inwardly. Though he had been reborn, the Spring Autumn Cicada had not traveled back with him.

Humans are the spirit of all living things; Gu are the essence of heaven and earth. Gu were bizarre and varied, countless in number. Some Gu would completely dissipate after being used once or twice. Other Gu, as long as they were not used excessively, could be used repeatedly. Perhaps the Spring Autumn Cicada was simply that kind of expendable Gu that could only be used once.

“However, even if it is gone, I can simply refine another one. If I could refine it in my past life, could I not in this life?” Pity aside, a surge of ambitious pride could not help rising in Fang Yuan’s heart.

To be able to be reborn, the loss of the Spring Autumn Cicada was entirely acceptable. Moreover, he still carried a priceless treasure; he was far from empty-handed. This treasure was his five hundred years of memories and experience. Within his memory lay countless secret troves, currently unopened by anyone. There lay major historical events one by one, allowing him to easily grasp the pulse of history. There lay countless figures: some were hidden experts who cultivate in deep seclusion, some were extraordinary prodigies, and some were not even born yet. There also lay the grueling experience of bitter cultivation over these five hundred years, and a rich reservoir of battle experience.

With these, he undoubtedly held the overall situation and the initiative. As long as he played his cards well, roaming the mortal realm and recreating his former glory as a demonic overlord and ruthless hegemon would be no problem at all, and he could even take a step further, charging towards an even higher realm!

“Then how should I proceed…?” Fang Yuan was extremely rational. He quickly gathered his emotions and turned towards the night rain outside the window, falling into deep thought.

Thinking like this, he felt a thousand loose ends and ten thousand tangled threads. After pondering for a moment, his brow furrowed deeper and deeper. A span of five hundred years was truly rather long. Not to mention those memories that were already blurred and could no longer be recalled, even among the secret treasure grounds and immortal opportunities he did remember, although numerous, most were either separated by hundreds of thousands of miles, or required specific times to open.

“The most crucial factor is still cultivation base. Right now, my primeval sea is not yet open; I have not even stepped onto the cultivation path of a Gu Master. I am fundamentally a mortal! I must cultivate as quickly as possible and raise my cultivation base, moving ahead of history to seize the initiative as much as possible and grab enough benefits.”

Moreover, without sufficient cultivation, he could not make full use of many hidden troves even if he obtained them. Instead, they would become dangerous liabilities, merely possessing such treasures would make him a target. The first difficult problem placed before Fang Yuan was his cultivation base. He had to raise his cultivation base as quickly as possible; if he was as slow as in his past life, even the daylilies would have gone cold.

“To raise my cultivation base quickly, I must rely on the resources of the clan. In my current situation, I fundamentally lack the capability to traverse the peril-laden mountain ranges; even an ordinary wild boar could take my life. Only if I reach the cultivation base of a Rank 3 Gu Master will I have the baseline ability to protect myself and traverse the mountains and rivers of this world.”

Through the eyes of a demonic overlord tempered by five hundred years, this Qing Mao Mountain was truly too small, and Gu Yue Village was more like a cage. However, while a cage imprisons freedom, a sturdy cell often represents a kind of safety.

“Hmph. For now, I’ll make my moves within this cage. As long as I advance to Rank 3 Gu Master, I will leave this impoverished mountain backwater. Fortunately, tomorrow is the Aperture Opening Ceremony, and shortly after, I can officially begin cultivating as a Gu Master.”

At the thought of the Aperture Opening Ceremony, memories long buried in dust surfaced within Fang Yuan’s heart.

“Aptitude, is it…?” Looking outside the window, he could not help letting out three cold chuckles.

Just at this moment, the room door was gently pushed open, and a youth walked in.

“Brother, why are you standing by the window letting the rain fall on you?”

The youth was thin in build, slightly shorter than Fang Yuan, his face bearing an uncanny resemblance to his own. Fang Yuan turned his head to look at this youth, a trace of complexity flashing across his face.

“So it is you, my twin brother.” He raised his brows slightly, his expression returning to his habitual coldness.

Fang Zheng lowered his head, looking down at his toes, his trademark habit. “Seeing that brother’s window wasn’t closed, I wanted to slip in quietly and shut it. Tomorrow is the Aperture Opening Ceremony. Brother, if Uncle and Aunt find out you’re still up this late, I’m afraid they’ll worry.” He was not surprised by Fang Yuan’s coldness, because from childhood to now, his brother had always been like this. Sometimes he would think that perhaps geniuses were just different from ordinary mortals. Although he shared a nearly identical face with his brother, he felt as ordinary as an ant.

Born from the same womb at the same time, why was heaven so unfair? It had given his brother diamond-like talent, while he was ordinary like a roadside stone. Everyone around him, whenever mentioning him, would always say: “This is Fang Yuan’s brother.” His uncle and aunt also often lectured him: “You must learn from your brother.” Sometimes, even looking at his own face in the mirror filled him with a certain disgust! These thoughts had existed for many years, accumulating deep inside his heart day by day. They weighed on his chest like a boulder. Over the years, Fang Zheng’s head had hung lower and lower, and he had grown increasingly silent.

“Worry…?” Thinking of his uncle and aunt, Fang Yuan scoffed inwardly.

He remembered very clearly that the parents of this body had both fallen during a clan mission. When he was merely three years old, he and his brother had become orphans. Under the pretext of raising them, his uncle and aunt had openly and legally usurped their parents’ inheritance, and treated the two brothers harshly. Originally, as a transmigrator, he had planned to bide his time in obscurity. But the hardship of survival had forced Fang Yuan to choose displaying talent different from normal people. The so-called “genius” was actually nothing more than the rationality of a mature soul, paired with a few Tang poems and Song verses from Earth that echoed through generations.

Just testing his hands like this, he was amazed at as a heavenly being, receiving widespread attention. Under external pressure, it also made the young Fang Yuan have to choose a cold expression to disguise and protect himself, reducing the chance of exposing his secrets. Over time, coldness instead became his habitual expression. Just like this, his uncle and aunt could no longer treat him and his brother harshly. As his age grew and his future looked brighter, his treatment also increased along with it. However, this was not love, but an investment.

The laughable thing was that this brother of his had never seen through this truth. Not only was he blinded by their uncle and aunt, but he had even harboured a buried resentment towards Fang Yuan. Don’t look at him being so well-behaved and honest now; in Fang Yuan’s memories, once he tested with Grade A aptitude and was heavily nurtured by the clan, all of his hidden hatred and jealousy had been released, and he had spared no effort in targeting, making things difficult for, and suppressing his own elder brother.

And as for his own aptitude…? Heh. At best, merely of Grade C. Fate always loved to play jokes. Born as twins, the elder brother’s aptitude was only of Grade C, yet monopolized the name of genius for over a decade. The younger brother had been unknown, yet possessed Grade A natural talent. The result of the Aperture Opening Ceremony had left the entire clan stunned. It also completely reversed the two brothers’ situation and treatment. The younger brother was like a crouching dragon ascending the heavens; the elder brother was like a fledgling phoenix crashing to the earth.

After that, came repeated difficulties from his brother, cold eyes from his uncle and aunt, and contempt from the clansmen.

Did he hate them?

In his past life, Fang Yuan had hated. He hated his own insufficient aptitude, hated the clan’s heartlessness, and hated fate’s unfairness. But now, as he reexamined that part of his life with five hundred years of experience behind him, he felt not a ripple of hatred.

What was there to resent?

Thinking from their perspective, he could also understand his brother, his uncle and aunt, as well as those righteous powerhouses who besieged him five hundred years later. The strong devour the weak; survival of the fittest, that was the very nature of this world. Moreover, everyone has their own ambitions, all fighting for that sliver of heavenly opportunity. Between each other suppressing and killing, what was there not to understand? Five hundred years of experience had long made him see through all of this; in his heart, there remained only the Great Dao of Eternal Life.

If someone stood in the way of this pursuit, no matter who, it was nothing more than: either you die or I live. The wild ambition in his heart was too immense. Stepping onto this path, one was destined to make enemies of the entire world, destined to walk alone, and destined to face relentless tribulations. This was the enlightenment distilled from five hundred years of life.

“Vengeance is not my intention, and the demonic path also never has the word compromise.” Thinking to this point, Fang Yuan could not help laughing. Turning his head towards this brother of his, taking a faint glance, he said: “You can go.”

Fang Zheng felt a sudden tremor in his heart, feeling that his brother’s gaze was as sharp as an icy blade, seeming to pierce into the deepest part of his heart. Under that gaze, he felt as if he were standing naked in the snow, every secret laid bare.

“Then… see you tomorrow, brother.” Daring not speak more right now, Fang Zheng slowly closed the room door, murmuring compliance as he retreated.

# Chapter 3: Kindly Piss Off
## 第三节：请一边玩去

Clack, clack-clack. Clack, clack-clack. The night watchman was making his rounds, striking his wooden clapper in a steady rhythm. As the hollow strikes drifted into the stilt house, Fang Yuan opened his dry, heavy eyelids, murmuring inwardly: “It is the fifth watch.” Yesternight, he had lain in bed pondering long into the night, making plans and arrangements. By his reckoning, he had slept for barely a little over two hours. This body had not yet begun cultivation and had little stamina; he still felt tired, and sleep kept tugging at his body and mind.

Yet five hundred years of trials had long tempered Fang Yuan’s willpower into profound, unyielding steel. A faint trace of lethargy meant nothing to him. Without hesitation, he tossed aside the thin silk quilt and rose to his feet with crisp precision. Pushing open the wooden window, he saw that the spring rain had ceased. Shen Cui attended him with gentle care, a bright smile on her face and coquettish warmth in her eyes. As she helped Fang Yuan dress and fasten his clasps, she brushed her plump bosom against his arm and back from time to time. Fang Yuan remained expressionless, his heart like still water. This maidservant was not only his aunt and uncle’s informant, but she was also vain and fickle. In his past life, he had once been blinded by her charms; yet after the Aperture Opening Ceremony, when his status plummeted overnight, she instantly turned against him, giving him no shortage of cold disdain.

Living with his elder brother all these years and benefiting from Fang Yuan’s care, he too had a servant to attend him. Yet it was not a young maid like Shen Cui, but a fat, bloated old woman.

Originally, he had no servant to attend him; it was actually Fang Yuan who took the initiative to demand one for him. Although Fang Zheng was the master and Shen Cui the servant, he ordinarily did not dare look down on her. Her mother was Granny Shen, who attended their aunt and also managed the entire household. Deeply trusted by their aunt, she wielded considerable authority.

To her, Fang Yuan had a bright future and was very likely to possess Grade A aptitude. If she could become his concubine, she could rise from servant to mistress, a single leap to the heavens. In his previous life, Fang Yuan had been deceived, even falling in love with this maidservant. Now, reborn, he saw through her completely, his heart as cold as frost.

Shen Cui pouted slightly, feeling puzzled and a little hurt by his indifference to her advances today. She wanted to say something coquettish, but an inexplicable air about Fang Yuan, barely perceptible, intimidated her. She opened her mouth several times, but in the end merely said, “Yes,” and obediently left.

His younger brother stood blankly at the doorway. Lowering his head to look at his toes, he gave a quiet murmur of assent. He had actually woken during the fourth watch. Too nervous to sleep, he had slipped out of bed and prepared himself early. There were dark circles under both his eyes. Fang Yuan nodded. In his previous life, he had not understood what was on his younger brother’s mind. But in this life, how could he fail to understand? There was no point in exposing it now, however. He simply said, “Then let’s go.”

By now, the horizon glowed with the light of dawn, and Fang Yuan’s shadow fell across his face. The morning sun was gradually rising, yet Fang Zheng suddenly felt that he was walking into darkness. This darkness came from his elder brother. Perhaps, for his entire life, he would never escape the enormous shadow his brother cast over him. Tightness gripped his chest again and again, until even breathing became difficult. This damned feeling brought one word to mind: suffocation!

Devoid of love and loyalty, she is nothing more than flesh. Her, in my harem? She’s not worthy. So what if they were his uncle and aunt, the clan leader, or the clan elders? They were all merely passing through his life. Why exhaust his ingenuity and waste his energy putting these passersby in their place?

He chuckled.

As long as they do not block my path, they can piss off. I wouldn’t even deign to step on them.

# Chapter 5: Ren Zu's Three Gu, Hope Opens the Aperture

He gained a reliable source of food and the strength to protect himself. He took eagerly to fighting and defeated many Predicaments. But he soon suffered for it. He decided to sustain the most beautiful of the three Gu with his middle years, when he was in the prime of his strength. Then the second Gu gave him wisdom. With wisdom, Ren Zu learned to think and reflect on his actions, and began accumulating experience. He discovered that wisdom was often more effective than strength. With both, he was able, for a time, to overcome much that had previously been beyond him and kill more Predicaments. He ate their flesh and drank their blood, tenaciously surviving in this way. But the good times did not last. Ren Zu grew old, older and older. This was because he had offered his youth and middle years to Strength Gu and Wisdom Gu.

Once a person grows old, their muscles waste away and their mind no longer works as quickly.

Human, all you need to do is give me your heart.”

# Chapter 7: Gu Masters Have Nine Ranks; Flower-Wine Monk Leaves His Hidden Inheritance

Soon, a week had gone by.

The wonder and power of Gu Masters had long taken root in the youths' hearts, so everything the elder taught left them completely captivated.

"If you cultivate diligently from now on, your cultivation will naturally rise, and advancing to Rank II or Rank III will also be possible. Of course, the higher your aptitude, the greater the chance of advancement."

"With Grade D aptitude, the primeval sea occupies twenty to thirty percent of the aperture; such people can at most cultivate to Rank I or Rank II. With Grade C aptitude, the primeval sea occupies forty to fifty percent of the aperture; such people usually stall at Rank II, with only a tiny fraction lucky enough to break through to Rank III initial stage. With Grade B aptitude, the primeval sea occupies sixty to seventy percent of the aperture; such people can cultivate to Rank III, and even as high as Rank IV. With Grade A aptitude, the primeval sea occupies eighty to ninety percent of the aperture and is abundant; naturally, such people possess the highest talent and are the most suited to Gu cultivation, capable of reaching Rank V."

The youths all pricked up their ears, listening intently with eyes shining bright.

Many could not help but look towards Gu Yue Fang Zheng, who sat in solemn posture in the first row. This was a Grade A aptitude, after all! Their gazes were filled with envy and jealousy.

At the same time, some gazes drifted towards the corner in the very last row of the classroom.

In that corner by the window, Gu Yue Fang Yuan was sprawled over his desk, fast asleep and snoring soundly.

"Look, he's still sleeping," someone whispered.

"He's been sleeping through classes for a week straight. Hasn't he recovered yet?" someone said with a sneer.

"More than that! I heard he doesn't even return home at night, wandering all around the outskirts of the village."

As the murmurs around him grew louder and louder, the academy elder's frown deepened.

In the entire classroom, the youths all sat straight and proper, radiating youthful vigor; this made Fang Yuan, who lay sprawled asleep over his desk, stand out even more glaringly.
