# WebNovel Official Mistakes — Called Out

## Chapter 9
- **P1 红日西沉** = red sun sinks westward. WebNovel: "sunset was a red hue setting over the east." [m.webnovel.com/book/reverend-insanity.../two-people-who-start-on-the-same-road-gradually-becoming-distant_21545157006517880] — East is physically impossible, sun sets west. Correct: westward.
- **Title:** WebNovel "Two people who start on the same road, gradually becoming distant" — over-literal, long, loses literary parallel to final scene 渐行渐远 drifting into dark road vs myriad lights behind. Our literary: Drifting Further Apart captures both literal walk + metaphorical drift.

## Chapter 1
- WebNovel correct on west: "sunset painted the peaks in wildfire hues" matches Chinese 夕阳西下 [tw.hjwzw.com/Book/Read/32808,6420546] — no east/west error here, unlike Ch9.
