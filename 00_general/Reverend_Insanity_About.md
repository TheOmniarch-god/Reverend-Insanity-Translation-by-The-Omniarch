# About This Translation

## Read closely. Translate faithfully.

This is an independent English translation project for *Reverend Insanity* (《蛊真人》) by Gu Zhen Ren, edited by **The Omniarch**.

The aim is straightforward: to bring the novel into natural, compelling English without quietly replacing the author’s meaning with the translator’s imagination. Its severity, humor, coarse dismissals, philosophical reflections, and moments of beauty deserve the same attention as its cultivation terminology.

Good prose matters. So does knowing where the Chinese ends and interpretation begins.

## How the work is done

Each passage begins with the Chinese source. Our review follows four stages: the original text, a close breakdown of its language, an explanation of the translation choices, and a proposed English rendering.

That process makes small decisions visible. Does a word describe a feeling or a physical action? Is a title an official rank, a social position, or a figurative expression? Does an idiom need its original image, or an English expression that carries the same force? These questions are considered before the prose is polished.

When a passage depends on the novel’s mechanics, later explanations are checked for context. They help us understand the scene; they are not inserted early into the translation. Chapter text and boundaries are verified against the project’s Chinese EPUB rather than reconstructed from online summaries. Secondary references may help orient a question, but the Chinese text remains the authority for what a passage says.

## Faithfulness without stiffness

Literal wording is not always faithful English. An idiom translated character by character can obscure what the author meant. Yet fluent English can be equally misleading if it introduces ancestry, rank, scenery, emotion, or explanation that the source never supplies.

We work between those risks: preserving meaning and emphasis while giving the English a clear, natural rhythm. We do not soften an insult merely because it is crude, embellish a scene merely because it could sound grander, or turn every Chinese expression into an English cliché.

Names and recurring terms are tracked for consistency. When a decision changes, affected earlier chapters are revisited. Translation notes record important distinctions, uncertainties, and the reasoning behind revisions.

## A collaborative, revisable edition

The project uses AI-assisted drafting and analysis alongside close editorial review. The Omniarch directs the English edition and makes the final editorial choices. Neither a fluent draft nor an automated explanation is treated as proof of accuracy; consequential claims must be checked against the source.

This is a developing translation, not a claim of perfection. Reader questions can reveal unclear staging, misplaced emphasis, or a word that carries more than the Chinese intended. Corrections are part of the work, not something to hide.

The reading text stays uncluttered. Poems retain their line breaks, italics are used with restraint, and paragraphs follow the movement of the scene rather than breaking after every sentence. Detailed discussion belongs in the accompanying notes.

## The work and its author

*Reverend Insanity* and its original Chinese text are the work of **Gu Zhen Ren**. This project is an independent fan translation; it does not claim to be an official or authorized edition.
