# Literary Editing Notes — Modern Tolkien Standard

**British:** grey, colour, behaviour, favour, realising, travelled, centre
**No em dashes:** Use comma, period, or colon
**No asterisks:** No markdown bold/italic in peak, plain prose
**No <em> currently:** Strict rule — only inwardly/in his heart/gleam flashed/counting/philosophical detachment, NOT gossip or elder lecture
**Breaks:** • • • with class scene-break, centered
**Poem box:** .prose-poem preserved when present
**Width:** 1000px max for prose
**Tolkien:** Third-person limited close to protagonist + omniscient stepping back
**Grammar:** How time slips by not In a blink, tucked away not put away, loosened drawstring, Colour rose
**Perfection checks:** Leo + grammar + British sweep
