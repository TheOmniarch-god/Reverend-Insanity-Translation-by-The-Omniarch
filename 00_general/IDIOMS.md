# Idioms — Chapter 9

- 养育之恩: kindness of raising, 晋袁宏后汉纪
- 白手起家: starting with empty hands, 朱子语类
- 小兔崽子: little rabbit brat, 晋张华博物志
- 渐行渐远: drifting ever further away, 欧阳修
- 红日西沉: red sun sinks westward, evening decline
- 眉头拧成了疙瘩: brows knitted into a knot
- 名副其实: worthy of name, in all but name
- 人为财死鸟为食亡: Man dies for wealth as bird dies for food
