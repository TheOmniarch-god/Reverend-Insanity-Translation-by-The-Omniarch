## I. The Core Confusion — Why It Feels Inconsistent

In English we have one word: **immortality**. In Chinese, there are **five overlapping but different layers**, all translated as immortality in English xianxia:

1. **长生** chang sheng — long life
2. **长生不老** chang sheng bu lao — long life without aging
3. **不朽** bu xiu — imperishable, immortal (spiritual/moral)
4. **永生** yong sheng — eternal life
5. **仙** xian — ascended/transcendent (person in mountain)
6. **寿** shou — lifespan years

In modern colloquial Chinese, 长生 sounds like "living long". In **Daoist/cultivation register** — which RI operates in — 长生 has meant transcendence for 2000 years.

That's the register issue you flagged, and Tieba confirms author knows his Chinese perfectly.

**Tieba proof:** Poem by 武庸 (Wu Yong), Wisdom Path Gu Immortal, 8-turn:
> **永生缥缈非我求，长生无为老愧羞。**
> 界壁消散乱世起，宿命一去竞自由。
> 鹰击长空鲸霸海，不试怎知龙与蚯。
> 凡夫俗子岂识我，非到末路不甘休。

Translation: "Eternal life is ethereal, not what I seek; To do nothing for long life and grow old in shame."
— Even Wu Yong distinguishes: 永生 is ethereal/distant, 长生 is active pursuit; not doing anything for 长生 is shameful.

**Second poem — 无极魔尊 Limitless Demon Venerable:**
> 从来是疯魔道痴
> 俯仰问阴阳乾坤
> 百万年筹谋积蓄
> **待今朝无极永生**
> — Waiting today for limitless eternal life

Unji spent million years planning for 永生.

## II. Daoist Classical Foundation — Where Terms Come From

### 1. 仙 xian — The character you asked about

**Shuowen Jiezi (100 CE):**
- 神: 天神，引出万物者也 — God, heavenly deity, who leads forth all things, innate
- 僊/仙: 长生僊去 — long life and leave, transcend
- 仚: 人在山上，从人从山 — person on mountain, from person + mountain

**Meaning:** 仙 is not Western immortal who cannot die. It's **person who moves to mountain to cultivate Dao**, attains transcendence. That's why Chinese says 老而不死曰仙 — old without dying is called xian.

**Gu Zhen Ren redefinition in novel:** 蛊 is 天地真精 (true essence of heaven and earth). So 蛊仙 = Gu + mountain person = **Ascended Gu Master** who has immortal aperture, dao marks, immortal essence. But still has finite lifespan, can be killed.

**Daoist classification (Baopuzi):**
- 天仙: 上士举形升虚 — upper scholar lifts form to void
- 地仙: 中士游于名山 — middle scholar wanders famous mountains
- 尸解仙: 下士先死后蜕 — lower scholar dies first then sheds

**True person vs immortal:** 真人不知悦生，不知恶死 — True person doesn't delight in life, doesn't hate death, seeks spiritual eternity. 仙人 seeks not old not dead. Fang Yuan is closer to 真人 in pursuit.

**This is why English Gu Immortal is mistranslation but kept:** 18,895 mentions in epub. True meaning = **Gu Celestial / Gu Ascended / Transcendent**. We keep Gu Immortal for familiarity but understand nuance.

### 2. 长生 chang sheng — Long life

**Daodejing Chapter 59:**
> 是谓深根固柢，长生久视之道。
> This is called deep root firm base, the way of long life and lasting vision.

**Huangdi Neijing:**
> 余闻上古有真人者，提挈天地，把握阴阳，呼吸精气，独立守神，肌肉若一，故能寿敝天地，无有终时，此其道生。
> Ancient true persons could live as long as heaven and earth without end — this is Dao gives life.

**Daoist definition:** 长生 at minimum = 120 years (human natural lifespan 43,200 days). 不老 = still looks 14-15 years old. So 长生不老 = 120+ years but looks teenage.

**But in xianxia genre:** 长生 is defined as **Immortality — The Ultimate Goal**. 长生系统 (Longevity System) subgenre explicitly described as pursuit of immortality.

**Baopuzi 318 CE:** Uses 长生 as state of extended life that is prerequisite and expression of becoming xian.

**Therefore in RI register:** 长生 = **immortality** (becoming xian, Gu Immortal, transcending mortal 100 years, but still killable, still needs Lifespan Gu)

### 3. 永生 yong sheng — Eternal life

**Modern definition (Wikipedia, Baidu Baike):** 永远生存，或永远存在的生命，也就是不死的意思 — forever surviving, never dying.

**Distinction in Chinese Wikipedia:**
- 长生不老: lifespan long, no aging, but can still be killed (doesn't mean won't die)
- 长生不死: infinite lifespan but still aging
- 不老不死: no aging no death
- 不朽: immortality (spiritual)
- 永生: forever surviving without dying, but aging

**In RI, author makes his own stricter definition (more demanding than normal Chinese):**

From **Tieba 10-year interview, Gu Zhen Ren himself asked:**
> 问：真人，书中应该是没有永生蛊存在的吧？永生代表无限的寿命和无敌的力量，书中多次强调过：没有无敌的蛊虫，只有无敌的蛊师，如果真的有这样一只永生蛊可以直接给蛊仙这两样，那岂不是违背了？

> 答：**方源的最终目标，就是炼制永生蛊。炼道是基础，但炼制这只蛊虫的代价远超他的想象。这和最终的大结局有关。**

And:

> 问：永生的基础是拥有至尊仙胎蛊嘛？
> 答：永生蛊的炼制，是另外一件事情。

**Fang Yuan's own words (Ch 464, 599, 833):**
> 只有永生，只有永生才是应该追求的目标！如果不能永生，九转蛊仙和阴沟里的粪堆有什么区别？！
> Only eternal life, only eternal life should be pursued! If cannot live forever, what's difference between Rank 9 Gu Immortal and shit in gutter?!

> Immortal Venerables and Demon Venerables could only have a long life, but his goal was the greater level of eternal life! This was a realm venerables could not reach.

> As to whether eternal life really existed or not, did that matter? Even if he died along the way, would there be regret? (Ch 833)

**Reddit r/ReverendInsanity synthesis:**
- Eternal life = infinite lifespan + invincible power
- Even venerables only have longevity (long life) not eternal life
- 10,000-year turtle has longevity but not eternal life
- 永生远大于长生 — eternal life far greater than long life

**Gu Zhen Ren interview on energy conservation:**
> 问：蛊界本身也没有能量守恒的迹象……永生这个概念也是，一点都不符合能量守恒和现实逻辑。
> 答：蛊界是在成长的，是在不断壮大的，能量也是守恒的。蛊界成长的营养来自于哪里，很容易猜得到吧？比如像方源这样的穿越者，比如混沌。

So author's world does have conservation, but grows by absorbing outsiders and chaos. Eternal life would require breaking even that.

**Final author hint on ending:** Hope Gu is related to final refinement of Eternal Life Gu and final world vs Fang Yuan boss.

## III. 寿 shou — Lifespan — The Currency

**Baidu Baike 寿蛊 entry (verified):**
> 寿蛊是小说《蛊真人》中可直接增添寿命的蛊虫，外形如参须或老树根，触感粗糙沧桑。其静止时难以察觉，飞行速度超过光速，需借助规矩二蛊、八十八角真阳楼等特殊手段捕捉。

- **Type:** 天道蛊虫, only mortal level (Rank 1-5), cannot be refined, only naturally born where life gathers
- **Ranks:** 2转 十年寿蛊 (10-90y), 3转 百年寿蛊 (100y), 4转 千年寿蛊 (1000y)
- **Best method:** No side effects. Other methods have huge flaws:
  - 僵尸蛊: need suck blood
  - 还童蛊: fixed as child
  - 阴阳延寿法: one gains life other loses
  - 宙道延寿法: engraves dao marks, mutually exclusive with 寿蛊
  - 夺舍: needs 6转夺舍仙蛊 + 3000 mortal Gu
- **Hard currency:** 寿蛊是蛊仙交易中，最强劲的硬通货 — strongest hard currency among Gu Immortals
- **Heaven's Will control:** 天道为了限制尊者长存，会刻意降低寿蛊产量 — Heavenly Dao deliberately lowers Lifespan Gu production to limit venerables

**Epub sample:** 寿蛊只是凡蛊。/ 要想延寿，还是寿蛊第一。

**Tieba confusion solved:** Why do many Gu Masters live hundreds of years if normal lifespan 100 and Lifespan Gu hard to find?
Answer from Tieba: 
- 宿命蛊定好的 — Fate Gu decides your lifespan allocation, will arrange Lifespan Gu for you via various means
- Other side methods exist, just not optimal
- Immortal aperture top experience can produce Lifespan Gu
- 天意 has 人劫 to block you from getting others' Lifespan Gu

**English placeholder:** **Lifespan Gu** (NOT Longevity Gu, to avoid confusion with 长生)

## IV. Full Hierarchy — Author's True Differentiation (from epub + Tieba)

**Mortal 100 years → Lifespan Gu (寿蛊) adds years → Long life/Immortality (长生) long but finite, killable, Gu Immortal → Eternal life (永生) infinite + invincible**

**Author's own formula from novel (epub found):**
> 寻得长生的人，想要更进一步，获得永生。
> Those who have found long life/immortality want to go further and obtain eternal life.

**Proof that even venerables don't have eternal life:**
- Ch 599: 仙尊魔尊只能长生
- Tieba: 万古愁活过万年只有四个人: 元始、星宿、元莲、万古愁。异人荒兽寿命更长，但都不是永生

**Unji Demon Venerable case:**
> 无极魔尊在疯魔窟第九层重生后，借助气绝魔仙达成不完美永生。最终因尝试吞吸混沌之气失败而陨落。
> Achieved imperfect eternal life via Qi Jue Mo Xian, failed swallowing chaos, died.

So even imperfect eternal life fails.

**Philosophical twist from Chinese community:** 
- 永生就是永死，死亡才是永恒 — Eternal life is eternal death, death is eternity
- In Chinese myths, eternal life doesn't exist; longevity exists (Sun Wukong ate peaches gives 1000y but still dies). Myths punish seeking eternal life because it doesn't exist.
- Eternal life would mean immutability and stagnation, what Dao (which represents change) is antithesis.

Gu Zhen Ren himself in Ch 833 says he doesn't care if eternal life exists — he enjoys process.

## V. What Each Should Say — Implementation Placeholders

### 寿蛊 → Lifespan Gu
- **Pinyin:** shou gu
- **Literal:** lifespan Gu
- **True:** Adds years, no side effects, mortal, hard currency, Heaven's Will controlled
- **Use:** Always Lifespan Gu, never Longevity Gu
- **Example:** 八十年寿蛊 → eighty-year Lifespan Gu

### 长生 → Immortality (in RI xianxia register) / Longevity (literal)
- **Pinyin:** chang sheng
- **Literal:** long life
- **Daoist:** Long life lasting vision, transcending mortality, 120+ years looks 14
- **Xianxia:** Immortality Ultimate Goal
- **RI:** Immortality — becoming Gu Immortal, long but finite, killable, needs Lifespan Gu. 10k turtle has this, venerables have this, not eternal life.
- **Use:** 
  - In early chapters (Ch2, Ch5): **immortality** to match genre register (author knows Chinese)
  - When emphasizing finite vs infinite: can gloss as **longevity** to contrast with eternal life, but primary = immortality
- **Places:**
  - 心中唯有长生大道 → Great Dao of Immortality / Eternal Life
  - 这就是长生的希望 → hope of immortality
  - 长生即逆天 → Long life is defying heaven
  - 哪一个能得长生？ → Which can obtain long life?
- **Recommendation:** Use **immortality** for Ch2/Ch5, as you locked. It matches xianxia register.

### 长生大道 → Great Dao of Immortality / Great Dao of Eternal Life
- **Pinyin:** chang sheng da dao
- **Literal:** Great Dao of Long Life
- **True:** Ultimate path of transcending mortality, becoming xian, transcendent dao: 但凡长生大道，都是超脱之道 — Any Great Dao of Long Life is transcendent dao
- **Epub:** 2 occurrences
- **Use:** 
  - Strict faithful: Great Dao of Immortality (matches 长生 register)
  - Ultimate gloss: Great Dao of Eternal Life (links to Fang Yuan's final goal 永生, Vol 6 魔尊永生)
- **Decision:** Keep **Great Dao of Eternal Life** for Fang Yuan's vow in Ch2 P32, note Chinese is 长生大道 = immortality in register. Both valid, but Eternal Life links forward.

### 永生 → Eternal Life
- **Pinyin:** yong sheng
- **Literal:** eternal life
- **RI true:** Infinite lifespan + invincible power, nothing can threaten, realm venerables cannot reach, greater than longevity
- **Epub:** 26 occurrences
- **Use:** Always eternal life, never immortality (to differentiate)
- **Places:**
  - 方源矢志永生 → Fang Yuan vows eternal life
  - 寻得长生的人，想要更进一步，获得永生 → Those who found immortality want further eternal life
  - 永生缥缈非我求 → Eternal life is ethereal, not what I seek (Wu Yong)
  - 待今朝无极永生 → Awaiting today limitless eternal life
- **Vol title:** 魔尊永生 → Demon Venerable, Eternal Life

### 永生之蛊 → Eternal Life Gu
- **Pinyin:** yong sheng zhi gu
- **Literal:** Eternal Life Gu
- **True:** Legendary Rank 10 consumable Immortal Gu, rumor from Legends of Ren Zu, requires person as catalyst, Mao Min tried but failed due to Tai Ri Yang Mang escape
- **Epub:** 5 occurrences, always 永生之蛊, never 永生蛊
- **Use:** Eternal Life Gu
- **Example:** 难道是传说中的永生之蛊？！ → Could it be the legendary Eternal Life Gu?!

### 蛊仙 → Gu Immortal (keep term, true meaning Ascended)
- **Pinyin:** gu xian
- **True:** Rank 6+, immortal aperture (blessed land/grotto-heaven), dao marks, immortal essence, finite lifespan, needs Lifespan Gu, not truly immortal in lifespan
- **Epub:** 18,895 mentions
- **Use:** Gu Immortal (familiarity), but understand = Gu Celestial / Ascended
- **Example:** 七转蛊仙 → Rank 7 Gu Immortal

### 仙蛊 → Immortal Gu
- **Pinyin:** xian gu
- **True:** Rank 6+ unique, one per world, hard to destroy, revives elsewhere
- **Epub:** 13,169
- **Use:** Immortal Gu

### 仙人 → Immortal / Celestial / True Person (contextual)
- **Pinyin:** xian ren
- **True:** Ascended person
- **Epub:** 436
- **Use:** Immortal (when Gu Immortal synonym), but note true = celestial

### 长生天 → Longevity Heaven
- **Pinyin:** chang sheng tian
- **True:** Faction, Giant Sun's grotto-heaven
- **Use:** Longevity Heaven (not Eternal Life Heaven, Chinese is 长生)

### 不朽 → Imperishable / Immortal (moral)
- **Pinyin:** bu xiu
- **True:** Three immortals: 立德、立言、立功 — moral immortality, not lifespan
- **Use:** Imperishable, not used for lifespan in RI
