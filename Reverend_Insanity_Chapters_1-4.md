## Chapter 1: Though the Flesh Perish, the Demonic Heart Knows No Regret

“Fang Yuan! Hand over the Spring Autumn Cicada without struggle, and I shall grant you a quick death!”

“Old Demon Fang, harbour no delusions of defiance! Today, the revered sects of the Righteous Path have united for one purpose: to trample your demonic lair into dust. We have long since laid an inescapable dragnet here. This time, you shall be beheaded!”

“Fang Yuan, you damned devil! To refine that cursed Spring Autumn Cicada, you slaughtered tens of millions of innocent lives! You have committed towering atrocities, unpardonable, unredeemable, innumerable, beyond all count!”

“Monster! Three hundred years ago, you violated me and robbed me of my chastity. You killed my entire family and slaughtered all my kin. From that moment on, I’ve wanted nothing more than to devour your flesh and drink your blood! Today, I’ll make you wish you were dead!”

……

Fang Yuan stood cloaked in a tattered emerald robe, his hair wild and unkempt, his body bathed in blood, as his eyes swept over the surrounding host. The mountain wind whipped his blood-soaked garments, fluttering and snapping like a war banner. Fresh blood gushed out from the hundreds of wounds across his body. In the mere moment he had stood there, a large pool of blood had already gathered at his feet.

Besieged on every flank by bitter foes, every path to life had long been severed. The trap had snapped shut; on this day, death was absolute.

Fang Yuan perceived his doom with crystalline clarity. Yet even as the shadow of death loomed before him, he remained expressionless, his demeanor unmoved. His eyes were abyssal, like an ancient well, unfathomably deep, without shore and without bottom.

The righteous host besieging him comprised distinguished sect leaders and young heroes renowned far and wide. Yet as they surrounded Fang Yuan, their composure fractured: some bared their teeth in savage roars, some sneered with brittle arrogance, some narrowed their eyes with venomous suspicion, while others clutched bleeding wounds, looking upon him with lingering dread.

None of them dared make a move; every soul among them trembled before the final, dying wrath of Old Demon Fang.

In this suffocating deadlock, six hours slipped into eternity. The sun sank beneath the western ridge; its dying embers set the clouds ablaze, bathing the mountain crest in a fierce, incandescent conflagration of twilight.

Fang Yuan, who had stood as motionless as a statue, slowly turned around. That solitary motion sent a convulsion through the host; the multitude recoiled in unison, retreating a full pace in panic.

At this moment, the grey-white mountain rocks beneath Fang Yuan’s feet had long been dyed dark red with fresh blood. His face, pale from excessive blood loss, was reflected by the sunset glow, suddenly taking on an alluring, delicate radiance.

Gazing upon the green mountains and the setting sun, Fang Yuan let slip a soft, low chuckle:

*“Green mountains beneath the setting sun, autumn moon and spring breeze.*  
*Truly, in the morning one’s hair is like black silk, by dusk it turns to snow;*  
*Right and wrong, success and failure, all vanish in the turning of a head.”*

As the verse left his lips, memories of his past life on Earth surged unbidden before his eyes.

He had originally been a Chinese student on Earth, cast into this foreign world through a serendipitous twist of fortune. Three hundred years of drifting, tossed like flotsam upon tempestuous tides; two hundred years of unbridled dominion, carving his will across the world, more than five centuries, yet in the retrospect of memory, gone in the span of an eyeblink.

Countless memories buried deep within the bedrock of his soul awakened, playing across his mind with breathtaking, indelible clarity.

“In the end, I failed after all,” Fang Yuan sighed in his heart. A trace of wistfulness stirred, yet not the faintest whisper of regret.

This outcome was nothing he had not foreseen; the moment he made his choice centuries ago, he was already mentally prepared.

For what was the Demonic Path? It was to cultivate no virtuous karma, committing murder and arson; spurned by heaven and reviled by earth, making enemies with the whole world, and still running wild, following the whim of one’s own heart to the very end.

“If this newly refined Spring Autumn Cicada works, I will still be a demon in my next life!” Thinking like this, Fang Yuan could not help laughing out loud.

“Old Demon, what are you laughing at?!”

“Everyone be careful, the demon is at death’s door and wants to counterattack!”

“Quickly hand over the Spring Autumn Cicada!!”

The righteous host surged forward, greed and panic driving them into frenzy. And in that very breath, 

With a thunderous blast, Fang Yuan brazenly self-detonated.

……

Spring rain fell continuously, silently nourishing Qing Mao Mountain. Night was already deep, and threads of cool wind blew through the fine rain.

Qing Mao Mountain was not dark, however; from the mountainside down to the foothills, faint, shimmering lights glowed, as if draping a brilliant ribbon of radiance across the slope. These lights came from the stilt houses, not ten thousand households, but several thousand nonetheless. This was Gu Yue Village, nestled upon Qing Mao Mountain, giving the vast, tranquil mountain range a distinct sense of human habitation.

At the very heart of Gu Yue Village stood a grand and glorious pavilion. Right now a grand sacrificial ceremony was being held, so it was brightly lit and brilliantly glorious.

“Ancestors bless us! Hope that in this Aperture Opening Ceremony, many youths with excellent aptitude can emerge to bring fresh blood and hope to our clan!”

The Gu Yue Clan Leader appeared middle-aged, his temples touched with frost. Wearing plain white, solemn sacrificial robes, he knelt upon the brownish-yellow floorboards, his upper body erect, hands pressed together, closing both eyes to pray sincerely.

He faced a tall, black-lacquered altar table with three tiers, enshrining the spirit tablets of the ancestors. On both sides of the tablets stood red-copper incense burners, thin plumes of fragrant smoke coiling upward.

Behind him, a dozen or so figures knelt in matching white sacrificial robes. These were the clan elders and decision-makers, each wielding power over various affairs of the clan.

After concluding his prayers, the Clan Leader was the first to bend down, palms flat against the wooden floorboards, kowtowing. His forehead met the brown floor with soft, rhythmic thuds. The elders behind him, their expressions solemn, followed suit in silence. For a time, the ancestral hall was filled only with the gentle cadence of foreheads knocking against the floor.

When the ceremony concluded, everyone slowly rose from the floor and quietly filed out of the solemn hall. In the corridor, the elders breathed a quiet sigh of relief, the tense atmosphere easing noticeably. Murmurs of conversation soon began to rise:

“Time truly flies. In the blink of an eye, another full year has gone by.”

“The previous Aperture Opening Ceremony feels like it happened just yesterday; the memory is still vivid.”

“Tomorrow is the annual Aperture Opening Ceremony. I wonder what kind of fresh blood our clan will produce this year?”

“Sigh, I only hope a youth with Grade A aptitude emerges. It has been three years since our Gu Yue clan had such a genius.”

“Indeed. Both Bai Village and Xiong Village have seen remarkable talents emerge over recent years. In particular, Bai Ning Bing of the Bai clan, his natural talent is truly terrifying.”

Whoever mentioned the name Bai Ning Bing, a shadow of worry involuntarily crossed the faces of the clan elders.

This youth’s talent was extraordinary. In just two short years, he had already cultivated to Rank 3 Gu Master, standing unrivaled among the younger generation. Even the older generation felt immense pressure from this rising star. Given time, he would undoubtedly become the pillar of Bai Village, at least a powerhouse standing on his own. No one ever doubted this.

“However, among the youths participating in this year’s Aperture Opening Ceremony, it is not as though we have no hope.”

“Quite right. A genius youth has appeared in the Fang lineage. He could speak at three months and walk at four. By age five, he could compose poems and recite verses, exceptionally intelligent and overflowing with talent. It is just a pity his parents died early, and he is currently being raised by his aunt and uncle.”

“Mm, he possesses precocious wisdom and grand ambitions. In recent years, I have heard of the poems he composed, *Bring in the Wine*, *Ode to the Plum Blossom*, and *River Town*. He is truly a genius!”

The Gu Yue Clan Leader was the last to exit the ancestral hall. As he slowly closed the door, the murmurs of the elders reached his ears. He immediately knew that the elders were discussing a youth named Gu Yue Fang Yuan. As the leader of the clan, he naturally paid close attention to outstanding juniors, and Gu Yue Fang Yuan was the most prominent and dazzling among them.

Experience had shown that those gifted with photographic memories or strength rivaling an adult from childhood almost always possessed superior cultivation talent.

“If this child tests with Grade A aptitude and is properly nurtured, he may well be able to contend with Bai Ning Bing. Even if he only has Grade B aptitude, he will certainly become a stalwart pillar and a banner for the Gu Yue clan in the future. Yet with such precocious intellect, Grade B is unlikely, it is overwhelmingly probable that he has Grade A.” With this thought, the corners of the Clan Leader’s lips lifted slightly into a smile.

He cleared his throat with a dry cough and addressed the elders: “Everyone, the hour is late. For the sake of tomorrow’s Aperture Opening Ceremony, tonight please be sure to rest well and conserve your energy.”

Hearing this, the elders paused slightly. In the glances they exchanged, a subtle trace of vigilance was concealed. The Clan Leader’s words were polite and restrained, but everyone understood their deeper meaning.

Every year, in order to recruit talented juniors into their own factions, the elders fought tooth and nail until their faces flushed red and heads bled. It was indeed time to rest and gather strength, so they could contend fiercely tomorrow.

Especially for that Gu Yue Fang Yuan, the probability of him possessing Grade A aptitude was exceptionally high. Moreover, both his parents were deceased, leaving him and his brother as the only surviving orphans of the Fang lineage. If one could bring him into their own faction and groom him well, it could guarantee a century of prosperity for their line!

“However, let me make the ugly words clear first. Compete openly and honorably; underhanded schemes that harm clan unity will not be tolerated. Elders, bear this firmly in mind!” the Clan Leader warned sternly.

“We wouldn’t dare, we wouldn’t dare.”

“We shall definitely keep it firmly in mind.”

“Then we shall take our leave. Lord Clan Leader, please do not trouble yourself to see us off.”

Harbouring their own calculations, the elders dispersed one by one.

Before long, the long corridor grew quiet and desolate. Spring rain and slanted wind blew through the window; the Clan Leader stepped lightly toward the window frame. Instantly, a mouthful of fresh, moist mountain air filled his chest, refreshing to the soul.

This was the third floor of the attic; the Clan Leader looked out, and more than half of Gu Yue Village could be seen in a single glance. Though it was late at night, lights still burned in most homes in the village, quite unlike usual. Tomorrow was the Aperture Opening Ceremony, touching upon everyone’s personal interests. An excited and nervous atmosphere covered the clansmen’s hearts, naturally making many sleep restlessly.

“This is the future hope of our clan,” with dots of light reflected in his eyes, the Clan Leader sighed softly.

……

At this very moment, another pair of clear eyes quietly gazed upon those twinkling lights in the deep of the night, brimming with complex emotions.

“Gu Yue Village… this is five hundred years ago?! The Spring Autumn Cicada really had effect…” Fang Yuan’s gaze was dark and profound as he stood beside the window, letting the wind and rain beat against his body.

The effect of the Spring Autumn Cicada was to reverse time. Ranked seventh among the Ten Mystical Gu, it was naturally no ordinary existence.

In short: rebirth.

“Reborn using the Spring Autumn Cicada… back to five hundred years ago!” Fang Yuan reached out his hand, staring intently at his young, tender, and somewhat pale palm. Then he slowly clenched it into a fist, feeling the vivid reality with all his strength.

He heard the gentle pitter-patter of drizzle tapping against the window lattice. He closed his eyes slowly, opening them only after a long moment, letting out a wistful sigh: “Five hundred years of experience… it truly feels like a dream.”

Yet he knew with absolute certainty: this was no dream.

## Chapter 2: Reversing Time, Five Hundred Years of Enlightenment

In legends, there exists a River of Time in this world, supporting the flow of all existence. And by utilizing the power of the Spring Autumn Cicada, one can travel upstream against the current, returning to the past.

Regarding this rumor, the world was divided in opinion. Many did not believe it, while some half-believed and half-doubted. Almost no one was truly certain.

Because every time the Spring Autumn Cicada was used, one had to pay with their life, sacrificing their entire body and all of their cultivation base as the driving power. This price was truly too steep, and what made it even harder to accept was that even after paying with one’s life, the outcome remained unknown.

Even if someone obtained the Spring Autumn Cicada, they would never dare use it casually on a whim. What if the rumor was false, nothing more than a hoax?

If Fang Yuan had not been driven to a dead end, he would not have used it so readily.

However, now, Fang Yuan believed completely and thoroughly. Because the ironclad truth lay before his eyes, beyond all dispute. He had indeed been reborn!

“It is only a pity for such a good Gu. Back then, it took monumental effort, the slaughter of hundreds of thousands of people, stirring the wrath of heaven and the resentment of men, enduring endless tribulations before I finally refined it…” Fang Yuan sighed inwardly. Though he had been reborn, the Spring Autumn Cicada had not traveled back with him.

Humans are the spirit of all living things; Gu are the essence of heaven and earth.

Gu were bizarre and varied, countless in number. Some Gu would completely dissipate after being used once or twice. Other Gu, as long as they were not used excessively, could be used repeatedly. Perhaps the Spring Autumn Cicada was simply that kind of expendable Gu that could only be used once.

“However, even if it is gone, I can simply refine another one. If I could refine it in my past life, could I not in this life?” Pity aside, a surge of ambitious pride could not help rising in Fang Yuan’s heart.

To be able to be reborn, the loss of the Spring Autumn Cicada was entirely acceptable.

Moreover, he still carried a priceless treasure; he was far from empty-handed. This treasure was his five hundred years of memories and experience.

Within his memory lay countless secret troves, currently unopened by anyone. There lay major historical events one by one, allowing him to easily grasp the pulse of history. There lay countless figures: some were hidden experts who cultivate in deep seclusion, some were extraordinary prodigies, and some were not even born yet. There also lay the grueling experience of bitter cultivation over these five hundred years, and a rich reservoir of battle experience.

With these, he undoubtedly held the overall situation and the initiative. As long as he played his cards well, roaming the mortal realm and recreating his former glory as a demonic overlord and ruthless hegemon would be no problem at all, and he could even take a step further, charging towards an even higher realm!

“Then how should I proceed…?” Fang Yuan was extremely rational. He quickly gathered his emotions and turned toward the night rain outside the window, falling into deep thought.

Thinking like this, he felt a thousand loose ends and ten thousand tangled threads. After pondering for a moment, his brow furrowed deeper and deeper.

A span of five hundred years was truly rather long. Not to mention those memories that were already blurred and could no longer be recalled, even among the secret treasure grounds and immortal opportunities he did remember, although numerous, most were either separated by hundreds of thousands of miles, or required specific times to open.

“The most crucial factor is still cultivation base. Right now, my primeval sea is not yet open; I have not even stepped onto the cultivation path of a Gu Master. I am fundamentally a mortal! I must cultivate as quickly as possible and raise my cultivation base, moving ahead of history to seize the initiative as much as possible and grab enough benefits.”

Moreover, without sufficient cultivation, he could not make full use of many hidden troves even if he obtained them. Instead, they would become dangerous liabilities, merely possessing such treasures would make him a target.

The first difficult problem placed before Fang Yuan was his cultivation base. He had to raise his cultivation base as quickly as possible; if he was as slow as in his past life, even the daylilies would have gone cold.

“To raise my cultivation base quickly, I must rely on the resources of the clan. In my current situation, I fundamentally lack the capability to traverse the peril-laden mountain ranges; even an ordinary wild boar could take my life. Only if I reach the cultivation base of a Rank 3 Gu Master will I have the baseline ability to protect myself and traverse the mountains and rivers of this world.”

Through the eyes of a demonic overlord tempered by five hundred years, this Qing Mao Mountain was truly too small, and Gu Yue Village was more like a cage. However, while a cage imprisons freedom, a sturdy cell often represents a kind of safety.

“Hmph. For now, I’ll make my moves within this cage. As long as I advance to Rank 3 Gu Master, I will leave this impoverished mountain backwater. Fortunately, tomorrow is the Aperture Opening Ceremony, and shortly after, I can officially begin cultivating as a Gu Master.”

At the thought of the Aperture Opening Ceremony, memories long buried in dust surfaced within Fang Yuan’s heart.

“Aptitude, is it…?” Looking outside the window, he could not help letting out three cold chuckles.

Just at this moment, the room door was gently pushed open, and a youth walked in.

“Brother, why are you standing by the window letting the rain fall on you?”

The youth was thin in build, slightly shorter than Fang Yuan, his face bearing an uncanny resemblance to his own. Fang Yuan turned his head to look at this youth, a trace of complexity flashing across his face.

“So it is you, my twin brother.” He raised his brows slightly, his expression returning to his habitual coldness.

Fang Zheng lowered his head, looking down at his toes, his trademark habit. “Seeing that brother’s window wasn’t closed, I wanted to slip in quietly and shut it. Tomorrow is the Aperture Opening Ceremony. Brother, if Uncle and Aunt find out you’re still up this late, I’m afraid they’ll worry.”

He was not surprised by Fang Yuan’s coldness, because from childhood to now, his brother had always been like this. Sometimes he would think that perhaps geniuses were just different from ordinary mortals. Although he shared a nearly identical face with his brother, he felt as ordinary as an ant.

Born from the same womb at the same time, why was heaven so unfair? It had given his brother diamond-like talent, while he was ordinary like a roadside stone.

Everyone around him, whenever mentioning him, would always say: “This is Fang Yuan’s brother.” His uncle and aunt also often lectured him: “You must learn from your brother.”

Sometimes, even looking at his own face in the mirror filled him with a certain disgust!

These thoughts had existed for many years, accumulating deep inside his heart day by day. They weighed on his chest like a boulder. Over the years, Fang Zheng’s head had hung lower and lower, and he had grown increasingly silent.

“Worry…?” Thinking of his uncle and aunt, Fang Yuan scoffed inwardly.

He remembered very clearly that the parents of this body had both fallen during a clan mission. When he was merely three years old, he and his brother had become orphans. Under the pretext of raising them, his uncle and aunt had openly and legally usurped their parents’ inheritance, and treated the two brothers harshly.

Originally, as a transmigrator, he had planned to bide his time in obscurity. But the hardship of survival had forced Fang Yuan to choose displaying talent different from normal people.

The so-called “genius” was actually nothing more than the rationality of a mature soul, paired with a few Tang poems and Song verses from Earth that echoed through generations.

Just testing his hands like this, he was amazed at as a heavenly being, receiving widespread attention. Under external pressure, it also made the young Fang Yuan have to choose a cold expression to disguise and protect himself, reducing the chance of exposing his secrets. Over time, coldness instead became his habitual expression.

Just like this, his uncle and aunt could no longer treat him and his brother harshly. As his age grew and his future looked brighter, his treatment also increased along with it.

However, this was not love, but an investment.

The laughable thing was that this brother of his had never seen through this truth. Not only was he blinded by their uncle and aunt, but he had even harboured a buried resentment toward Fang Yuan. Don’t look at him being so well-behaved and honest now; in Fang Yuan’s memories, once he tested with Grade A aptitude and was heavily nurtured by the clan, all of his hidden hatred and jealousy had been released, and he had spared no effort in targeting, making things difficult for, and suppressing his own elder brother.

And as for his own aptitude…?

Heh. At best, merely a Grade C.

Fate always loved to play jokes.

Born as twins, the elder brother’s aptitude was only Grade C, yet monopolized the name of genius for over a decade. The younger brother had been unknown, yet possessed Grade A natural talent. The result of the Aperture Opening Ceremony had left the entire clan stunned. It also completely reversed the two brothers’ situation and treatment. The younger brother was like a crouching dragon ascending the heavens; the elder brother was like a fledgling phoenix crashing to the earth.

After that, came repeated difficulties from his brother, cold eyes from his uncle and aunt, and contempt from the clansmen.

Did he hate them?

In his past life, Fang Yuan had hated. He hated his own insufficient aptitude, hated the clan’s heartlessness, and hated fate’s unfairness. But now, as he reexamined that part of his life with five hundred years of experience behind him, he felt not a ripple of hatred.

What was there to resent?

Thinking from their perspective, he could also understand his brother, his uncle and aunt, as well as those righteous powerhouses who besieged him five hundred years later.

The strong devour the weak; survival of the fittest, that was the very nature of this world.

Moreover, everyone has their own ambitions, all fighting for that sliver of heavenly opportunity. Between each other suppressing and killing, what was there not to understand?

Five hundred years of experience had long made him see through all of this; in his heart, there remained only the Great Dao of Eternal Life. If someone stood in the way of this pursuit, no matter who, it was nothing more than: either you die or I live.

The wild ambition in his heart was too immense. Stepping onto this path, one was destined to make enemies of the entire world, destined to walk alone, and destined to face relentless tribulations.

This was the enlightenment distilled from five hundred years of life.

“Vengeance is not my intention, and the demonic path also never has the word compromise.” Thinking to this point, Fang Yuan could not help laughing. Turning his head toward this brother of his, taking a faint glance, he said: “You can go.”

Fang Zheng felt a sudden tremor in his heart, feeling that his brother’s gaze was as sharp as an icy blade, seeming to pierce into the deepest part of his heart. Under that gaze, he felt as if he were standing naked in the snow, every secret laid bare.

“Then… see you tomorrow, brother.” Daring not speak more right now, Fang Zheng slowly closed the room door, murmuring compliance as he retreated.

## Chapter 3: Kindly Piss Off

Clack, clack-clack. Clack, clack-clack. The night watchman was making his rounds, striking his wooden clapper in a steady rhythm. As the hollow strikes drifted into the stilt house, Fang Yuan opened his dry, heavy eyelids, murmuring inwardly: “It is the fifth watch.”

Yesternight, he had lain in bed pondering long into the night, making plans and arrangements. By his reckoning, he had slept for barely a little over two hours. This body had not yet begun cultivation and had little stamina; he still felt tired, and sleep kept tugging at his body and mind.

Yet five hundred years of trials had long tempered Fang Yuan’s willpower into profound, unyielding steel. A faint trace of lethargy meant nothing to him. Without hesitation, he tossed aside the thin silk quilt and rose to his feet with crisp precision.

Pushing open the wooden window, he saw that the spring rain had ceased. Fresh, moist air carrying the scents of earth, trees, and wildflowers rushed into his face. Fang Yuan immediately felt his mind clear, his drowsiness swept away completely.

The sun had not yet crested the horizon; the sky was a deep, brooding azure, caught between shadow and dawn. Looking out across the expanse, stilt dwellings constructed of green bamboo and timber blended seamlessly against the surrounding mountains, painting a tranquil panorama of verdant emerald.

These stilt houses were built at least two stories high, an architectural hallmark of the mountain folk. Because the rugged terrain was steep and uneven, the ground level consisted of massive timber stilts, while the upper floor served as living quarters. Fang Yuan and his younger brother, Fang Zheng, lived on this second floor.

“Young Master Fang Yuan, you are awake. Your servant will come up right away to attend your morning wash.” Just then, a girl’s voice came from downstairs.

Fang Yuan glanced down to see his personal maidservant, Shen Cui. Her looks were merely average, but she dressed with care, wearing a green tunic with long sleeves and matching trousers, embroidered shoes on her feet, and a single pearl hairpin nestled in her dark hair, radiating youthful vitality from head to toe.

She cast a delighted glance up at Fang Yuan, then hurried up the wooden stairs with a basin of water, her brisk footsteps pattering against the planks.

The water had been prepared to a gentle warmth for washing his face. To rinse his mouth, a willow twig dipped in fine snow-salt was used to clean and whiten the teeth.

Shen Cui attended him with gentle care, a bright smile on her face and coquettish warmth in her eyes. As she helped Fang Yuan dress and fasten his clasps, she brushed her plump bosom against his arm and back from time to time.

Fang Yuan remained expressionless, his heart like still water.

This maidservant was not only his aunt and uncle’s informant, but she was also vain and fickle. In his past life, he had once been blinded by her charms; yet after the Aperture Opening Ceremony, when his status plummeted overnight, she instantly turned against him, giving him no shortage of cold disdain.

When Fang Zheng came in, he saw Shen Cui smoothing the creases in Fang Yuan’s clothes over his chest, and a glint of envy and jealousy involuntarily flickered in his eyes.

Living with his elder brother all these years and benefiting from Fang Yuan’s care, he too had a servant to attend him. Yet it was not a young maid like Shen Cui, but a fat, bloated old woman.

“If Shen Cui could attend to me like this one day, how would that feel?” Fang Zheng thought about it somewhat, yet dared not think about it fully.

That their aunt and uncle favored Fang Yuan was common knowledge throughout the household. Originally, he had no servant to attend him; it was actually Fang Yuan who took the initiative to demand one for him.

Although Fang Zheng was the master and Shen Cui the servant, he ordinarily did not dare look down on her. Her mother was Granny Shen, who attended their aunt and also managed the entire household. Deeply trusted by their aunt, she wielded considerable authority.

“Enough. There’s no need to fuss anymore.” Fang Yuan impatiently brushed aside Shen Cui’s soft little hands. His clothes had long been smooth; she was doing more to entice him than to straighten them.

To her, Fang Yuan had a bright future and was very likely to possess Grade A aptitude. If she could become his concubine, she could rise from servant to mistress, a single leap to the heavens.

In his previous life, Fang Yuan had been deceived, even falling in love with this maidservant. Now, reborn, he saw through her completely, his heart as cold as frost.

“You can go.” Without so much as looking at Shen Cui, Fang Yuan adjusted his cuffs.

Shen Cui pouted slightly, feeling puzzled and a little hurt by his indifference to her advances today. She wanted to say something coquettish, but an inexplicable air about Fang Yuan, barely perceptible, intimidated her. She opened her mouth several times, but in the end merely said, “Yes,” and obediently left.

“Are you ready?” Fang Yuan looked toward Fang Zheng.

His younger brother stood blankly at the doorway. Lowering his head to look at his toes, he gave a quiet murmur of assent. He had actually woken during the fourth watch. Too nervous to sleep, he had slipped out of bed and prepared himself early. There were dark circles under both his eyes.

Fang Yuan nodded. In his previous life, he had not understood what was on his younger brother’s mind. But in this life, how could he fail to understand? There was no point in exposing it now, however. He simply said, “Then let’s go.”

The two brothers left their residence. Along the way, they encountered quite a few youths their own age, in twos and threes, evidently heading to the same destination.

“Look, those are the two Fang brothers.” Cautious whispers reached their ears.

“The one walking in front is Fang Yuan, the Fang Yuan who writes poetry,” someone emphasized.

“So that’s him. Expressionless, acting as though no one else exists, he really is as arrogant as the rumors say.” The speaker’s sour tone carried both envy and jealousy.

“Hmph. If you could do what he does, you could act that arrogant too!” someone answered with a cold snort, concealing a note of discontent.

Fang Zheng listened expressionlessly. He had long grown accustomed to such talk. Head lowered, he followed silently behind his elder brother. By now, the horizon glowed with the light of dawn, and Fang Yuan’s shadow fell across his face.

The morning sun was gradually rising, yet Fang Zheng suddenly felt that he was walking into darkness. This darkness came from his elder brother. Perhaps, for his entire life, he would never escape the enormous shadow his brother cast over him.

Tightness gripped his chest again and again, until even breathing became difficult. This damned feeling brought one word to mind: *suffocation*!

“Hmph. Such talk. Truly, the tree that rises above the forest bears the brunt of the wind.” Listening to the comments around him, Fang Yuan sneered inwardly.

No wonder that, after his aptitude had been tested as Grade C, he had found himself surrounded by enemies, subjected to harsh treatment, cold disdain, and neglect for a long time.

He also caught every sound of his younger brother’s increasingly labored breathing behind him. What he had failed to notice in his previous life, he now perceived down to the smallest detail. This keen insight came entirely from five hundred years of life experience.

He suddenly thought of his uncle and aunt. They certainly had their methods. They had assigned Shen Cui to monitor him at close quarters, while giving his younger brother an old woman. There were other differences in their treatment, too, in the details of daily life. All of this was deliberate, intended to stir his younger brother’s sense of injustice and sow discord in the brotherly bond between them.

People worry not about having little, but about having unequal shares.

In his previous life, he had been too inexperienced, and his younger brother too foolish and naive. Their uncle and aunt had succeeded in sowing that discord.

Now that he had been reborn, the Aperture Opening Ceremony was already at hand. The situation seemed too deeply entrenched to reverse, but with Fang Yuan’s methods and wisdom as a demonic overlord, it could still be changed.

This younger brother of his could certainly be subdued and brought to heel. Shen Cui, a mere slip of a maid, could all the more easily be taken as one of his concubines ahead of time. As for his uncle and aunt, the clan leader, and the clan elders, he had at least several hundred ways to put them in their place.

“But I have no wish to do any of that…” Fang Yuan sighed inwardly, untroubled.

So what if he was his own younger brother? Without familial affection to speak of, he was merely an outsider. Discard him, and so be it.

Even if Shen Cui were even more beautiful, so what? Devoid of love and loyalty, she is nothing more than flesh. Take her as one of my concubines? She’s not worthy.

So what if they were his uncle and aunt, the clan leader, or the clan elders? They were all merely passing through his life. Why exhaust his ingenuity and waste his energy putting these passersby in their place?

He chuckled.

As long as they do not block my path, they can piss off. I wouldn’t even deign to step on them.

## Chapter 4: Gu Yue Fang Yuan!

The morning sun rose, the clouds aglow with brilliant light. The mountain mist was thin, and sunlight pierced it easily like a sharp sword.

More than a hundred fifteen-year-old youths were now gathered before the Clan Leader’s Pavilion.

The Clan Leader’s Pavilion stood at the very heart of the village, five stories high, with projected eaves and upturned corners. It was heavily guarded. A square lay before it, and inside were enshrined the spirit tablets of the Gu Yue ancestors. Each successive clan leader also lived there. Whenever an important ceremony or a major unforeseen event arose, the clan elders would be convened there to deliberate. It was the center of power for the entire village.

“Very good. You’ve all arrived on time. Today is the Aperture Opening Ceremony, a major turning point in your lives. Enough preliminaries. Follow me.” Leading them was the Academy Elder. His hair and beard were white, but he remained full of vigor as he led the youths into the Clan Leader’s Pavilion.

However, they did not go upstairs. Instead, they passed through an entrance in the ground-floor hall and headed down. They followed the stone steps down into an underground cavern.

The youths exclaimed in wonder. The underground cavern was magnificent. Its stalactites radiated seven colours, red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, and violet, illuminating their faces with rainbow brilliance.

Standing amid the crowd, Fang Yuan quietly examined everything, thinking inwardly: “Several hundred years ago, the Gu Yue clan migrated from Central Continent to Southern Border and settled on Qing Mao Mountain. They chose this place for a spirit spring in the underground cavern. That spring produces large quantities of primeval stones, it could be called the very foundation of Gu Yue Village.”

As they walked several hundred steps farther, their surroundings grew darker, and they began to hear the faint sound of water. Rounding a bend, they came upon an underground river just over ten metres wide.

Here, the coloured light of the stalactites had vanished entirely. Yet in the darkness, the river gave off a faint blue glow, like the river of stars in the night sky. The river flowed from the cavern’s dark depths, its water so clear that they could see fish swimming within it, aquatic plants, and even the sand and stones on the riverbed.

On the opposite bank lay a field of flowers.

These were moon orchids, deliberately cultivated by the Gu Yue clan. Their crescent-shaped petals had a delicate blue-pink hue. Their stems resembled jade, and their centers shone with the soft luster of pearls catching the light. At first glance, against the dark backdrop, the flower field beside the river resembled a vast blue-green carpet studded with countless pearls.

“Moon orchids are food for many kinds of Gu. This flower field could be called the clan’s largest growing site.” Fang Yuan understood this perfectly.

“So beautiful.”

“It really is lovely.”

It was an eye-opening sight for the youths. Their eyes shone, each of them both excited and nervous.

“All right,” the elder said. “Listen as I call your names. When your name is called, cross the river to the opposite bank. Walk as far as you can, the farther, the better, of course. Has everyone understood?”

“Understood,” the youths answered. Their families or seniors had already explained it before they came: the farther they walked, the better their aptitude, and the greater their future achievements would be.

“Gu Yue Chen Bo.” Holding the list, the elder called the first name.

Though wide, the river reached only to his knees. His expression solemn, Chen Bo stepped into the flower field on the opposite bank. He immediately felt an invisible pressure resisting his advance, as though a wall stood before him.

As he struggled to take another step, a sparse cluster of pure-white lights suddenly rose from the flowers beside his feet. The lights converged on Chen Bo and entered his body.

Chen Bo immediately felt the pressure ease considerably. The invisible wall suddenly seemed to soften. Gritting his teeth, he strained forward, forcing his way through the resistance. After three steps, the pressure ahead increased sharply again. It was like a wall once more; he could not advance any farther.

The elder sighed at the sight. As he recorded the result, he announced, “Gu Yue Chen Bo: three steps. No aptitude to become a Gu Master. Next, Gu Yue Zao Xie.”

Chen Bo’s face immediately turned pale. Teeth clenched, he crossed back through the river to where he had stood before. Without aptitude, he could only live as a mortal, occupying the very bottom of the clan’s hierarchy.

He swayed, nearly collapsing. The blow was devastating, as though it had crushed every hope he held for his life.

Many looked at him with pity, but even more had turned their attention to the second youth, who had reached the opposite bank. Unfortunately, this youth managed only four steps. He, too, lacked aptitude.

Not everyone possessed the aptitude to cultivate. Generally, five people out of ten being able to cultivate was already considered good. In the Gu Yue clan, the proportion was somewhat higher: six out of ten.

This was because the Gu Yue ancestor, the first clan leader, had been a renowned, legendary expert. His cultivation had caused genes carrying power to lie hidden within his bloodline. As his descendants, members of the Gu Yue clan generally possessed higher aptitude.

The two youths had proved, one after the other, to have no aptitude. The expressions of the other clan elders observing discreetly darkened. Even the seasoned, composed Gu Yue Clan Leader frowned slightly.

Just then, the Academy Elder called out the third name: “Gu Yue Mo Bei.”

“Here!” A horse-faced youth in hemp clothing answered with a short shout and stepped out from the crowd. He was tall and broad, far sturdier than others his age, with a hint of fierceness about him.

In a few strides, he crossed the river and stepped onto the opposite bank. Ten steps, twenty steps, thirty steps, faint lights continued to enter his body. He reached thirty-six steps before he could go no farther.

The youths across the river stared, their eyes wide and mouths agape. The Academy Elder cried out in delight, “Excellent! Gu Yue Mo Bei, Grade B aptitude! Come here and let me examine your primeval sea.”

Gu Yue Mo Bei returned to the Academy Elder’s side. The elder rested a hand on his shoulder, closed his eyes, and concentrated as he examined him. Then he withdrew his hand, nodded, and wrote on the paper: “Gu Yue Mo Bei. Primeval sea: sixty-six percent. Worth substantial investment in his development.”

Aptitude was divided into four grades, from highest to lowest: A, B, C, and D.

With about three years of training, a youth with Grade D aptitude could become a seasoned Rank 1 Gu Master, forming part of the clan’s foundation.

After two years of training, most youths with Grade C aptitude could become Rank 2 Gu Masters, forming the backbone of the clan.

A youth with Grade B aptitude warranted special care. Usually groomed as a future clan elder, such a youth could become a Rank 3 Gu Master in six or seven years.

As for Grade A aptitude, the emergence of even a single such talent was a stroke of good fortune for the entire clan. With attentive care and priority access to resources, such a youth could become a Rank 4 Gu Master in around ten years. At that point, they could compete to become clan leader!

In other words, provided Gu Yue Mo Bei fulfilled his potential, he would become an elder of the Gu Yue clan. No wonder the Academy Elder laughed in delight. The elders observing discreetly all breathed a sigh of relief, then turned to one of their colleagues with envy.

Gu Yue Mo Chen had the same horse-like face. Already smiling, he cast a challenging glance at his longtime rival. “Well, Gu Yue Chi Lian? My grandson isn’t bad, is he?”

The red-haired elder, Gu Yue Chi Lian, gave a cold snort but made no reply, his expression sullen and grim.

An hour later, half the youths had entered the flower field. Quite a few had shown Grade C or Grade D aptitude, though nearly half of those tested had none at all.

“Alas, our bloodline grows ever more diluted, and few Rank 4 experts have emerged in recent years to strengthen it. The fourth clan leader was our only Rank 5 expert, but he perished alongside the Flower-Wine Monk and left no descendants. The aptitude of our younger generations keeps declining.” The clan leader sighed deeply.

Just then, the Academy Elder called, “Gu Yue Chi Cheng.”

At the name, the elders turned to look at Gu Yue Chi Lian. This was his grandson.

Gu Yue Chi Cheng was short and slight, his face covered in pockmarks. With his fists clenched and sweat covering his face, he looked especially nervous.

He stepped onto the opposite bank, and points of light entered his body one after another. He continued for thirty-six steps before stopping.

“Another Grade B!” the Academy Elder exclaimed.

A stir ran through the youths as they turned envious eyes toward Gu Yue Chi Cheng.

“Thirty-six steps! Thirty-six!” Gu Yue Chi Lian shouted with a triumphant laugh, glaring challengingly at Gu Yue Mo Chen.

This time, it was Gu Yue Mo Chen’s turn to look livid.

“Gu Yue Chi Cheng…” Amid the crowd, Fang Yuan thoughtfully rubbed his chin.

Fang Yuan remembered that Chi Cheng had been severely punished by the clan for cheating during the Aperture Opening Ceremony. His aptitude was actually only Grade C, but his grandfather, Gu Yue Chi Lian, had arranged the deception that made it appear to be Grade B.

If Fang Yuan wanted to cheat, he had dozens of methods in mind, some more sophisticated than the one used for Gu Yue Chi Cheng. If he appeared to possess Grade B or even Grade A aptitude, the clan would certainly devote substantial resources to his development.

Firstly, too little time had passed since his rebirth. Given his circumstances, preparing such a deception would have been difficult.

Secondly, even if he succeeded, he could not conceal his subsequent cultivation speed. The deception would still come to light.

Gu Yue Chi Cheng, however, was different. His grandfather, Gu Yue Chi Lian, was one of the clan’s two most powerful elders and could cover for him.

“Gu Yue Chi Lian and Gu Yue Mo Chen have long been enemies. They head the clan’s two largest factions. To undermine his rival, Chi Lian needs his grandson to have outstanding aptitude. It was his protection behind the scenes that allowed Chi Cheng to conceal the truth for a time. As I remember it, the deception would never have come to light without that unexpected incident.”

A sharp gleam flickered in Fang Yuan’s eyes as he considered how to exploit this knowledge for the greatest possible benefit.

Exposing the deception now would earn him a small reward from the clan, but it would also antagonize the influential, high-ranking Gu Yue Chi Lian. That was out of the question. Blackmail was no option for now, either. He was too weak; attempting it would only bring disaster upon himself.

As he considered this, he suddenly heard the Academy Elder call his name:

“Gu Yue Fang Yuan!”
