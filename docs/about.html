The Omniarch Translation

A Restored Literary Edition

Reverend Insanity is a modern philosophical novel clothed in the brutal machinery of cultivation. This project exists to give Fang Yuan’s saga an English edition worthy of its ambition — faithful to the Chinese, exacting in its language, and compelling in its own right.

Read Closely. Translate Faithfully.

Every passage begins with the Chinese source and passes four gates: the original text, a close breakdown of its language, the reasoning behind each choice, and the final English rendering. Severity, humor, philosophy, and beauty — all receive the same scrutiny.

What the author meant is never quietly replaced by what the translator imagined. That is the whole discipline.

The Golden Standard

No chapter quotas. No unexamined phrasing. No shortcuts around difficult passages. Every chapter is reviewed line by line and polished for clarity, rhythm, and force — English that reads as literature without losing its allegiance to the original.

Gu Zhen Ren's Masterpiece

Penned by Gu Zhen Ren, Reverend Insanity spans 2,334 chapters across six volumes — an unflinching study of ambition, pragmatic cruelty, free will, and eternal life under preordained fate. Censored into hiatus in 2019; its legend has only grown.

Companion Work: The Legends of Ren Zu

Woven through the novel is its mythic scripture, The Legends of Ren Zu. Our immersive companion reader for the 44 ancestral parables — audio, codexes, theme guides — lives at thelegendsofrenzu.theomniarch.com.ng.
