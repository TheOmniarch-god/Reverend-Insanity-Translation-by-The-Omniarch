Who he is

Gu Yue Chi Cheng is the boy at the centre of the ceremony's first scandal, described without flattery:

Gu Yue Chi Cheng was short and slight, his face covered in pockmarks.

Chapter 4, paragraph 46

What the clan was told

He is announced as Grade B — a result that draws exactly the reaction his grandfather wanted:

A stir ran through the youths as they turned envious eyes toward Gu Yue Chi Cheng.

Chapter 4, paragraph 49

What is true

The result is a forgery. His real Aptitude is Grade C, and the deception is his grandfather Gu Yue Chi Lian's work:

His aptitude was actually only Grade C, but his grandfather, Gu Yue Chi Lian, had arranged the deception that made it appear to be Grade B.

Chapter 4, paragraph 53

Why he matters

Chi Cheng is a boy with no idea what he is standing in the middle of, and the reader knows it before he does. He is the first demonstration of how the world actually works: results are announced, and what is announced is not always what happened.
