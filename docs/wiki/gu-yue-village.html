What it is

Gu Yue Village is the Gu Yue Clan's home, and the novel introduces it as part of the mountain rather than separate from it.

This was Gu Yue Village, nestled upon Qing Mao Mountain, giving the vast, tranquil mountain range a distinct sense of human habitation.

That spring produces large quantities of primeval stones, and it could be called the very foundation of Gu Yue Village.
