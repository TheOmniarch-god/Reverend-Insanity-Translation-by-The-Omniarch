What they are

The novel states the world's premise in one line, and it is worth quoting in full because everything else grows out of it:

Humans are the spirit of all living things; Gu are the essence of heaven and earth.

Chapter 2, paragraph 8

Gu are creatures. They are refined, held, fed and used — and a person who holds the right ones can do what the rest of the world would call magic.

What they make possible

The clan's entire economy rests on them. The one concrete Gu named so far, the Spring Autumn Cicada, was expensive enough to be worth a massacre:

To refine that cursed Spring Autumn Cicada, you slaughtered tens of millions of innocent lives!

Chapter 1, paragraph 3

And the Gu Yue Village's wealth is the raw material for them:

That spring produces large quantities of primeval stones—it could be called the very foundation of Gu Yue Village.

Chapter 4, paragraph 7

Where a person starts

To use Gu at all, you must first be able to hold them — which is what the Aperture Opening Ceremony decides:

Right now, my primeval sea is not yet open; I have not even stepped onto the cultivation path of a Gu Master.

Chapter 2, paragraph 18

Why they matter

Every wiki can define Gu. This one can show you the sentence where the novel defines them — and where the reader learns that in this world, insects are what people are made of, what they kill for, and what they become.
