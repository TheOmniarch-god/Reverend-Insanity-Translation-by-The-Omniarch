Moon Orchid

月兰花 · 月光兰

Gu Yue Clan · Moon Orchid · Cultivation

Delicate blue-pink subterranean flowers cultivated by the Gu Yue clan, serving as the essential food source for Moonlight Gu and related species.

In the text

This name is not written in the published chapters yet. The entry is assembled from what they describe — and it will be rewritten the moment a chapter names it.

Read to chapter 4 →

What they are

Moon orchids are luminous flowers cultivated within the subterranean spirit cavern beneath Gu Yue Village. Their presence turns the dark cavern into a vibrant sea of pale petals.

These were moon orchids, deliberately cultivated by the Gu Yue clan. Their crescent-shaped petals had a delicate blue-pink hue.

Chapter 4, paragraph 11

Why the clan cultivates them

Gu are living creatures that require regular nourishment to survive. As Chapter 4 explains, this deliberate garden exists because moon orchids are the irreplaceable food supply for the clan's core signature Gu worms.

Moon orchids are food for many kinds of Gu. This flower field could be called the clan’s largest growing site.

Chapter 4, paragraph 12

Why they matter

The moon orchid field is also the proving ground of the Aperture Opening Ceremony. When prospective youths step among the blossoms, the resonance between their latent bloodline and the subterranean flowers draws out the lights that awaken their apertures.
