Spring Autumn Cicada 春秋蝉

First Appearance · p1 · Rank 7 Mystical

Seventh among the Ten Great Mystical Gu. Time-path Gu that reverses time, sending will back 500 years. Fang Yuan refines it at cost of ten million lives, self-detonates rather than yield it. Chun Qiu = Spring Autumn Annals, history's cycle. Cicada = rebirth, shedding shell. Core of rebirth premise. Superscript ¹ because appears before Huaxia.

Huaxia 华夏

First Appearance · p11 · Earth Origin

Ancient poetic name for China — central civilized lands, cradle of Tang poetry Fang Yuan recites. Retaining pinyin preserves marker that he is a Chinese student on Earth (approved), not just "from Earth". First seen when Fang Yuan recalls Earth life. Links to Preface.

Gu Yue Village 古月山寨

First Appearance · Ch1 · Location · 3D Pin

古月 = Ancient Moon — clan surname. Several thousand stilt houses draped as brilliant ribbon at night. Grand pavilion heart where Aperture Opening Ceremony held. Fang lineage lives under aunt/uncle. Through 500-year demon lord eyes, a cage — but sturdy cage is safety. 3D pin x0.9 z1.1 in squircle world above, click to fly in.

Qing Mao Mountain 青茅山

First Appearance · Ch1 · Location · 3D Terrain

青茅 = Green Thatch — verdant thatch mountain. Vast tranquil range, nourished by spring rain. Home to three villages: Gu Yue, Bai, Xiong — three-way balance. Spirit spring in cavern produces primeval stones, foundation of village. 3D terrain in squircle world — north/south towers, mountain gate, Bai/Xiong peaks.

3

Demonic Path / Righteous Path 魔道 / 正道

First Appearance · p14 · Philosophy

Mo Dao — no good karma, only murder and arson; spurned by heaven, enemies of whole world, still wild following whim. Zheng Dao — revered sects, order and law. Not good vs evil — two cultivation philosophies. Fang Yuan: "If this newly refined Spring Autumn Cicada works, I will still be a demon in next life!"

Kowtow 叩头

Culture · Ch1 p24 · Ritual

叩头 = knock head — forehead meets wood with soft thuds, palms flat, upper body erect. Ancestral hall ritual before spirit tablets. Shows clan reverence, hierarchy. Fang Yuan's kowtow perfunctory — 500-year demon lord bowing to mortals — irony. Different from 跪拜 — more solemn, audible.

4

Aperture Opening Ceremony 开窍大典

First Appearance · p22 · System Core

Ritual where fifteen-year-olds open aperture — primeval sea within, crystal membrane. Gu Zhen Ren writes 两三成 not % — preserve as words: forty-four percent Grade C limit (Fang Yuan), twenty to thirty-nine percent Grade D, sixty+ B, eighty+ A. Tolkien tone, no %. First to Ninth Revolution later Ch7. Click Aperture tab in 3D card to see sea visualization.

8

Primeval Essence 真元 · Primeval Stones 元石

System · Ch1/Ch2 · Currency

真元 = true primeval essence — drives Gu, quantified as sea %. 元石 = primeval stones — condensed essence of heaven and earth, currency + cultivation resource, like gold standard. "Humans are spirit of all living things; Gu are true essence of heaven and earth." Stones from spirit spring. C-grade 44% means limited essence.
