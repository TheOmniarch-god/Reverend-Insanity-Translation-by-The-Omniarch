Who they are

The Righteous Path appears in the novel's first lines as a coalition on the hunt.

Today, the revered sects of the Righteous Path have united with a single purpose, to trample your demonic lair into dust.

Chapter 1, paragraph 2

What the novel does with them

It does not let them be simply heroes. They surround a dying man, they want what he is carrying, and they offer him a quick death in exchange for it.

Hand over the Spring Autumn Cicada without struggle, and I shall grant you a quick death!

Chapter 1, paragraph 1

And the mob does not move even when the man is finished, because they have seen what he is still capable of.

None of them dared make a move; every soul among them trembled before the final, dying wrath of Old Demon Fang.

Chapter 1, paragraph 10

Why it matters

The Righteous Path is the first moral claim the novel examines, and it fails the examination inside a page. That is the reader's first lesson in how this book treats institutions, and it is why its opposite, the Demonic Path, is not simply the villain's banner either.
