Primeval Stone

元石

Cultivation · Gu Yue Clan · Primeval Stones

The crystalline mineral produced by natural spirit springs, serving as the universal currency of the Gu world and the essential fuel for primeval cultivation.

In the text

What it is

Primeval stones are natural crystalline deposits rich in primeval energy. In Chapter 4, they are identified as the very lifeblood sustaining Gu Yue Village.

That spring produces large quantities of primeval stones, and it could be called the very foundation of Gu Yue Village.

Chapter 4, paragraph 7

How the clan uses them

Primeval stones serve a dual role in mortal society: they are the standard currency used for all trade, and they provide the raw essence required by a Gu Master to replenish their Primeval Sea and nourish their Gu.

Why they matter

The spirit spring hidden beneath the Clan Leader's Pavilion produces these stones continuously. Control over that spring is what enabled the Gu Yue Clan to migrate from Central Continent, establish a fortress on Qing Mao Mountain, and maintain their sovereignty against rival clans.
