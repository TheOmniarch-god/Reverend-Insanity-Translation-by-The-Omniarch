Xiong Clan

熊族 · 熊家寨

Southern Border · Rival Clan · Qing Mao Mountain

One of the three major mortal clans sharing Qing Mao Mountain, locked in cautious rivalry with the Gu Yue and Bai clans.

Who they are

The Xiong Clan inhabits Xiong Village upon Qing Mao Mountain, forming one leg of the uneasy tripod of power on the mountain alongside the Gu Yue Clan and the Bai Clan.

Both Bai Village and Xiong Village have seen remarkable talents emerge over recent years.

Chapter 1, paragraph 41

Their position on the mountain

While Bai Ning Bing has drawn the most alarmed attention from the Gu Yue elders, the Xiong Clan remains an active and dangerous competitor, regularly producing its own crop of capable young Gu Masters.

Why they matter

The presence of the Xiong Clan prevents either the Gu Yue or Bai clans from acting recklessly. The three factions maintain a watchful truce, competing intensely during each annual Aperture Opening Ceremony to secure superior cultivation talent.
