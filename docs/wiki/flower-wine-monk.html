Who he was

The Flower Wine Monk was a mysterious, powerful demonic cultivator who clashed with the Gu Yue Clan several generations before the story begins.

The fourth clan leader was our only Rank 5 expert, but he perished alongside the Flower Wine Monk and left no descendants.

Chapter 4, paragraph 43

His impact on the clan

The battle between the Flower Wine Monk and the fourth clan leader cost the Gu Yue clan its sole Rank 5 cultivator. Because the leader perished without passing down his lineage, the clan's overall bloodline talent entered a steady decline.

Why he matters

Even in death, the Flower Wine Monk remains a looming historical shadow over Qing Mao Mountain. His clash with the righteous establishment embodies the violent collision between solitary demonic wanderers and entrenched clan power.
