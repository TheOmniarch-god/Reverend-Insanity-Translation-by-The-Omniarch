Reverend Insanity

by Gu Zhen Ren · 蛊真人

Xianxia Martial Arts Reincarnation Action Drama

Synopsis

Humans are the spirit of all living things; Gu are the true essence of heaven and earth.

A story of a transmigrator continuously reborn through time.

A wondrous, bizarre world of raising Gu, refining Gu, and using Gu.

Spring Autumn Cicada Moonlight Gu Liquor Worm One Qi Golden Light Worm Black Hair Gu Hope Gu
Majestic mountains — the endless path is truly like iron;
Yet today, I stride forward and cross it from the start.
Facing peril, I refine the essence of Heaven and Earth into my body;
My heart, as ever, gazes at the Heavens
