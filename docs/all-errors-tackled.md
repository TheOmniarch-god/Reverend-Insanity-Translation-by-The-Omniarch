### 1. 50 Suns Continents vs Caravans — Fandom Bullshit, Raw Says 7-8 Earths

**Alleged error:** Mortal Gu Masters caravan around continents while continents supposed to be size of 50 suns combined.

**Full raw scan 2334 chapters:**
- Search for "五十" + "太阳" across all 2342 files: 0 results.
- Search for "5000万公里" "五十万公里" "中洲" + "公里": 0 geography results. Only primeval stone trade in Ch432.
- Only size line in entire raw: Ch37 raw: "这个世界面积广大无边，单单方源生活的南疆，就比七八个地球的总面积还要广阔。这里的生活环境十分恶劣，人类通常以家族的形式，建造山寨，龟缩一隅。"
- Translation: This world is vast, just Southern Border where Fang Yuan lives is already broader than total area of seven to eight Earths.

**Classification:** Fandom bullshit / TN error, not author error. 50 suns comes from misreading star path killer moves that summon star shadows, not actual geography. 50 million km comes from fandom wiki Central Continent page "over fifty million kilometers wide" — no source in raw.

**Canon continent sizes from raw + fandom wiki reference but corrected:**
- Northern Plains: 5 billion km², about 10 times Earth surface (510M km² x 10 = 5.1B). Source wiki Northern Plains.
- Southern Border: 7 to 8 Earths = 3.5 to 4 billion km², matches raw Ch37.
- Central Continent: If 50,000,000 km wide, area = pi * (25M)^2 = 1.96 quadrillion km² = 2.5 quadrillion square, about 5 million times Earth. Absurd, dwarfs other regions 1 million times, caravans impossible. Corrected to 50,000 km wide = pi * (25k)^2 = 1.96B km² circular, 2.5B square, about 5 to 15 times Earth, same ballpark as others. If 50k is diameter, area ~7.8B km² reasonable.
- Eastern Sea and Western Desert: no explicit size in raw, assumed each at least 1 Earth, wiki says each ~7 Earths like Southern Border.
- Total 5 regions: ~5B + 3.5B + 7.8B + 3.5B + 3.5B = ~23B km² = ~45 times Earth surface.
- Plus Black Heaven and White Heaven: each is size of 5 regions combined, so 5 regions x 2 = 10 regions more. Total world = 15 regions = ~70B km² = ~105 to 120 times Earth.

**Implementation:** When chapter says 50 suns, we write 7 to 8 Earths directly. When says 50,000,000 km, we write 50,000 km directly. Chapter 4 migration from Central Continent to Southern Border stays plausible.
