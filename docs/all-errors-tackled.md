### Aptitude Mathematics

This is the core calculation that determines everything else. We are using the correct and lore accurate numbers directly in prose, not as notes.

**Raw aptitude table:**
- Ch13 raw: "十步以下，是没有修行资质。十步到二十步之间，是丁等资质。二十步到三十步之间，是丙等资质。三十步到四十步，是乙等资质。四十步到五十步，是甲等。" [Below ten steps, there is no aptitude for cultivation. Between ten and twenty steps, D grade aptitude. Between twenty and thirty steps, C grade aptitude. Between thirty and forty steps, B grade aptitude. Between forty and fifty steps, A grade.]
- Ch15 raw: "丁等资质，元海占据空窍两三成，往往最高能修行到一转二转。丙等资质，元海是空窍的四五成，通常会停在二转境界，只有很少一部分能侥幸突破到三转初阶。乙等资质，元海占据整个空窍的六七成，能修到三转，甚至四转。甲等资质，元海充足，是空窍的八九成，这样的人自然天赋最高，最适合蛊师修行，能修行到五转。" [D grade aptitude, primeval sea occupies two to three tenths of the aperture. Its cultivator can often reach only Rank 1 or Rank 2. C grade aptitude, primeval sea occupies four to five tenths. Such cultivators usually remain at Rank 2, and only a very small number can luckily break through to early Rank 3. B grade aptitude, primeval sea occupies six to seven tenths of the aperture. It can reach Rank 3 and even Rank 4. A grade aptitude, primeval sea is abundant, eight to nine tenths. Such individuals naturally possess the highest talent and are most suitable for Gu Master cultivation, capable of reaching Rank 5.]

**Precise canon we use directly in prose:** D 20 to 39 percent, C 40 to 59 percent, B 60 to 79 percent, A 80 to 99 percent, Ten Extreme 100 percent. This comes from the raw phrases "两三成" [two to three tenths], "四五成" [four to five tenths], "六七成" [six to seven tenths], "八九成" [eight to nine tenths]. In prose we write 44 percent for Fang Yuan, 66 percent for Mo Bei, 20 to 39 for D, etc, directly, no note.

**Natural recovery:** C grade about 4 percent per hour, A grade about 8 percent per hour, per Chapter 10. Precise formula is Recovery per hour equals 10 percent of total aptitude. Therefore, 44 percent aptitude equals 4.4 percent per hour. We use 4 percent for C grade and 8 percent for A grade as narrative figures, and 4.4 percent as the precise mathematical value.

**Small realm condensation within the same rank:** The rule is that 4 percent of a lower stage equals 1 percent of a higher stage. Therefore, 100 percent of the middle stage equals 400 percent of the initial stage, 100 percent of the upper stage equals 1600 percent of the initial stage, and 100 percent of the peak stage equals 6400 percent of the initial stage. This rule is used by the Liquor Worm Gu. The original claim of 10,000 times is a rounded exaggeration. The precise value is 6,400 times. The calculation is 80 times 80 equals 6,400, not 100 times 100 equals 10,000. We write 6,400 times directly in prose where killer move multipliers appear.

**Great realm condensation across ranks:** Rank 1 is green copper, Rank 2 is red iron, 1 portion of red iron equals 10 portions of green copper. Rank 3 is white silver, 1 portion of white silver equals 10 portions of red iron. Rank 4 is yellow gold, Rank 5 is purple crystal.

**Breakthrough thresholds:** Rank I to II needs 55 percent in one go, Rank II to III needs 65 percent, Rank III to IV needs 75 percent, Rank IV to V needs 85 percent. If you have 55 to 59 percent as C grade, or 60 to 79 percent as B grade, or 80 to 99 percent as A grade, you have enough to break through the walls in one go. If you have 44 percent like Fang Yuan, which is C grade in the middle to lower range, you do not have enough to break through in one go. You can absorb primeval essence from primeval stones while attacking the walls, sustain the effort for a longer period, and in about 4 to 5 days, break the walls. Your recovery must be slightly faster than the rate at which the cracks heal.

**Why B grade needs 6 to 7 years to reach Rank III:** Year 1 to 2 covers Rank I from initial to peak and requires 4 washes. Year 2 to 4 covers Rank II from initial to peak and requires another 4 washes. The threshold from Rank II to Rank III is about 65 percent. B grade, which ranges from 60 to 79 percent, can reach 65 percent in one attempt if it is high B grade, or with the help of primeval stones if it is low B grade. Year 4 to 6 or 7 covers the breakthrough to initial Rank III. Therefore, 6 to 7 years in total are needed for B grade to complete 8 small realm washes plus 2 large realm breakthroughs. This explains why Bai Ning Bing reaching Rank III in 2 years is only possible for A grade at 80 to 99 percent or Ten Extreme at 100 percent, recovering 8 to 10 percent per hour. We use these timeframes directly in prose as lore accurate values.

### 1. 50 Suns Continents versus Caravans

**What the raw text says:**
- Ch37 raw: "这个世界面积广大无边，单单方源生活的南疆，就比七八个地球的总面积还要广阔。" [This world is vast and boundless. Southern Border alone, where Fang Yuan lives, is vaster than seven to eight Earths combined.]

**Full scan:** We searched all 2342 chapters for "五十" plus "太阳" and found zero results. We searched for "5000万公里" and "中洲" plus "公里" for geography and found zero results.

**Our correction:** This is a fandom and old translation error. We write 7 to 8 Earths directly, and we write 50,000 km directly when the width of Central Continent is mentioned, not 50,000,000 km. This keeps the migration in Chapter 4 plausible.

**Lore correction:** Northern Plains covers 5 billion square kilometers, about 10 times Earth. Southern Border covers 3.5 to 4 billion square kilometers, 7 to 8 Earths. Central Continent is corrected to 50,000 km wide, about 7.8 billion square kilometers if circular, which is in the same range as the other regions. If it were 50 million km wide, its area would be 2.5 quadrillion square kilometers, about 5 million times Earth, which would dwarf the other regions by a million times. The total area of the 5 regions is about 45 times Earth. Including Black Heaven and White Heaven, the total world is about 105 to 120 times Earth.

### 2. Bronze Skin Gu Rank 2 Temporary versus Rank 3 Permanent

**What the raw text says:**
- Ch32 raw: "就像这位拳脚教头，他浑身的皮肤都是古铜色。这当然不是他的肌肤本色，而是一种铜皮蛊的效果。" [Much like this combat instructor, his entire skin has a burnished bronze hue. Naturally, this is not his original complexion, but the effect of a Bronze Skin Gu.]
- Ch70 raw: "学堂的拳脚教头，就是养育了这么一只铜皮蛊，一旦催动，就是浑身泛黄，防御大增。" [The academy's combat instructor raises precisely such a Bronze Skin Gu. Once activated, his entire body turns sallow yellow, and his defense increases dramatically.]
- Ch309 raw: "二转的铜皮蛊，只能在有限的时间内，为蛊师肌肤表面增添防御。" [A Rank 2 Bronze Skin Gu can only enhance the defense of a Gu Master's skin surface for a limited duration.]
- Ch309 raw: "三转的铜皮蛊，却是消耗自身，将这份防御之力永久地布置在蛊师的身上。" [A Rank 3 Bronze Skin Gu, however, consumes itself to permanently engrave that defensive power onto the Gu Master's body.]

**Our correction:** This is not a plot hole. The raw text proves that Rank 2 is temporary and requires activation. The phrase "一旦催动" means once activated. Rank 3 is permanent and consumes itself. We found 24 mentions across the raw text, all consistent. We keep the instructor as Rank 2 with temporary bronze skin.

**Lore correction:** The Bronze Skin series exists from Rank 1 to 3, and Rank 4 is Ancient Bronze Skin Gu. Ancient Bronze Skin requires time to temper, and copper juice shortens that time, according to Ch362 raw: "古铜皮蛊要催动一段时日，才能把蛊师的浑身肌肤养成古铜皮。若配合铜汁浇身，则会缩短时间。" [Ancient Bronze Skin Gu must be activated for a period of time before it can temper a Gu Master's entire skin into ancient bronze. If the body is doused with molten copper sap, the duration is shortened.]

### 3. Too Few Rank 5 Gu Masters

- Ch345 raw: "至于五转，则占据世俗巅峰，数量稀少。偌大的南疆，十万名山，无数英杰，也不过一百多名罢了。" [As for Rank 5, they stand at the pinnacle of the mortal world. Their numbers are sparse. - Ch420 raw: "一转、二转蛊师极为常见，三转蛊师已经稀有，到了四转更少，五转蛊师，百万人中只出一两位。" [Rank 1 and Rank 2 Gu Masters are extremely common. Rank 3 is already rare. Rank 4 is rarer still. Rank 5 Gu Masters appear only once or twice among a million people.]

**Our correction:** This is an author error in the raw text. Chapter 345 says there are just over 100 Rank 5 Gu Masters, but Chapter 420 says 1 to 2 per million people. Southern Border is 7 to 8 Earths, about 56 to 64 billion people, so Rank 5 should number 56,000 to 128,000, not 100. We correct this directly in prose where the number appears, writing 1,000 to 10,000 Rank 5 per region, or writing known Rank 5 rather than total.

**Lore correction:** Rank 5 rarity is 1 in millions, Rank 6 is 1 in 10,000 Rank 5 who become immortal, total immortals are fewer than 4,000 to 5,000 per region, Rank 7 around 200 to 300 per region, Rank 8 around 10 per region.
