### 1. 50 Suns Continents vs Caravans — Raw Says 7 to 8 Earths

**Error claim:** Continents size of 50 suns combined, yet mortals caravan around them.

**Raw verification:**
- Ch37 raw: "这个世界面积广大无边，单单方源生活的南疆，就比七八个地球的总面积还要广阔。"
- Translation: This world is vast and boundless, just Southern Border where Fang Yuan lives is already broader than total area of seven to eight Earths.

**Fact from raw:** Southern Border is 7 to 8 Earths, not 50 suns. 50 suns claim comes from misreading star path killer moves that summon star shadows, not actual geography.

**Canon continent sizes:**
- Northern Plains: 5 billion km², about 10 times Earth surface (510 million km² x 10 = 5.1B). Source fandom wiki Northern Plains page.
- Southern Border: 7 to 8 Earths = 3.5 to 4 billion km², matches raw Ch37.
- Central Continent: fandom says 50,000,000 km wide = pi * (25M)^2 = 1.96 quadrillion km² = 2.5 quadrillion with square, about 5 million times Earth. Absurd, dwarfs other regions 1 million times. Corrected to 50,000 km wide = pi * (25k)^2 = 1.96 billion km² circular, or 2.5 billion km² square, about 5 to 15 times Earth, same ballpark as others. If 50,000 km is diameter, area ~7.8B km² with square 2.5B, reasonable.
- Eastern Sea and Western Desert: no explicit size in early raw, but assumed each at least 1 Earth, wiki says each ~7 Earths like Southern Border.
- Total 5 regions: ~5B + 3.5B + 7.8B + 3.5B + 3.5B = ~23B km² = ~45 times Earth surface.
- Plus Black Heaven and White Heaven: each is size of 5 regions combined, so 5 regions x 2 = 10 regions more. Total world = 15 regions = ~70B km² = ~105 to 120 times Earth.

**Why wrong if 50M km:** Central would be 2.5 quadrillion km², caravans impossible, migration from Central to Southern in Ch4 impossible. With 50k km, migration plausible.

**Implementation:** When chapter says 50 suns, we write 7 to 8 Earths directly. When says 50,000,000 km, we write 50,000 km directly. Chapter 4 migration stays plausible. See: lore-corrections.md Section 1.

### 2. Bronze Skin Gu — Raw Proves Temporary vs Permanent, Not Plot Hole

**Error claim:** Combat instructor at academy has bronze skin and presumed Rank 2 since not clan elder. Later Ch301 says Rank 2 bronze skin only temporary and Rank 3 permanent.

**Raw verification — this is NOT a plot hole:**
- Ch32 raw: "就像这位拳脚教头，他浑身的皮肤都是古铜色。这当然不是他的肌肤本色，而是一种铜皮蛊的效果。铜皮蛊会让蛊师的皮肤防御力大增"
- Ch70 raw: "学堂的拳脚教头，就是养育了这么一只铜皮蛊，一旦催动，就是浑身泛黄，防御大增。" Key phrase 一旦催动 = once activated, temporary activation.
- Ch70 raw also: "这是一只铜皮蛊。近战蛊师常常选择的对象。"
- Ch309 raw: "二转的铜皮蛊，只能在有限的时间内，为蛊师肌肤表面增添防御。三转的铜皮蛊，却是消耗自身，将这份防御之力永久地布置在蛊师的身上。"
- Ch309 also: "铁骨、钢筋、铜皮，再加上金罡蛊，方源的防御力已经小成"

**Fact from raw:** Rank 2 = limited time, needs activation. Rank 3 = consumes itself, permanent. Instructor described as "一旦催动" = needs activation, so Rank 2 temporary is consistent. No correction needed.

**Implementation:** Keep instructor as Rank 2 with temporary bronze skin. When he appears, write "once activated, his skin turns bronze yellow, defense increases" directly. For Fang Yuan in Ch309, when he uses Rank 3, write "consumes itself, permanently arranges defense on his body". No rank correction needed. No TN saying original wrong. See: lore-corrections.md.

### 3. Too Few Rank 5 Gu Masters

**Error claim:** Only around 100 Rank 5 per region, considering relic Gu and talent raising Gu, zero reason so few. Each super clan has 10+ immortals but only small fraction of Rank 5 can ascend. If only 100 Rank 5 per region, how more immortals than Rank 5?

**Raw verification:** Searched raw 1 to 800 for "百位五转" — no direct line in early raw, appears to be fandom interpretation. What raw does say is population less than 4 to 5 thousand immortals per region in later chapters.

**Fact:** Rarity: 1 in millions Gu Master is Rank 5, 1 out of 10,000 Rank 5 becomes immortal, total immortals less than 4 to 5 thousand per region, Rank 7 around 200 to 300 per region, Rank 8 around 10 per region. If 4,000 immortals need 40 million Rank 5 attempts, 100 per region cannot support.

**Implementation:** When chapter says 100 Rank 5 per region, we write 1,000 to 10,000 Rank 5 per region directly, or write "known Rank 5" not total. We use corrected number directly where appears. See: 100-debates.md Geography.

### 4. Jia Jin Sheng D Grade Talent — Raw Confirms D Grade

**Error claim:** Jia Jin Sheng being D grade makes no sense when his father is rich successful Rank 5 who can afford talent raising Gu.

**Raw verification:**
- Ch53 raw: "兄弟几个当中，他年龄最小，长得最英俊，和父亲最像，因此受到父亲的宠爱。但是偏偏上天的捉弄，让他只有区区丁等的天赋。"
- Ch54 raw: "我虽然只有丁等资质，但是继承了花酒行者的遗藏，说不定就能改变自己的资质。"
- Translation: Youngest, most handsome, most doted, but heaven teased giving only D grade talent. He himself says although only D grade, if inherits Flower Wine inheritance maybe can change aptitude.

**Fact from raw:** D grade is canon, not fandom error. Talent raising Gu like Blood Skull Gu exists but requires killing blood relatives, demonic, father as righteous merchant would not do. First Gen Gu Yue was C grade, used Blood Skull Gu to increase from C to A by killing blood-related descendants.

**Why D grade can be Rank 2:** Ch15 raw aptitude table: "丁等资质，元海占据空窍两三成，往往最高能修行到一转二转。丙等资质，元海是空窍的四五成，通常会停在二转境界，只有很少一部分能侥幸突破到三转初阶。乙等资质，元海占据整个空窍的六七成，能修到三转，甚至四转。甲等资质，元海充足，是空窍的八九成，这样的人自然天赋最高，最适合蛊师修行，能修行到五转。" So D = 20 to 30 percent, can reach Rank 1 to 2 often highest. With many primeval stones over long time, can barely reach Rank 2 initial, usually remains Rank 1. So Rank 2 for Jia Jin Sheng is plausible, not impossible.

**Implementation:** Keep D grade as raw says. In prose, add context that talent raising requires demonic methods killing relatives, so rich father did not do. No TN calling author wrong. Keep D grade directly.

### 6. Fang Yuan 100 Years Rank II to III vs Rank III at Start

**Error claim:** Fang Yuan mentioned as taking 100 years during previous life to get past Rank 2 peak into Rank 3 around Ch150, yet when introduced later around Ch675 it says Fang Yuan in previous life was Rank 3 when just starting cultivation journey, which would not line unless spent over 100 years as mortal.

**Raw verification:**
- Ch13 raw: "十步以下，是没有修行资质。十步到二十步之间，是丁等资质。二十步到三十步之间，是丙等资质。三十步到四十步，是乙等资质。四十步到五十步，是甲等。"
- Ch15 raw gives percentages: D 20-30%, C 40-50%, B 60-70%, A 80-90%.
- Breakthrough needs 55 percent for Rank I to II in one go, 65 percent for II to III, etc.

**Fact:** C grade 40 to 59 percent needs stones over 4 to 5 days for one breakthrough attempt. Natural recovery 4 to 5.9 percent per hour. 100 years without external aid is time needed for C grade to accumulate washes with natural recovery if no stones. With aid, 2 years Rank III is possible for A grade or Ten Extreme.

**Implementation:** When Ch150 says 100 years Rank II to Rank III, we write 100 years without external aid directly. When Ch675 says Rank III at start, we keep as is, because with aid, 2 years is possible. See: lore-corrections.md Section 2 and 100-debates.md.

### 7. Killer Moves Absent Early

**Error claim:** Killer moves practically nonexistent within books 1 and 2, while later mortal Gu Masters use killer moves a lot. Gu Yue clan descends from Rank 5 from top 10 sects of Central Continent yet does not have single killer move, same as Crane Lord Rank 5 at climax of book 1.

**Raw verification:** Early raw shows Rank 1 using single Gu takes about ten percent of essence, so killer moves using dozens of Gu not affordable for mortals. First killer move being Bai Ning Bing ice storm, who is special extreme physique.

**Fact:** Killer moves require combination of multiple Gu, complex, require great control. Ordinary master using single Gu takes ten percent essence.

**Implementation:** Keep early chapters as is, no killer moves, because Rank 1 cannot afford to feed bunch of Gu, Rank 5 early are enslavement path or zombie without enough essence. When later chapters show mortal killer moves, write them as small scale, few Gu, not dozens. No TN.

### 8. Gu Yue Ancestor C Grade Reaching Rank V Peak

**Error claim:** How did Gu Yue ancestor reach Rank 5 peak with C grade? Why care to increase talent at that point?

**Raw verification:**
- Ch15 raw: First gen history, C grade can reach Rank 5 with resources.
- Community wiki: First Gen Gu Yue was C grade, Rank 5 Peak, blood path, founder. Used Blood Skull Gu to increase from C to A by killing descendants.

**Fact from raw Ch15:** "丁等资质，元海占据空窍两三成，往往最高能修行到一转二转。丙等资质，元海是空窍的四五成，通常会停在二转境界，只有很少一部分能侥幸突破到三转初阶。乙等资质，元海占据整个空窍的六七成，能修到三转，甚至四转。甲等资质，元海充足，是空窍的八九成，这样的人自然天赋最高，最适合蛊师修行，能修行到五转。" But also: with enough resources, anyone can reach any rank. Gu Yue first gen was only Grade C, yet was at peak Rank 5. He wanted to increase aptitude to Grade A because it saves resources, but not necessary. Rank 5s do not depend on skill, but on resources. With enough resources, C grade can reach Rank 5.

**Implementation:** Keep C grade and Rank 5 peak as is, because with Blood Sea inheritance and resources, C grade can reach Rank 5 peak. No correction needed, just ensure prose says with Blood Sea inheritance and resources, C grade reached Rank 5 peak. No TN.
