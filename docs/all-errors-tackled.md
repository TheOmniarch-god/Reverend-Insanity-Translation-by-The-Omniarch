### 1. 50 Suns Continents vs Caravans

**What was claimed:** Continents size of 50 suns combined, yet mortals caravan around them.

**What raw says:**
- Ch37 raw: "这个世界面积广大无边，单单方源生活的南疆，就比七八个地球的总面积还要广阔。这里的生活环境十分恶劣，人类通常以家族的形式，建造山寨，龟缩一隅。" [This world is vast and boundless. Southern Border alone, where Fang Yuan dwells, is vaster than seven to eight Earths combined. The environment here is brutally inhospitable. Mankind survives by clustering into clans, raising fortified villages upon the mountains, each cowering within its own secluded corner.]

**Full scan:** Searched all 2342 chapters for "五十" plus "太阳", zero results. Searched for "5000万公里" and "中洲" plus "公里" for geography, zero results.

**Our fixation:** This is not author error, it is fandom and old translation error. We will write 7 to 8 Earths directly when size is mentioned, and write 50,000 km directly when Central Continent width is mentioned, not 50,000,000 km. This keeps Chapter 4 migration from Central Continent to Southern Border plausible, and keeps caravans plausible.

**Lore correction:** Northern Plains 5 billion km², about 10 times Earth. Southern Border 3.5 to 4 billion km², 7 to 8 Earths, matches raw Ch37. Central Continent corrected to 50,000 km wide, about 7.8 billion km² if circular, same ballpark as other regions. If it were 50 million km wide, area would be 2.5 quadrillion km², about 5 million times Earth, which would dwarf other regions by a million times and make caravans impossible. Eastern Sea and Western Desert each about 7 Earths. Total 5 regions about 45 times Earth. Plus Black Heaven and White Heaven, each size of 5 regions, total world about 105 to 120 times Earth.

### 2. Bronze Skin Gu Rank 2 Temporary vs Rank 3 Permanent

**What was claimed:** Combat instructor has bronze skin and is presumed Rank 2, but later says Rank 2 bronze skin only temporary and Rank 3 permanent.

**What raw says:**
- Ch32 raw: "就像这位拳脚教头，他浑身的皮肤都是古铜色。这当然不是他的肌肤本色，而是一种铜皮蛊的效果。" [Much like this combat instructor, his entire skin bears a burnished bronze hue. Naturally, this is not his native complexion, but the effect of a Bronze Skin Gu.]
- Ch70 raw: "学堂的拳脚教头，就是养育了这么一只铜皮蛊，一旦催动，就是浑身泛黄，防御大增。" [The academy's combat instructor raises precisely such a Bronze Skin Gu. Once activated, his entire body flushes sallow yellow, and his defense surges dramatically.]
- Ch309 raw: "二转的铜皮蛊，只能在有限的时间内，为蛊师肌肤表面增添防御。" [A Rank 2 Bronze Skin Gu can only, for a limited duration, augment the defense of a Gu Master's skin surface.]
- Ch309 raw: "三转的铜皮蛊，却是消耗自身，将这份防御之力永久地布置在蛊师的身上。" [A Rank 3 Bronze Skin Gu, however, consumes itself to permanently engrave that defensive power upon the Gu Master's body.]

**Our fixation:** This is not a plot hole. Raw proves Rank 2 is temporary and needs activation, phrase "一旦催动" means once activated. Rank 3 is permanent and consumes itself. We found 24 mentions across raw, all consistent. We will keep instructor as Rank 2 with temporary bronze skin, writing "once activated, his skin turns bronze yellow" directly. For Fang Yuan at Rank 3, we will write "consumes itself to permanently engrave defense upon his body".

**Lore correction:** Bronze Skin series goes Rank 1 to 3, Rank 4 is Ancient Bronze Skin Gu. Ancient Bronze Skin needs time to temper, copper juice shortens time, per Ch362 raw: "古铜皮蛊要催动一段时日，才能把蛊师的浑身肌肤养成古铜皮。若配合铜汁浇身，则会缩短时间。" [Ancient Bronze Skin Gu must be activated for a span of time before it can temper a Gu Master's entire skin into ancient bronze. If complemented by dousing the body in molten copper sap, the duration is shortened.]

### 3. Too Few Rank 5 Gu Masters

**What was claimed:** Only around 100 Rank 5 per region, too few to support immortals.

**What raw says:**
- Ch345 raw: "至于五转，则占据世俗巅峰，数量稀少。偌大的南疆，十万名山，无数英杰，也不过一百多名罢了。" [As for Rank 5, they occupy the pinnacle of the mortal world. Their numbers are sparse. Throughout the vast Southern Border, a hundred thousand famed mountains, countless outstanding heroes, there are but a little over a hundred of them.]
- Ch420 raw: "一转、二转蛊师极为常见，三转蛊师已经稀有，到了四转更少，五转蛊师，百万人中只出一两位。" [Rank 1 and Rank 2 Gu Masters are exceedingly common. Rank 3 already rare. Rank 4 rarer still. Rank 5 Gu Masters, among a million people, only one or two emerge.]

**Our fixation:** This is author error in raw. Ch345 says 100 plus Rank 5 in Southern Border, but Ch420 says 1 to 2 per million. Southern Border is 7 to 8 Earths, about 56 to 64 billion people, so Rank 5 should be 56k to 128k, not 100. Also immortals are less than 4 to 5 thousand per region, need 40 million Rank 5 attempts at 1 in 10k rate. We will correct directly in prose where number appears, writing 1,000 to 10,000 Rank 5 per region, or writing "known Rank 5" not total, to keep math consistent.

**Lore correction:** Rank 5 rarity 1 in millions, Rank 6 1 in 10k Rank 5 becomes immortal, total immortals less than 4 to 5 thousand per region, Rank 7 around 200 to 300 per region, Rank 8 around 10 per region.

**What raw says:**

- Ch54 raw: "我虽然只有丁等资质，但是继承了花酒行者的遗藏，说不定就能改变自己的资质。" [Although I possess only D grade aptitude, should I inherit Flower Wine Wanderer's hidden inheritance, perhaps I might yet transform my own aptitude.]
- Ch15 aptitude table raw: "丁等资质，元海占据空窍两三成，往往最高能修行到一转二转。丙等资质，元海是空窍的四五成，通常会停在二转境界，只有很少一部分能侥幸突破到三转初阶。乙等资质，元海占据整个空窍的六七成，能修到三转，甚至四转。甲等资质，元海充足，是空窍的八九成，这样的人自然天赋最高，最适合蛊师修行，能修行到五转。" [D grade aptitude, primeval sea occupies two to three tenths of the aperture. Often its highest attainment is Rank 1 to Rank 2. C grade aptitude, primeval sea is four to five tenths. It typically stalls at Rank 2, only a very small fortunate portion luckily breaking through to initial Rank 3. B grade aptitude, primeval sea occupies six to seven tenths. It can cultivate to Rank 3, even Rank 4. A grade aptitude, primeval sea is abundant, eight to nine tenths. Such a person naturally possesses the highest talent, most suited to Gu Master cultivation, capable of reaching Rank 5.]

**Our fixation:** This is not error, it is plausible and tragic. Raw says D grade canon. Talent raising Gu like Blood Skull Gu exists but requires killing blood relatives, it is demonic, a righteous merchant father would not do it. Also D grade can reach Rank 2 with many primeval stones over long time, per "往往最高能修行到一转二转" which means often highest can cultivate to Rank 1 to Rank 2. So we keep D grade directly in prose, and add small context that raising talent needs demonic methods.

**Lore correction:** Aptitude steps raw Ch13: "十步以下，是没有修行资质。十步到二十步之间，是丁等资质。二十步到三十步之间，是丙等资质。三十步到四十步，是乙等资质。四十步到五十步，是甲等。" [Below ten steps, no cultivation aptitude. Between ten and twenty steps, D grade aptitude. Between twenty and thirty steps, C grade aptitude. Between thirty and forty steps, B grade aptitude. Between forty and fifty steps, A grade.] This translates to 20 to 39 percent, 40 to 59 percent, 60 to 79 percent, 80 to 99 percent for precise implementation.
