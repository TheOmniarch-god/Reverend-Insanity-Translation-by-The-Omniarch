# REVEREND INSANITY (蛊真人)
## Official Synopsis & Premise (内容简介)

> **人是万物之灵，蛊是天地真精。**  
> *Humans are the spirit of all living things; Gu are the true essence of heaven and earth.*

---

### The Synopsis

This is the story of a transmigrator continuously reborn through time.  
A wondrous, bizarre world of raising, refining, and using Gu.  

Spring Autumn Cicada, Moonlight Gu, Liquor Worm, One Qi Golden Light Worm, Black Hair Gu, Hope Gu...  

---

### The Oath

> **雄山漫道真如铁，如今迈步从头越。**  
> **险就一身乾坤精，我心依旧望苍天！**  
>  
> *Say not that the mighty mountains are truly like iron;*  
> *Today, I stride forward and cross them anew.*  
> *Facing peril, I fashion my body from the essence of Heaven and Earth;*  
> *My heart, as ever, gazes at the Heavens.*
