Spring Autumn Cicada 春秋蝉

First Appearance

Seventh among the Ten Great Mystical Gu. Time-path Gu that reverses time. Fang Yuan refines it at cost of ten million lives, detonates himself rather than yield it. Five hundred years collapse into a heartbeat. Chun Qiu Chan — Spring Autumn evokes seasons, history's cycle.

2

Huaxia 华夏

First Appearance

Ancient poetic name for China, not modern political China. Huaxia = central civilized lands, cradle of Tang poetry and Song lyrics Fang Yuan recites. Retaining pinyin preserves cultural marker. First seen in Ch1 when Fang Yuan recalls Earth life.

📖 Ch1 · p11 Also in: Preface, Ch5 Saga of Gu ↗ Encyclopedia ↗
3

Demonic Path / Righteous Path 魔道 / 正道

First Appearance

Mo Dao — to accumulate no good karma, only murder and arson; spurned by heaven and reviled by earth, making enemies of whole world, still running wild following whim of own heart. Zheng Dao — revered sects united, order and law. Not good vs evil — two cultivation philosophies.

📖 Ch1 · p14 Wuxia · Jianghu morality Saga of Gu ↗
4

Aperture Opening Ceremony 开窍大典

First Appearance

Ritual where fifteen-year-olds open aperture — primeval sea within. Gu Zhen Ren writes 两三成 (two-three tenths) not %. We preserve as words: forty-four percent Grade C limit, twenty to thirty-nine percent Grade D, etc. Tolkien tone, no % symbol.
