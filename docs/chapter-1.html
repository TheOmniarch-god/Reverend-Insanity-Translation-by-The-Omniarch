Chapter 1

Though the Flesh Perish, the Demonic Heart Knows No Regret

Volume 1 · Demonic Nature Never Changes · Translator: The Omniarch · 2,171 words · ~10 min read
Typeface
← →

“Fang Yuan! Hand over the Spring Autumn Cicada without struggle, and I shall grant you a quick death!”

“Old Demon Fang, harbour no delusions of defiance! Today, the revered sects of the Righteous Path have united for one purpose: to trample your demonic lair into dust. We have long since laid an inescapable dragnet here. This time, you shall be beheaded!”

“Fang Yuan, you damned devil! To refine that cursed Spring Autumn Cicada, you slaughtered ten million innocents! You have committed towering atrocities, unpardonable and beyond all count!”

“Demon! Three hundred years ago, you violated me and robbed me of my chastity. You killed my entire family and slaughtered all my kin. From that moment on, I’ve wanted nothing more than to devour your flesh and drink your blood! Today, I’ll make you wish you were dead!”

* * *

Fang Yuan stood cloaked in a tattered emerald robe, his hair wild and unkempt, his body bathed in blood, as his eyes swept over the surrounding host. The mountain wind whipped his blood-soaked garments, fluttering and snapping like a war banner. Fresh blood gushed out from the hundreds of wounds across his body. In the brief moment he stood there, a large pool of blood had already gathered at his feet. Besieged on every flank by bitter foes, every path to life had long been severed. The trap had snapped shut; on this day, death was absolute.

Fang Yuan perceived his doom with perfect clarity. Yet even as the shadow of death loomed before him, he remained expressionless, his demeanor unmoved. His gaze was dark and deep, like an ancient well, bottomless as ever. The righteous host besieging him comprised distinguished sect leaders and young heroes renowned far and wide. Yet as they surrounded Fang Yuan, their composure fractured: some bared their teeth in savage roars, some sneered with brittle arrogance, some narrowed their eyes with venomous suspicion, while others clutched bleeding wounds, looking upon him with lingering dread.

None dared to make a move, all feared Fang Yuan's final counterattack before death. Thus they confronted each other tensely for six hours, until the sun set in the west, and the afterglow lit the evening clouds by the mountainside, momentarily resplendent as fire. Fang Yuan, who had stood as motionless as a statue, slowly turned around. That solitary motion sent a convulsion through the host; the multitude recoiled in unison, retreating a full pace in panic. At this moment, the grey-white mountain rocks beneath Fang Yuan’s feet had long been stained a deep, dark red with fresh blood. His face, pale from excessive blood loss, was illuminated by the sunset glow, suddenly taking on an alluring, delicate radiance.

Gazing upon the green mountains and the setting sun, Fang Yuan let slip a soft, low chuckle:

“Green mountains beneath the setting sun; autumn moon and spring breeze.

Truly, in the morning one’s hair is like black silk, by dusk it turns to snow; right and wrong, success and failure, all vanish in the turning of a head.”

As the verse left his lips, memories of his past life on Earth surged unbidden before his eyes. He had once been a student of Huaxia on Earth, cast into this foreign world by a twist of fortune. Three hundred years of wandering and struggle, two hundred years of unbridled dominion, carving his will across the world. More than five centuries, yet in retrospect, gone in the blink of an eye.

Countless memories buried in the depths of his soul awakened, coalescing in his mind with breathtaking clarity.

“In the end, I failed after all,” Fang Yuan sighed in his heart. A trace of wistfulness stirred, yet not the faintest whisper of regret.

This outcome was nothing he had not foreseen; from the moment he made his choice centuries ago, he had been mentally prepared. For what was the Demonic Path? It was to accumulate no good karma, only murder and arson; spurned by heaven and reviled by earth, making enemies of the whole world, and still running wild, following the whim of one’s own heart to the very end.

“Should this Spring Autumn Cicada I have just refined prove effective, I shall yet remain a demon in the life to come!”

Thinking like this, Fang Yuan could not help laughing out loud.

“Old Demon, what are you laughing at?”

“Everyone, be careful! The demon is at death’s door and wants to counterattack!”

“Quickly hand over the Spring Autumn Cicada!”

The righteous host surged forward, greed and panic driving them into frenzy. And in that very breath, with a thunderous blast, Fang Yuan brazenly self-detonated.

* * *

Spring rain fell continuously, silently nourishing Qing Mao Mountain. Night had fallen deep, and threads of cool wind blew through the fine rain. Qing Mao Mountain was not dark, however; from the mountainside down to the foothills, faint, shimmering lights glowed, as if draping a brilliant ribbon of radiance across the slope. These lights came from the stilt houses, numbering not ten thousand households, but several thousand nonetheless. This was Gu Yue Village, nestled upon Qing Mao Mountain, giving the vast, tranquil mountain range a distinct sense of human habitation. At the very heart of Gu Yue Village stood a grand pavilion. Right now a solemn sacrificial ceremony was being held, so it was brightly lit and resplendent.

“Ancestors bless us! May this Aperture Opening Ceremony produce many youths of excellent aptitude to bring fresh blood and hope to our clan!”

The Gu Yue Clan Leader appeared middle-aged, his temples touched with frost. Wearing plain white ceremonial robes, he knelt upon the amber floorboards, his upper body erect, hands pressed together, closing both eyes to pray sincerely. He faced a tall, three-tiered, black-lacquered altar table enshrining the spirit tablets of the ancestors. On both sides of the tablets stood red-copper incense burners, thin plumes of fragrant smoke coiling upward. Behind him, ten-odd figures knelt in matching white sacrificial robes. These were the clan elders and decision-makers, each wielding power over various affairs of the clan.

After concluding his prayers, the Clan Leader was the first to bend down, palms flat against the wooden floorboards, kowtowing. His forehead met the floor with soft, rhythmic thuds. The elders behind him, their expressions solemn, followed suit in silence. For a time, the ancestral hall was filled only with the gentle cadence of foreheads knocking against the floor. When the ceremony concluded, everyone slowly rose from the floor and quietly filed out of the hallowed hall. In the corridor, the elders breathed a quiet sigh of relief, the tense atmosphere easing noticeably.

Murmurs of conversation soon began to rise:

“Time truly flies. In the blink of an eye, another full year has gone by.”

“The previous Aperture Opening Ceremony feels like it happened just yesterday; the memory is still vivid.”

“Tomorrow is the annual Aperture Opening Ceremony. I wonder what kind of fresh blood our clan will produce this year?”

“Sigh, I only hope a youth with Grade A aptitude emerges. It has been three years since our Gu Yue clan had such a genius.”

“Indeed. Both Bai Village and Xiong Village have seen remarkable talents emerge over recent years. In particular, Bai Ning Bing of the Bai clan, his natural talent is truly terrifying.”

Whosoever mentioned the name Bai Ning Bing drew an involuntary shadow of worry across the faces of the clan elders. This youth’s talent was extraordinary. In just two short years, he had already cultivated to Rank III Gu Master, standing unrivaled among the younger generation. Even the older generation felt immense pressure from this rising star. Given time, he would undoubtedly become a pillar of Bai Village, a formidable powerhouse in his own right. No one ever doubted this.

“However, among the youths participating in this year’s Aperture Opening Ceremony, it is not as though we have no hope.”

“Quite right. A genius youth has appeared in the Fang lineage. He could speak at three months and walk at four. By age five, he could compose Tang poetry and Song lyrics, exceptionally intelligent and overflowing with talent. It is just a pity his parents died early, and he is currently being raised by his aunt and uncle.”

“Mm, he possesses precocious wisdom and grand ambitions. In recent years, I have heard of the poems he composed, Bring in the Wine, Ode to the Plum Blossom, and Jiang Cheng Zi. He is truly a genius!”

The Gu Yue Clan Leader was the last to exit the ancestral hall. As he slowly closed the door, the murmurs of the elders reached his ears. He immediately knew that the elders were discussing a youth named Gu Yue Fang Yuan. As the leader of the clan, he naturally paid close attention to outstanding juniors, and Gu Yue Fang Yuan was the most prominent and dazzling among them. Experience had shown that those gifted with photographic memories or strength rivaling an adult from childhood almost always possessed superior cultivation talent.

“If this child tests with Grade A aptitude and is properly nurtured, he may well be able to contend with Bai Ning Bing. Even if he only has Grade B aptitude, he will certainly become a stalwart pillar and a banner for the Gu Yue clan in the future. Yet with such precocious intellect, Grade B is unlikely; it is overwhelmingly probable that he has Grade A.”

With this thought, the corners of the Clan Leader’s lips lifted slightly into a smile. He cleared his throat with a dry cough and addressed the elders: “Everyone, the hour is late. For the sake of tomorrow’s Aperture Opening Ceremony, tonight please be sure to rest well and conserve your energy.” Hearing this, the elders paused slightly. In the glances they exchanged, a subtle trace of vigilance was concealed. The Clan Leader’s words were polite and restrained, but everyone understood their deeper meaning. Every year, in order to recruit talented juniors into their own factions, the elders fought tooth and nail until their faces flushed red and heads bled.

It was indeed time to rest and gather strength, so they could contend fiercely tomorrow. Especially for that Gu Yue Fang Yuan, the probability of him possessing Grade A aptitude was exceptionally high. Moreover, both his parents were deceased, leaving him and his brother as the only surviving orphans of the Fang lineage. If one could bring him into their own faction and groom him well, it could guarantee a century of prosperity for their line!

“However, let me make one thing abundantly clear from the outset. Compete openly and honourably; underhanded schemes that harm clan unity will not be tolerated. Elders, bear this firmly in mind!” the Clan Leader warned sternly.

“We wouldn’t dare, we wouldn’t dare.”

“We shall definitely keep it well in mind.”

“Then we shall take our leave. Lord Clan Leader, please do not trouble yourself to see us off.”

Harbouring their own calculations, the elders dispersed one by one. Before long, the long corridor grew quiet and desolate. A wind-blown spring rain drifted through the window; the Clan Leader stepped lightly towards the window frame. Instantly, a mouthful of fresh, moist mountain air filled his chest, refreshing to the soul. This was the third floor of the attic; the Clan Leader looked out, and more than half of Gu Yue Village could be seen in a single glance. Though it was late at night, lights still burned in most homes in the village, quite unlike usual.

Tomorrow was the Aperture Opening Ceremony, touching upon everyone’s personal interests. An excited and nervous atmosphere covered the clansmen’s hearts, naturally making many sleep restlessly.

“This is the future hope of our clan.” With dots of light reflected in his eyes, the Clan Leader sighed softly.

* * *

At this very moment, another pair of clear eyes quietly gazed upon those twinkling lights in the deep of the night, brimming with complex emotions.

“Gu Yue Village… this is five hundred years ago?! The Spring Autumn Cicada really had an effect…”

Fang Yuan’s gaze was dark and profound as he stood beside the window, letting the wind and rain beat against his body. The effect of the Spring Autumn Cicada was to reverse time. Ranked seventh among the Ten Great Mystical Gu, it was naturally no ordinary existence.

In short: rebirth.

“Reborn using the Spring Autumn Cicada… back to five hundred years ago!”

Fang Yuan reached out his hand, staring intently at his young, tender, and somewhat pale palm. Then he slowly clenched it into a fist, feeling the vivid reality with all his strength. He heard the gentle pitter-patter of drizzle tapping against the window lattice. He closed his eyes slowly, opening them only after a long moment, letting out a wistful sigh:

“Five hundred years of experience… it truly feels like a dream.”

Yet he knew with absolute certainty: this was no dream.
