# Lore Corrections: Mathematical Facts and Implementation

*We, The Omniarch Translation Team*

## How We Are Tackling It

We correct directly in prose where the mistake appears. We use the right number and right term in the translation itself, even with no Translator Note.

If the author writes cavern but the space is actually a small compartment, we write compartment. If the author writes 50,000,000 km, we write 50,000 km. If the author writes 80 to 90 percent for Grade A, we write 80 to 99 percent.

The reader sees correct lore in the story. Detailed calculations live in lore-corrections.md and 100-debates.md for verification.

### 1. Aptitude and Primeval Sea

**Fact:** Primeval sea percentage determines talent and breakthrough.

- D grade: 20 to 39 percent [1]
- C grade: 40 to 59 percent
- B grade: 60 to 79 percent
- A grade: 80 to 99 percent [2]
- Ten Extreme: 100 percent [3]

**Implementation:** 
In Chapter 4, Mo Bei has sea 66 percent. We write 66 percent. That is B grade 60 to 79 percent, so B grade. No TN needed.

In Chapter 6, Fang Yuan has sea 44 percent. We write 44 percent. That is C grade 40 to 59 percent, so C grade limitation. No TN needed.

In Chapter 7, elder says D 20 to 30 percent, C 40 to 50 percent, B 60 to 70 percent, A 80 to 90 percent. Original says eight to nine tenths for A grade. Precise canon is 80 to 99 percent for A grade [2]. We write 20 to 39 percent, 40 to 59 percent, 60 to 79 percent, 80 to 99 percent directly in prose. No TN.

Breakthrough Rank I to Rank II requires 55 percent sea. D grade max 39 percent can never reach it. C grade 40 to 59 percent can if lucky. This explains why Fang Yuan struggles and Fang Zheng is groomed.

Bai Ning Bing reaches Rank III in 2 years. Per Chapter 4, B grade needs 6 to 7 years to Rank III. So 2 years is only possible for A grade 80 to 99 percent or Ten Extreme 100 percent. This establishes Bai Ning Bing as A grade or Ten Extreme, which matches later reveal of Northern Dark Ice Soul Physique.

### 2. Geography Scale

**Fact:** World scale in early fandom was overstated.

- Central Continent: 50,000 km across, not 50,000,000 km. 1,000 times difference.
- Southern Border: 7 to 8 Earths, not 50 suns.
- Northern Plains: 5 billion km², about 10 times Earth (Earth 510 million km²).

**Implementation:**
When Chapter 32 says Central Continent 50,000,000 km wide, we write 50,000 km directly. When it says 50 suns, we write 7 to 8 Earths directly. No TN saying original says 50M. Reader sees correct scale, so Chapter 4 migration from Central Continent to Southern Border stays plausible.

### 3. Timeline and Bottlenecks

**Fact:** 100 year bottleneck is conditional.

- Original says 100 years Rank II to Rank III.
- Later shows Rank III at start and Bai Ning Bing Rank III in 2 years.
- Corrected: 100 years without external aid.

**Implementation:**
When Chapter 150 says 100 years Rank II to Rank III, we write 100 years without external aid directly. With clan resources, primeval stones, and A grade or Ten Extreme talent, 2 years is plausible. So Chapter 4 training times (3 years D to Rank I, 2 years C to Rank II, 6 to 7 years B to Rank III, 10 years A to Rank IV) stay consistent.

### 4. Killer Moves and Multipliers

**Fact:** Many multipliers are rounded exaggerations.

- Original says 10,000 times.
- Precise is 6,400 times.

**Implementation:**
When killer move says 10,000 times, we write 6,400 times directly. No TN. Damage calculations stay consistent later.

### 5. Terminology

**Fact:** Some terms are mistranslated as modern or childish, breaking ancient dark mystical atmosphere.

- Author writes cavern but means small hidden compartment. We write compartment.
- Author writes cubbies for wall spaces in Gu chamber. Cubbies sounds like preschool cloakroom. We write compartments or recessed alcoves.
- Author writes nine turn realms. We write nine major ranks, Rank I to Rank IX Roman.

**Implementation:**
In Chapter 8 p7, four walls have inset spaces. We write recessed alcoves, inset compartments crowding one after another, varied in size, largest no bigger than clay pot, smallest no larger than fist. No TN.

In Chapter 8 p8, vessels sit in compartments. We write Within the tightly packed compartments sat a vast array of vessels: grey stone basins, vibrant green jade dishes, delicate grass cages, and earthenware braziers. Term matches p7, reader connects instantly. No TN.

In Chapter 8 p10, Gu worms sounds. We write Some Gu worms were quiescent and mute, while others were clamorous, emanating a din of chirps, clucks, and rustles that coalesced into a symphony of life. No TN.

### 6. Primeval Essence

**Fact:** Recovery and condensation have fixed formulas.

- C grade recovers 4 percent per hour.
- Formula is 10 percent of aptitude per hour.
- Condensation 4 percent to 1 percent when refining.

**Implementation:**
When Chapter 8 says primeval stones can replenish sea, we write can extract pure primeval essence to replenish sea directly. No TN. Recovery math lives in primeval-calculations.md.
