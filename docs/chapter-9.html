Chapter 9

Drifting Further Apart

Volume 1 · Demonic Nature Never Changes · Translator: The Omniarch · 2134 words · ~10 min read
Typeface
← →

Westward the red sun sank, yet had not wholly set.

The sky still held a little light, yet all things seemed veiled in grey. Through the window, the distant mountains could be seen slowly sinking into a deepening, thickening darkness.

Within the living room, the light was dim. Uncle and Aunt sat high upon the seats of honour, their faces and expressions shrouded in shadow, scarcely to be discerned.

Seeing the two jars of wine Fang Yuan had brought, Uncle Gu Yue Dong Tu's brow furrowed deeply. He said, “How time flies, you are both fifteen already. You both possess Gu Master aptitude, Fang Zheng especially. Your aunt and I are proud of you both. I have set aside six primeval stones for each of you. Take them, brothers. Refining Gu worms consumes primeval essence at a great rate. You shall have need of these stones.”

As he spoke, a servant came forward and handed each brother a small pouch.

Fang Yuan tucked the pouch away, saying nothing.

Fang Zheng at once loosened the drawstring and looked inside. Within lay six oval, grey-white primeval stones, translucent, the size of a duck's egg. His face flushed with gratitude. He stood and bowed to Uncle and Aunt. “Thank you, Uncle and Aunt. Your nephew is indeed in need of primeval stones to replenish his primeval essence. You raised your nephew to this age, and your kindness in raising him is engraved upon his heart, never to be forgotten.”

Uncle smiled and nodded.

Aunt hastily waved her hands and said gently to Fang Zheng, “Quick, sit, sit. Though you two were not born of us, we have always raised you as our own sons. That you can amount to something, we too feel proud. Alas, we have no children of our own. At times we think how fine it would be if you could truly become our children.”

These words carried a deep meaning. Fang Zheng did not catch it, but Fang Yuan faintly furrowed his brow.

Sure enough, Uncle went on, “Aunt and I have discussed it. We wish to adopt you into our household, to become a true family. Fang Zheng, are you willing?”

Fang Zheng was taken aback, but swiftly joy flooded his face and he agreed at once. “To speak truly, since my parents died, your nephew has longed for a family of his own. To become one family with Uncle and Aunt, nothing could be better.”

Aunt's expression eased and she laughed. “Then you are our good son. Do you still call us Uncle and Aunt?”

“Father, Mother.” Fang Zheng understood and quickly corrected himself.

Uncle and Aunt both laughed heartily.

“Good son, not in vain have we raised you since you were five, a full ten years,” Aunt said, wiping tears from her eyes.

Uncle then turned his gaze upon the silent Fang Yuan. “Fang Yuan, what about you?”

Fang Yuan shook his head and said nothing.

“Brother,” Fang Zheng began, wishing to persuade him, but Uncle deterred him.

Uncle's tone did not change. He said again, “Since it is so, nephew Fang Yuan, we will not force you. But you are already fifteen; you ought to set up your own household. Thus it will be convenient for you to inherit your branch of the Fang lineage. Uncle has prepared two hundred primeval stones, as support for you.”

“Two hundred primeval stones!” Fang Zheng's eyes widened at once. He had never seen so many primeval stones, and could not help showing envy.

Yet Fang Yuan still shook his head.

Fang Zheng was greatly puzzled. Uncle's face changed faintly, and Aunt's face darkened as well.

“Uncle, Aunt, if there is nothing else, your nephew will take his leave first.” Fang Yuan afforded them no chance to speak again. Leaving these words behind, he lifted his wine jars and walked straight out of the hall.

Fang Zheng stood up. “Father, Mother, brother cannot think it through for the moment. Why not let me persuade him?”

Uncle waved his hand and heaved a deliberate sigh. “Alas, this matter cannot be forced. That you should have such a heart, as your father I am already greatly comforted. Someone, take young master Fang Zheng down and settle him properly.”

“Then your son shall take his leave.” Fang Zheng withdrew, and the hall fell into silence.

The sun sank wholly behind the mountains, and the hall grew ever more gloomy.

After a long while, Uncle's cold voice came from the gloom. “It seems this little bastard Fang Yuan has seen through our scheme.”

Among Gu Yue Clan rules, there is a clear provision: the eldest son at sixteen possesses the qualification to inherit family property.

Fang Yuan's parents had already passed away, leaving behind a not inconsiderable inheritance, all of it being kept by Uncle and Aunt.

The value of this inheritance was not to be compared with a mere two hundred primeval stones.

If Fang Yuan were also adopted by Uncle and Aunt like Fang Zheng, he would have no qualification to inherit this inheritance. If Fang Yuan established his own household at fifteen this year, he would likewise not conform to the clan's regulation for inheritance.

“Fortunately, we have won over Fang Zheng, and Fang Yuan has only Grade C aptitude.” Uncle sighed again, feeling immense relief.

“Then my lord, Fang Yuan plainly intends to go independent at sixteen, what ought we to do?” Aunt's voice grew urgent at the thought of that inheritance.

“Hmph, since he harbours ill intent, he can hardly blame us. So long as, before he goes independent, we seize on a grave fault of his and expel him from the household, we will strip him of his right to the inheritance.” Uncle snorted coldly.

“But this little bastard Fang Yuan is very clever, how would he commit a fault?” Aunt did not understand.

Uncle at once rolled his eyes and rebuked in a low voice, “How stupid you are! He will not commit a fault; can we not frame him? Let that girl Shen Cui lure Fang Yuan first, then cry out that he has violated her. We catch them red-handed in the act, then slander him with drunken debauchery, a crime of utter depravity. Are you afraid we cannot expel Fang Yuan?”

“My lord, you still have a method, a brilliant stratagem!” Aunt was overjoyed at once.

• • •

A dense night descended, the stars, mostly veiled by drifting clouds, filling the sky. In the stockade, household after household gradually lit their lamps.

Gu Yue Fang Zheng was led into a room.

“Young Master Fang Zheng, this is a room this old servant specially tidied and vacated upon my lord's personal instruction.” Granny Shen introduced it obsequiously, bowing her back, her face wreathed with flattering smiles.

Fang Zheng looked round, his eyes brightening. This room was more than twice as large as his former room. At its centre was a broad bed; by the window, a desk of sandalwood set with a fine brush, ink, paper and inkstone; the surrounding walls hung with exquisite ornaments. Even beneath his feet was not ordinary flooring, but a layer of soft, hand-made carpet.

From childhood to now, Fang Zheng had never lived in such a room. At once he nodded repeatedly. “This is very good, really quite fine. Thank you, Granny Shen.”

This Granny Shen was Aunt's most trusted person, managing all the servants in the household, a housekeeper in all but name.

Fang Yuan's personal maid Shen Cui was her daughter.

Granny Shen chuckled. “How could this old servant dare accept young master's thanks, it is as it should be, as it should be. Young master need only eat well and sleep well, if you want anything, shake the bell by the bedside, a servant will come up at once to await your orders. My lord has instructed that in these days young master should devote himself wholly to cultivation; other trifling matters should be left to us servants to handle.”

A fresh surge of gratitude rose in Fang Zheng's heart. He said nothing more, only silently resolved within himself: “I must seize first place, and not disappoint Uncle and Aunt!”

• • •

The clouds above thickened, and with them the night deepened. The stars were all but veiled, save for a few that still glimmered faintly, struggling against the darkness.

“Uncle and Aunt should be plotting how to expel me from the household. In the previous life they secretly instigated servants to provoke me, then framed me, and in the end expelled me. I wonder what change there will be in this life.” Fang Yuan walked along the street, sneering coldly within his heart.

As for the true colours of Uncle and Aunt, he had seen through them long ago.

Yet it could be understood.

Man dies for wealth as birds die for food, whether on Earth or in this world, there are always those who trample upon kinship, friendship and love for the sake of benefit.

In truth, there was no kinship at all. Uncle and Aunt had taken the brothers in for no other reason than coveting the inheritance. Only the two brothers had repeatedly surprised them.

“All things are difficult at the beginning, and for me doubly so. I possess neither outstanding aptitude nor the guidance of any elder. In truth, I am starting from scratch. My parents' inheritance may yet serve as a great springboard. In the previous life, Uncle and Aunt seized that inheritance, and for that loss I squandered two whole years before I could cultivate to the peak of Rank one. In this life, that error shall not be repeated.”

So he went his way, pondering as he walked.

He did not remain in his lodgings, but, carrying two jars of wine, headed straight for the outside of the stockade.

The night sky grew ever more overcast, dark clouds veiling starlight, the mountain wind whistling with a rising howl, ever stronger.

Mountain rain was about to break.

Still, he must explore. His parents' inheritance could only be reclaimed when he was sixteen next year, while Flower Wine Monk's legacy was something that might be obtained in the near term.

In the street, few folk were about. From houses by the roadside, dim light shone; some household refuse, leaves, and dust rolled along, blown by the wind, drifting aimlessly.

Fang Yuan's thin clothes could scarcely hold back this mountain wind, and he could not help but feel a chill.

He simply opened one of the jars he carried and took a small sip. Though it was turbid wine, once swallowed, warmth rose within him.

This was the first time in all these days that he had truly drunk at all.

The further from the stockade, the sparser the houses by the road, the dimmer the lights.

Ahead, gloom lay thicker. Wind pressed upon the mountain forest, branches swaying in the night, whistling as though beasts roared.

Fang Yuan's steps did not falter. He passed beyond the stockade gate and went on, his figure receding further and further into the dark road.

Behind him lay the myriad lights of home, bright and glorious.

Amid those lights there was a warm corner.

There, brother Fang Zheng sat at his desk, reviewing the notes taken in class. The room was bright with lamplight, solid walls keeping out the cold wind, and at his hand stood a cup of warm ginseng tea, steam curling and rising.

“Young Master Fang Zheng, the hot water for your bath is ready,” came Shen Cui's voice softly from outside the door.

Fang Zheng's heart stirred. “Then bring it in.”

Shen Cui entered, sashaying as she came, her face full of coquettish charm.

“This servant pays respects to Young Master Fang Zheng.” She gazed upon Fang Zheng with amorous eyes. Fang Yuan was only Grade C, but Fang Zheng was of Grade A aptitude. To latch onto him, that would be truly great fortune!
