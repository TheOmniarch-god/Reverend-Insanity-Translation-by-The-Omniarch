# Chapter 7 — Balanced Erudite Final (workspace)

> Over these past few days, he had bought a lot of wine and wandered idly around the village every night. He wanted to use the **redolence** of the wine to lure the Liquor Worm into showing itself.

> A Liquor Worm is a very precious kind of Gu. Still flushed with wine, that Gu Master risked entering the cave and reached a **clandestine chamber**.

> Under blue sky and white cloud, a **verdant massif** stretched away without end. Close by lay a bamboo grove.

> This was the spear bamboo found only on Qing Mao Mountain, each stalk straight as a line, its tip sharp as a spearhead.

> Not far off, the woods had come into new leaf. Tender shoots had just opened, a pale wash of **chartreuse**, where **plumaged** finches perched among the branches from time to time.

> A spring wind blew in, carrying the clean scent of green hills and clear water.

**Rule applied:** Erudite is allowed, stacking is not.
- OLD: luxuriant (lush) + verdant (lush) + massif = 3 for 1 mountain
- OLD: luteovirescent (yellow-green) + chartreuse (yellow-green) + expanse = 2 colours + noun
- NEW: one erudite per image — redolence / clandestine / verdant massif / chartreuse / plumaged
