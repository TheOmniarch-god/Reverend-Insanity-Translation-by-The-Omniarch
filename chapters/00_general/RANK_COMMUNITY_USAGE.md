# Rank = 转 — Community Usage, Mulan Shi, and What It Actually Is

## The Problem You Raised
"No one instinctively thinks rank equals revolutions" — Correct. In English, Rank = hierarchy, Revolution = circular movement. But Chinese 转 is both, and community usage is layered.

## 1. What Hanzi Is It?

**转 / 轉** — zhuǎn (verb: to turn, change direction, transfer) / zhuàn (verb: to revolve, to circle; noun/measure: revolution, turn, classifier for revolutions, for repeated actions, for ancient rank promotions)

From EPUB source:
- 一转蛊师, 二转蛊师, 九转, 一转巅峰 — all 转

## 2. Mulan Shi — 策勋十二转 — Does It Relate?

**Original:** 归来见天子，天子坐明堂。策勋十二转，赏赐百千强。

**Community explanations (Baidu, Zhidao, Wikipedia, Zhihu, Sina, Peking University professor Yan Buke):**

- **Baidu Baike 勋官十二转:** 唐朝军功勋级制度，最高一阶上柱国正二品需十二转，最低武骑尉从七品只需一转。"转"是授予勋官时用来衡量功绩的单位。 [baike.baidu.com/item/勋官十二转]

- **Zhihu:** "十二转并不是一般网上解释的需要用十二本书记录；根据《唐六典》，对于有军功武官有十二个等级，或者说十二个评分标准，其中一分被称作一转，按照现在的话说有点像绩效或者工分。" — 12 zhuan is 12 grading standards, one fen called one zhuan, like performance score. [zhihu.com/question/309221832]

- **Wikipedia 勋官:** 唐朝文武勋官统一通授，分十二级，每升一级称"转"。《木兰诗》"策勋十二转"即谓军功极大，升至最高等勋位上柱国。 [zh.wikipedia.org/zh-hans/勋官]

- **Baidu Zhidao:** 转相当于量词"次"。古时军功加一等，官爵升一级，叫做一转。 [zhidao.baidu.com]

- **Xinhua Dictionary / Zdic:** 量词，古代勋位每升一级称"一转"："策勋十二转，赏赐百千强。" [zd.hwxnet.com] ; 量词。等于说"次"。古时军功每加一等，则官爵随升一级，叫做一转 [zdic.net]

- **Sina history article:** 凡以军功授勋的，战场上由书记员记录战前情况、过程、胜负、杀死俘虏数字，上报吏部司勋郎中审查，拟定官阶。按照战前条件（上阵/中阵/下阵）和结果（上获/中获/下获），综合拟定转数。上阵上获为五转，上阵中获为四转... [k.sina.com.cn]

- **Peking University Prof Yan Buke:** 唐宋勋官上柱国最高正二品，最低骑都尉七品。唐代授勋依据上阵中阵下阵、上获中获下获、个人表现。上阵上获第一等给五转。木兰诗"策勋十二转"说木兰赢得最高级勋官第十二转上柱国，所以至少这两句一定是唐代写成的，因为是唐前期才有这种制度。 [edp.his.pku.edu.cn]

**Mulan list (Baike):**
- 12转 上柱国 视正二品
- 11转 柱国 从二品
- 10转 上护军 正三品
- ...
- 1转 武骑尉 从七品

**Does Mulan relate to Reverend Insanity?**
- Directly? No — Mulan is military merit, Gu Zhen Ren is cultivation.
- Indirectly? Yes — author borrowed the **measure word usage**: ancient 转 = rank promotion. So using 转 for cultivation ranks feels natural to Chinese readers because they already know 转 = rank from Mulan textbook (taught in middle school). Every Chinese student memorizes "策勋十二转".
- Also second historical usage: **道家炼丹的次数叫转。《抱朴子·内篇·金丹》："九转之丹。"** — Daoist alchemy, number of times refining pill called zhuan, nine-turn pill. [zdic.net] — This is even closer to cultivation: refining Gu, refining essence, nine transformations.

So Mulan gives the "rank" sense, Daoist alchemy gives the "transformation/revolution" sense. Gu Zhen Ren merges both.

## 3. Chinese Community — How Do They Actually Write/Understand 一转二转 in 蛊真人?

**Tieba question: 蛊真人里边的蛊虫一转二转什么意思** — "What does one zhuan two zhuan Gu worm mean in Gu Zhen Ren?" [tieba.baidu.com]
- Community answers treat it as **境界/等级/层次** — realm/level/layer, not literally "revolution". They explain it as cultivation realm, like Grade.

**Zhihu: 《蛊真人》的境界是怎么设计的？**
- Answer: "蛊师境界分为一到九转，每一转分为前期，中期，后期，巅峰四个小境界。一到五转为凡人，六转以上为蛊仙" — Gu Master realm divided into 1-9 zhuan, each zhuan 4 small realms, 1-5 mortal, 6+ immortal. [zhihu.com/question/8020787668]
- They explain difference by 真元 quality: 一转青铜, 二转赤铁, 三转白银, 四转黄金, 五转紫晶, each 10x quality. So community understands 转 as **大境界** (major realm) distinguished by essence quality.

**Baidu Baike 蛊 (novel):**
- "转数之分：一切蛊虫皆可升级，所谓转数不可尽信，只取文中初次出现时蛊虫的转数。（一到五转为凡蛊，六转到十转为仙蛊）" — Zhuan number division: all Gu worms can upgrade, so-called zhuan number not fully trustworthy, only take initial appearance zhuan number. (1-5 mortal Gu, 6-10 immortal Gu) [baike.baidu.com/item/蛊/13877051]
- "丁等资质...最高能修行到一转二转。丙等...停在二转...乙等...能修到三转甚至四转。甲等...能修行到五转。" — Grade D aptitude at most cultivate to 1-2 zhuan, C stops at 2 zhuan, B to 3-4 zhuan, A to 5 zhuan. Community writes it as **修行到X转** — cultivate to X zhuan, i.e., reach X rank.

**Steam Community Guide (Chinese):**
- Quotes original: "蛊师一共有九大境界，从下到上，分别是一转、二转、三转直至九转。每一转大境界中又分初阶、中阶、高阶、巅峰四个小境界。" — Gu Masters have nine major realms, from low to high, respectively 1 zhuan, 2 zhuan...9 zhuan. Each major realm divided into 4 small realms. [steamcommunity.com]
- Also: "一转蛊师是青铜真元，二转蛊师有赤铁真元，三转蛊师是白银真元。一份赤铁真元，可比十份的青铜真元。" — 1 zhuan bronze essence, 2 zhuan steel essence, 3 zhuan silver essence, 1 steel = 10 bronze.

**Community writing pattern:**
- They write "一转蛊师", "二转蛊师", "三转蛊师", "晋升二转", "一转巅峰", "二转初阶" — always **number + 转 + noun** or **verb + number + 转**.
- They rarely write "一转" alone as "one revolution" — they write it as **境界/修为** — realm/cultivation. Example: "他已经是二转修为" — he already is 2 zhuan cultivation.
- When explaining to newcomers, they say "转就是境界/等级" — zhuan is realm/level.

**So what does Chinese community instinctively think?**
- Not "revolution" as English speaker would picture wheel turning.
- They think **"境界" (realm) / "等级" (grade) / "层次" (layer)** — a major cultivation stage, with essence transformation.
- But they also know from Mulan textbook that 转 historically = rank promotion, so Rank translation feels natural, not strange.
- The "revolution" literal sense is background knowledge, not front-of-mind. Front-of-mind is "level".

## 4. What Is Rank Actually? Realization

**Rank = 转 = BOTH:**

- **Surface (community):** A cultivation **realm/level** — 1-9 major realms, each 4 small stages, distinguished by primeval essence quality (copper/steel/silver/gold/crystal) and aperture wall (light film/water film/ice film/crystal film). Chinese readers write "一转蛊师" meaning "a Gu Master of the first realm".

- **Literal etymology:** **Turn/Revolution/Transformation** — one complete turning/transformation of essence and aperture. Like Daoist alchemy 九转之丹 (nine-turn pill) where each turn is a refinement/transformation.

- **Historical classifier:** **Ancient rank promotion unit** — from Tang 勋官 system, one zhuan = one promotion, twelve zhuan = highest honor 上柱国. This is why Mulan's "十二转" means highest merit. Author chose 转 because Chinese readers already associate it with rank from school.

**Why no one instinctively thinks Rank = Revolution in English?**
- Because English "Rank" lost the "turn" sense. Chinese 转 kept both: physical turn + abstract rank. In English we split into two words.
- Chinese community doesn't think "revolution" either — they think "realm" — but they understand the transformation sense via Daoist alchemy context (九转金丹 is common in xianxia).

**Final Realization for Translation:**

- Keep **"Rank"** as English term for 转 in Gu Master/Gu worm hierarchy — it's what Chinese community means when they say 一转二转 (they mean Rank 1, Rank 2), and it's historically accurate via Mulan.
- But add lore note: **转 (zhuàn) literally = turn, revolution, transformation; historically a measure word for rank promotion (Mulan's 十二转 = 12 promotions to 上柱国) and for Daoist alchemical refinements (九转金丹 = nine-turn pill); in Reverend Insanity it means a major transformative realm where primeval essence qualitatively changes (10x quality per turn).**

So Rank is Turn is Realm is Transformation — one concept with three layers.
