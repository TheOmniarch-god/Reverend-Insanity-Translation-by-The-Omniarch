# P2 Research: Darkness vs Black — 沉重的黑色

**Chinese:** 远处的山，正渐渐地向沉重的黑色靠拢。
**User question:** Should be darkness not black/blackness debate — what do I think

**Answer:** Darkness is correct literary choice.

**Evidence:**

1. 黑色 definition includes both colour absence and light absence:
   - 百度百科 黑色: 既可以是缺少光造成的（漆黑的夜晚），也可以是色光被吸收而造成的 [baike.baidu.com/item/黑色/68106]
   - So 沉重的黑色 = heavy blackness that is both colour + night void

2. Literary Chinese usage for mountains at dusk:
   - "黑沉沉的夜，仿佛无边的浓墨涂抹在天际，连星星的微光也没有" [zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/131345249]
   - "群峰溶进了黑色的夜幕里" — peaks dissolve into black night curtain [same]
   - This is darkness dissolving, not paint.

3. 灰色 veil context:
   - 灰蒙蒙: 天阴沉沉的，灰暗的云朵 [zhidao.baidu.com/question/609961342.html]
   - So scene: grey veil over all things, mountains sinking into heavy thickening darkness = natural progression grey → black → night

**Peak decision:** 
- Before-Leo: heavy, thickening darkness (you liked)
- Final: slowly sinking into a heavy, thickening darkness
- Keeps both literal (black) and literary (darkness as void)

**Why not black alone:** Black as noun adjective feels colour swatch, darkness feels atmospheric, Tolkien-esque.

**Glossary lock:** 沉重的黑色 = heavy thickening darkness (for all similar dusk scenes)
