# Chapter 9 Punctuation Audit — Sentence by Sentence

**Method:** Every sentence examined for comma splices, introductory commas, vocative commas, dialogue punctuation, absolute phrases, British conventions, 0 em dash.

## Fixes Applied (Sentence by Sentence)

### Para 1
- Original: Westward the red sun sank, yet had not wholly set.
- Fix: OK — comma before yet joining contrasting clauses, period final. No change.

### Para 2
- S1: The sky still held a little light, yet all things seemed veiled in grey.
  - OK — comma before yet, period.
- S2: Through the window, the distant mountains could be seen slowly sinking into a heavy, thickening darkness.
  - OK — comma after introductory "Through the window,". "heavy, thickening" comma between cumulative adjectives optional but acceptable. Period.

### Para 3
- S1 Original: Within the living room the light was dim.
- Fix: Within the living room, the light was dim.
  - Added comma after introductory prepositional phrase per British standard for clarity.
- S2: Uncle and Aunt sat high upon the seats of honour, their faces and expressions shrouded in shadow, scarcely to be discerned.
  - OK — comma before absolute phrase "their faces...", comma before "scarcely to be discerned" participial. Period.

### Para 4 (Dialogue heavy)
- S1: At the sight of the two jars of wine Fang Yuan had brought with him, Uncle's brow furrowed.
  - OK — comma after long introductory phrase, apostrophe correct, period.
- S2: He said, “How time slips by.
  - OK — comma after said, opening curly quote, capital H, period inside quote for first sentence of dialogue.
- S3 inside quote: You are fifteen already.
  - OK — period inside.
- S4 inside quote: That you both possess Gu Master aptitude, and Fang Zheng in particular, Aunt and I are proud.
  - OK — comma before and joining clauses, commas around parenthetical "and Fang Zheng in particular,".
- S5 Original: I have set aside six primeval stones for each of you, brothers, take them.
  - FIX: I have set aside six primeval stones for each of you. Brothers, take them.
  - Reason: Comma splice — two independent clauses "I have set aside..." and "take them" joined by comma. Split into two sentences. Vocative comma "Brothers," correct.
- S6 Original: Refining Gu worms devours primeval essence at a great rate, you will need them.
  - FIX: Refining Gu worms devours primeval essence at a great rate, so you will need them.
  - Reason: Comma splice without conjunction. Added "so" as coordinating conjunction. Alternative semicolon, but "so" shows cause-effect.
