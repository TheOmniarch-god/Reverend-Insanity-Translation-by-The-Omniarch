# Chapter 7 — Purple / Erudite Audit

## OLD (highfalutin — before fix)

> Over these past few days, he had bought a lot of wine and wandered idly around the village every night. He wanted to use the **redolence** of the wine to lure the Liquor Worm into showing itself. Unfortunately, there was no sign of the creature at all, and the result was deeply disappointing.

> A Liquor Worm is a very precious kind of Gu. Still flushed with wine, that Gu Master risked entering the cave and reached a **clandestine chamber**. There, he discovered the skeletal remains of the Flower-Wine Monk, along with the inheritance left behind.

> After the Gu Master returned to the village and reported all his findings, it immediately caused a **massive sensation** throughout the entire clan.

> Under the blue sky and white clouds, a **luxuriant verdant massif** stretched endlessly into the distance. In the proximity lay a bamboo forest.

> This was the spear bamboo unique to Qing Mao Mountain, each one as straight as a line, its tip exceptionally sharp, just like a spearhead.

> Not far away, the woods had already taken on a flush of **luteovirescent green**. The newly sprouted tender shoots formed a sprawling, **chartreuse expanse**, where **beautifully plumaged** birds would perch upon the branches from time to time.

> The spring wind blew in, carrying the fresh air of **verdant mountains and clear waters** and scattering it across the world.

**Problems:**
- `luxuriant verdant` = stacked synonyms (both = lush green)
- `massif` = geology textbook for 山
- `luteovirescent` = luteo (yellow) + virescent (green) — Latin erudite for 黄绿
- `chartreuse` = French yellow-green, erudite repeat of same
- `beautifully plumaged birds` = thesaurus for 彩雀儿 (colourful finches)
- `redolence` = erudite for 酒香 (wine scent)
- `clandestine` = erudite for 暗洞 (hidden cave)
- `In the proximity` = stilted for 附近 (nearby)

## NEW (balanced — literary but not purple)

> Over these past few days, he had bought a lot of wine and wandered idly around the village every night. He wanted to use the **scent** of the wine to lure the Liquor Worm into showing itself. Unfortunately, there was no sign of the creature at all, and the result was deeply disappointing.

> A Liquor Worm is a very precious kind of Gu. Still flushed with wine, that Gu Master risked entering the cave and reached a **hidden chamber**. There, he discovered the skeletal remains of the Flower-Wine Monk, along with the inheritance left behind.

> After the Gu Master returned to the village and reported all his findings, it immediately caused a **great stir** throughout the entire clan.

> Under blue sky and white cloud, **forested ridges** stretched away without end. Close by lay a **bamboo grove**.

> This was the spear bamboo found only on Qing Mao Mountain, each stalk straight as a line, its tip sharp as a spearhead.

> Not far off, the woods had **come into new leaf**. Tender shoots had just opened, a **pale wash of green touched with yellow**, where **bright-coloured finches** perched among the branches from time to time.

> A spring wind blew in, carrying the **clean scent of green hills and clear water**.

**Why this is middle ground:**
- Not dry literal: `come into new leaf` / `pale wash of green touched with yellow` / `bright-coloured finches` = English literary idioms, not `fresh green` flat
- Not purple: no Latin `luteovirescent/chartreuse/massif/redolence/clandestine`
- British: `coloured`, `realise` etc kept, no em dashes
- Close to raw: 新绿 = new leaf, 黄绿 = green touched with yellow, 彩雀儿 = bright-coloured finches, 酒香 = scent, 青山绿水 = green hills and clear water
