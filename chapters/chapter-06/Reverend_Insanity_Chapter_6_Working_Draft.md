## The title

Working with **The Road to the Future Will Be Exciting**, but this is still open.

精彩 means splendid, brilliant, spectacular. It is a word of praise, not of understatement. It lands in three places: the title, L46, and L60 where Fang Yuan says it out loud to his brother. One English word should carry all three.

**Exciting** holds in all three. **Interesting** is a genuinely good villain word, because in English it is the classic way of damning something with faint praise, and a villain saying it about a boy he intends to use is quietly horrible. But it costs two things. It is not what 精彩 means, and it collapses at L46, where what makes a life interesting is a much smaller thought than the line is reaching for.

So: interesting is the better menace, exciting is the better translation, and exciting is the one that survives all three positions.

On the shape, 未来的路 is the road of the future, meaning the road ahead. **The Road to the Future** reads a hair more like a road leading toward a destination, but it is idiomatic and it sounds better.

## On heart

Han Dian's entry for 心 lists the organ, then the inner self, then this, which is the part that matters: the ancients treated the heart as the organ of thought, so the word came to stand in for the mind. It quotes Mencius, the office of the heart is to think.

So 心 is not automatically heart. The rule being worked to:

- thought, attention, judgment, so 心神 at L11 and 心中 used for reckoning, becomes **mind** or disappears into the verb
- feeling, resolve, courage, so 心存希望 at L45, stays **heart**
- where English already puts the feeling in the person, the organ goes, so *Fang Yuan was utterly calm* at L41 rather than his heart was calm

Applied at L11, L41, L45, L54, L59, L62. 心 survives at L42 and L45, where hope held in the heart is the legend's own image and the chapter's theme.

Source: [Han Dian, 心](https://www.zdic.net/hans/%E5%BF%83)

### L00

> 空窍玄妙异常，虽然寄托在方源体内，但是却不和五脏六腑同处一个空间。你可以说它无限大，又可以说它无限小。

**Rough:** The aperture is strange and profound beyond the ordinary. Although it is lodged inside Fang Yuan's body, it does not sit in the same space as the five zang and six fu organs. You can call it infinitely big, you can call it infinitely small.

**Draft:** The aperture was a thing of surpassing mystery. Though it was lodged within Fang Yuan's body, it did not share the same space as his internal organs. You could call it infinitely vast, and you could just as well call it infinitely small.

五脏六腑 is the Chinese medical inventory of organs. Spelling it out in English turns a throwaway phrase into a lecture, so I let it mean what it means in use, the internal organs. ### L01

> 有人称之为紫府，有人称之为华池。不过更多人称之为元海空窍。

**Rough:** Some call it Purple Palace, some call it Flowery Pool. But more call it the Primeval Sea Aperture.

**Draft:** Some called it the Purple Palace. Others called it the Flowery Pool. Most, however, called it the Primeval Sea Aperture.

Both names are borrowed Daoist vocabulary rather than invented ones. A Chinese reader hears two old cultivation words being offered as alternatives. That settles their provisional status for me. They stay capitalised.

### L05

> 海水平滑如镜，呈现一片碧青之色，却又浓稠无比，带着铜的光泽。

**Rough:** The seawater is smooth as a mirror, showing an expanse of jade-green, yet thick beyond compare, carrying the lustre of copper.

**Draft:** The water lay smooth as a mirror, a sheet of jade green, yet viscous beyond any water, and it carried the sheen of bronze.

You were right on viscosity. 浓稠 has no headword of its own in Han Dian, but the two characters are 浓, dense or concentrated, and 稠, thick in the sense of not flowing freely. Thick in English is ambiguous, it can mean deep or opaque. Viscous is the actual property, and it matters, because the reader needs to know this is not water. 铜 on its own is copper, but the next line names the substance bronze, so I took the sheen as bronze to keep the two lines agreeing.

### L13

> “不能再走了吗？”学堂家老抱着万分之一的希望，隔岸喊道。

**Rough:** 'Can you go no further?' The academy elder, holding on to a one ten-thousandth hope, shouted from across the bank.

**Draft:** “Can you go no further?” The academy elder called from across the river, still clinging to that one chance in ten thousand.

My earlier reason for keeping the number was bad and you caught it. Saying the clan's hopes are the chapter's subject does not tell you anything about why a numeral should survive, and I should not have dressed it up that way. Here is the actual reason, which is narrower. 万分之一 is a stock intensifier, exactly like your one in a million, and it means vanishingly small rather than a real probability. English has the same move available, so nothing is lost by keeping a numeral, and the alternative, the faintest hope, quietly drops a figure the author did write. That is the whole case. If you would rather have the faintest hope, it costs us very little.

### *Chapter 6: The Road to the Future Will Be Exciting*

The aperture was a thing of surpassing mystery. Though it was lodged within Fang Yuan's body, it did not share the same space as his internal organs. You could call it infinitely vast, and you could just as well call it infinitely small.

Some called it the Purple Palace. Others called it the Flowery Pool. Most, however, called it the Primeval Sea Aperture.

The aperture as a whole was spherical. White light flowed across its surface, forming a membrane of light. This was the Hope Gu from before, burst apart and condensed into a single layer of light.

It was precisely this membrane of light that held the aperture up and kept it from collapsing.

Within the aperture, naturally, lay the primeval sea.

The water lay smooth as a mirror, a sheet of jade green, yet viscous beyond any water, and it carried the sheen of bronze.

This was the bronze primeval essence that only a Rank 1 Gu Master possessed, condensed together. Commonly, it was called the bronze sea.

The surface of the sea did not reach half the height of the aperture. It stood at only forty-four percent. This too was the limit of a Grade C aptitude.

Every drop of that water was primeval essence. It represented the condensing of Fang Yuan's essence, qi and spirit, and it stood for the vital potential he had accumulated over fifteen years.

It was with this primeval essence that a Gu Master drove his Gu worms. Which was to say, from this moment on, Fang Yuan had formally stepped into the ranks of Rank 1 Gu Masters.

The aperture was open. No more Hope Gu flowed into Fang Yuan's body.

Fang Yuan withdrew his attention. He felt only that the pressure ahead was as thick and solid as a wall, and that he could not advance another step.

“Just as in my previous life.” He gave a faint, indifferent smile at the result.

“Can you go no further?” The academy elder called from across the river, still clinging to that one chance in ten thousand.

Fang Yuan simply turned and walked back, answering him by action.

At that, even the youths finally caught on.

At once there was a rising buzz, and the crowd erupted.

“What? Fang Yuan walked twenty-seven steps?”

“So he only has a Grade C aptitude?!”

“Unbelievable. A genius like him, only a Grade C?”

An uproar swept through the crowd.

“Brother...” In the crowd, Gu Yue Fang Zheng raised his head and stared in shock at Fang Yuan wading back across the river. He could not believe the scene before him. His own elder brother was only a Grade C?

He had always believed his elder brother would be a Grade A aptitude.

No, not only he. His uncle and aunt, and many others in the clan, had believed the same.

And yet now, this was the result!

“Damn it. Only a Grade C!” The Gu Yue clan leader clenched his fists in secret and let out a deep sigh, his disappointment plain beyond any words.

Among the elders watching in secret, some knitted their brows, some lowered their heads in discussion, and some tipped their heads back and let out a long sigh.

“Could there be an error in the test?”

“How could it be? The method is accurate beyond compare, and with us watching throughout, even cheating would be difficult.”

“But then how do you explain his earlier performance, and his talent?”

“The higher a youth's primeval sea aptitude, the more he will indeed display qualities beyond the ordinary. Intelligence, comprehension, memory, strength, agility and so on. But the reverse does not hold. Those qualities do not mean the aptitude must be high. In the end, the test result is what decides.”

“Haa. The greater the hope, the greater the disappointment. The Gu Yue clan grows worse with every generation.”

* * *

The icy river water soaked through his socks, cold enough to bite at the bone.

Fang Yuan walked on, his face as expressionless as ever. The distance closed, and he could see clearly the heavy look on the academy elder's face, and could feel keenly the gazes of more than a hundred youths turned upon him.

In those gazes there was astonishment, there was shock, there was mockery, there was delight at his misfortune, there was sudden understanding, and there was cold indifference.

The scene was identical, and it drew Fang Yuan's thoughts back to his previous life.

Back then it had felt as though the sky itself were falling. Crossing the river he had lost his footing and fallen, soaked to the skin, his wits scattered. And not one person had reached out to steady him.

Those disappointed, indifferent faces and eyes had been like sharp knives slicing his heart away piece by piece. His thoughts had run wild, and a dull ache had spread through his chest.

It was like falling from the clouds to the ground. The higher one stood, the harder one fell.

But in this life.

Standing once more in such a scene, Fang Yuan was utterly calm.

He thought of that legend. When predicaments come, entrust your heart to hope.

And now that hope lay within his own body. It was not a great hope, but it was already better than those with no aptitude for cultivation at all.

If others were disappointed by it, then let them be disappointed. What of it?

What is the disappointment of others to me? What matters is that one keeps hope in one's own heart!

Five hundred years of living had taught him one truth. What makes a life exciting is the pursuit of one's own dreams. There is no need to demand that others withhold their disappointment, or grant their approval.

Go your own way, and let others be disappointed, let them disapprove!

“Haa...” The academy elder let out a deep sigh, then called again. “Next. Gu Yue Fang Zheng.”

No one answered.

“Gu Yue Fang Zheng!” The elder shouted, and his voice went humming and echoing through the karst cavern.

“Ah? Here, I'm here!” Fang Zheng tore himself free of his shock and hurried forward, only to stagger underfoot. He went straight over in a tumble and fell with a splash, rolling, as luck would have it, right into the river.

At once, laughter broke out across the whole cavern.

“The Fang brothers. Nothing more than this.” The Gu Yue clan leader gave a cold snort, and a certain distaste for Gu Yue Fang Zheng rose in him as well.

“Now I've really disgraced myself!” Fang Zheng thrashed hard a few times in the water, but the riverbed was slick and he could not get to his feet at all. All his effort only made him look clumsier. Burst after burst of laughter reached his ears, and he grew more flustered still.

But just then, he suddenly felt a great force yank him. His head came clear of the water at last, and his body found its balance again.

He wiped his face in a sorry state and looked again, and saw that it was his elder brother Fang Yuan, gripping him by the collar and hauling him up.

“Brother...” He opened his mouth to speak, and got a throatful of water for it, which set off a violent fit of coughing.

“Haha, the Fang brothers, a matched pair of wretches!” Someone on the bank jeered aloud.

The laughter grew louder still, and the academy elder did not step forward to stop it. He frowned deeply, filled with disappointment.

Fang Zheng was at a complete loss, and then his brother's voice came at his ear. “Go on. The road to the future will be exciting.”

Fang Zheng's mouth fell open in surprise. Fang Yuan had his back to the crowd just then, so no one on the bank could see clearly. But Fang Zheng felt his calm distinctly, and saw that as he spoke the corners of his mouth even curved faintly upward, letting slip a deep, savouring trace of a smile.

“He's plainly only a Grade C aptitude. Why is my elder brother still so calm?” Fang Zheng filled with doubt in spite of himself.

Fang Yuan said nothing further. He patted Fang Zheng on the back, turned, and walked away.

Still dazed, Fang Zheng walked toward the flower field.

“I'd never have thought my elder brother could be this calm. If it were me, I'm afraid...” His head was down and he walked on without thinking, not knowing that he was in the middle of staging a miracle.

By the time he came to himself with a start, he was standing deep in the flower field, at a distance no one before him had reached.

Forty-three steps!

“Heavens, a Grade A aptitude!!!” The academy elder cried out, all composure gone.

“Grade A? Truly Grade A?!”

“Three years! At last the Gu Yue clan has produced another genius of Grade A aptitude!”

The elders watching in secret cried out at the same moment, their dignity abandoned entirely.

“Mm. The Fang lineage split off from our Chi lineage to begin with, so this Gu Yue Fang Zheng will be taken in by my Chi lineage.” Gu Yue Chi Lian announced it on the spot.

“How could that be? What virtue, what ability do you have, Chi Lian, you old ghost? Hampering other people's children is the one talent you do have. Better to hand the child to me, Gu Yue Mo Chen, to bring up.” Gu Yue Mo Chen roared it out at once, as though he had touched a live wire.

“None of you will contend for him. Whoever grooms this child, no one will do it as well as this clan leader grooming him in person. Anyone who objects, objects to me, Gu Yue Bo!” The Gu Yue clan leader's eyes were bloodshot, his earlier disappointment and dejection swept clean away, and he had gone entirely into a frenzy.

## Notes on presentation

**Scene breaks.** Three spaced asterisks is the workhorse in published English fiction. Unambiguous, survives reflow on a phone, reads instantly as a break. A blank line alone is elegant in print but vanishes at the bottom of a screen. A horizontal rule reads as a section division, too heavy for a beat inside a scene.

**栽培 at L73 and L74.** These are two different words and an earlier pass flattened both to raise. L73 is 抚养, plain childrearing, bring up. L74 is 栽培, the gardening verb extended to people, which Han Dian gives as train, foster, educate, and then as an old officialdom word for taking someone up and promoting them. Groom carries both the training and the patronage.
