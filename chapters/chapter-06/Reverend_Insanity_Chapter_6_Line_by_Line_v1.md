### L00

**Original:** 空窍玄妙异常，虽然寄托在方源体内，但是却不和五脏六腑同处一个空间。你可以说它无限大，又可以说它无限小。

**Literal:** The aperture is exceedingly mysterious and strange. Although it is lodged inside Fang Yuan's body, it does not occupy the same space as the five zang and six fu organs. You may say it is infinitely large, and you may equally say it is infinitely small.

**Judgment:** 玄妙异常 is 'profound and mysterious to an unusual degree', not 'bizarre'. 五脏六腑 is the standard Chinese medical inventory of five zang and six fu organs, used here simply to mean the body's internal organs, so I render the sense rather than the enumeration. The two 'you can say' clauses are a deliberate paradox and must both survive.

**Proposal:** The aperture was a thing of surpassing mystery. Though it was lodged within Fang Yuan's body, it did not share the same space as his internal organs. You could call it infinitely vast, and you could just as well call it infinitely small.

**Source:** zdic 空窍: 'the body's orifices, such as ears, eyes, mouth and nose', citing Han Feizi, 空窍者，神明之户牖也. This confirms 空窍 is an established anatomical word for an opening in the body, which is why 'aperture' is the right register rather than a coined term. https://www.zdic.net/hans/空窍

**Original:** “怎么可能？你赤练老鬼何德何能，误人子弟的本事倒是不错。这个孩子还不如交给我古月漠尘抚养。”古月漠尘像触电一般，立即咆哮起来。

**Literal:** 'How is that possible? You, Chi Lian, you old ghost, what virtue and what ability have you? Your skill at ruining other people's children is quite good, though. This child would be better handed to me, Gu Yue Mo Chen, to raise.' Gu Yue Mo Chen, as if electrified, immediately began to roar.

**Judgment:** Two research points. First, 何德何能 is normally a formula of self-deprecation, 'what merit or ability have I to deserve this', and Mo Chen weaponises it by turning it on someone else, which makes it a sneer rather than modesty. Second, 误人子弟 is the specific idiom for an incompetent teacher wrecking his students, so the insult is precisely aimed at Chi Lian's fitness to raise a prodigy. 老鬼 is 'old ghost', a rough insult between peers. 触电一般 is an explicitly modern simile, an electric shock; the narration has already accepted modern framing such as percentages, so I keep it rather than substituting a period-appropriate image, but flag it for your ruling.

**Proposal:** “How could that be? What virtue, what ability do you have, Chi Lian, you old devil? Ruining the young is the one talent you do possess. Better to hand the child to me, Gu Yue Mo Chen, to raise.” Gu Yue Mo Chen roared it out at once, as though he had touched a live wire.

**Source:** zdic 何德何能: 'What virtue? What ability? Usually a humble way of saying that one is not fit to accept some task or position', with the example 'What virtue or ability have I, that so heavy a charge should be entrusted to me!' The entry's note that the phrase is normally self-directed is what establishes the aggressive inversion here. https://www.zdic.net/hans/何德何能 | zdic 误人子弟: 'a teacher who, through insufficient learning or lack of conscientiousness, holds his students back', English 'to hamper students' progress, to lead people astray', citing Li Ruzhen's Jinghua Yuan ch.19; a second sense covers corrupting the young through bad example. https://www.zdic.net/hans/误人子弟

### L74

**Original:** “谁都别争了。这个孩子谁栽培，都没有本族长亲自栽培的好。谁有异议，就是反对我古月博！”古月族长双眼赤红，一扫先前的失望颓废，整个人都癫狂了。

**Literal:** 'None of you argue. Whoever cultivates this child, none is as good as this clan leader cultivating him personally. Whoever has an objection, is opposing me, Gu Yue Bo!' The Gu Yue Clan leader's eyes were red, sweeping away his earlier disappointment and dejection; the whole person had gone mad.

**Judgment:** 栽培 is the gardener's word for raising a plant, used of grooming a protege, and it chimes with the flower field the boy is standing in. 本族长 is him referring to himself by office, which is a claim of authority, so 'this clan leader' should be kept rather than smoothed to 'I'. 癫狂 is clinical in origin, a term of Chinese medicine for derangement, and colloquially describes someone beside themselves with excitement; here both readings are active and 'frenzy' carries them.

**Proposal:** “None of you will contend for him. Whoever raises this child, no one will do it as well as this clan leader raising him in person. Anyone who objects, objects to me, Gu Yue Bo!” The Gu Yue Clan leader's eyes were bloodshot, his earlier disappointment and dejection swept clean away, and the whole man had gone into a frenzy.

**Source:** zdic 癫狂: 'a pathological state of mental derangement and abnormal speech and behaviour', English 'demented; mad', and a second sense 'describing a state of extreme excitement to the point of forgetting oneself', English 'frantic; ecstatic'; the encyclopaedia entry treats 癫狂 as a named disorder in Chinese medicine. https://www.zdic.net/hans/癫狂

### *Chapter 6: The Road Ahead Will Be Exciting*

The aperture was a thing of surpassing mystery. Though it was lodged within Fang Yuan's body, it did not share the same space as his internal organs. You could call it infinitely vast, and you could just as well call it infinitely small.

Some called it the Purple Palace. Others called it the Flowery Pool. Most, however, called it the Primeval Sea Aperture.

The aperture as a whole was spherical. White light flowed across its surface, forming a membrane of light. This was the Hope Gu from before, burst apart and congealed into a single layer of light.

It was precisely this membrane of light that held the aperture up and kept it from collapsing.

Within the aperture, naturally, lay the primeval sea.

The water lay smooth as a mirror, a sheet of jade green, yet immensely thick, and it carried a copper lustre.

This was the bronze primeval essence that only a Rank 1 Gu Master possessed, congealed together. Commonly, it was called the bronze sea.

The surface of the sea did not reach half the height of the aperture. It stood at only forty-four percent. This too was the limit of a Grade C aptitude.

Every drop of that water was primeval essence. It represented the congealing of Fang Yuan's essence, qi and spirit, and it stood for the vital potential he had accumulated over fifteen years.

It was with this primeval essence that a Gu Master drove his Gu worms. Which was to say, from this moment on, Fang Yuan had formally stepped into the ranks of Rank 1 Gu Masters.

The aperture was open. No more Hope Gu flowed into Fang Yuan's body.

Fang Yuan withdrew his attention. He felt only that the pressure ahead was as thick and solid as a wall, and that he could not advance another step.

“Just as in my previous life.” He gave a calm smile at the result.

“Can you go no further?” The academy elder called from across the river, clinging to a hope of one in ten thousand.

Fang Yuan simply turned and walked back, answering him by action.

At that, even the youths finally understood.

At once there was a rising buzz, and the crowd erupted.

“What? Fang Yuan walked twenty-seven steps?”

“So he only has a Grade C aptitude?!”

“Unbelievable. A genius like him, only a Grade C?”

An uproar swept through the crowd.

“Brother...” In the crowd, Gu Yue Fang Zheng raised his head and stared in shock at Fang Yuan wading back across the river. He could not believe the scene before him. His own brother was only a Grade C?

He had always believed his brother would be a Grade A aptitude.

No, not only he. His uncle and aunt, and many others in the clan, had believed the same.

And yet now, this was the result!

“Damn it. Only a Grade C!” The Gu Yue Clan leader clenched his fists in secret and let out a deep sigh, his disappointment plain beyond any words.

Among the elders watching in secret, some furrowed their brows, some lowered their heads in discussion, and some sighed long at the heavens.

“Could the test be in error?”

“How could it be? The method is beyond precise, and with us watching throughout, even cheating would be difficult.”

“But then how do you explain his earlier performance, and his talent?”

“The higher a youth's primeval sea aptitude, the more he will indeed display qualities beyond the ordinary. Intelligence, comprehension, memory, strength, agility and so on. But the reverse does not hold. Those qualities do not mean the aptitude must be high. In the end, the test result is what decides.”

“Haa. The greater the hope, the greater the disappointment. The Gu Yue Clan grows worse with every generation.”

* * *

The icy river water soaked through his socks, cold enough to bite at the bone.

Fang Yuan walked on, his face as expressionless as ever. The distance closed, and he could see clearly the heavy look on the academy elder's face, and could feel keenly the gazes of more than a hundred youths turned upon him.

In those gazes there was astonishment, there was shock, there was mockery, there was delight at his misfortune, there was sudden understanding, and there was cold indifference.

The scene was identical, and it drew Fang Yuan's thoughts back to his previous life.

Back then it had felt as though the sky itself were falling. Crossing the river he had lost his footing and fallen, soaked to the skin, his wits scattered. And not one person had reached out to steady him.

Those disappointed, indifferent faces and eyes had been like sharp knives slicing his heart away piece by piece. His thoughts had run wild, and a dull ache had spread through his chest.

It was like falling from the clouds to the ground. The higher one stood, the harder one fell.

But in this life.

Standing once more in such a scene, Fang Yuan's heart was utterly calm.

He thought of that legend. When predicaments come, entrust your heart to hope.

And now that hope lay within his own body. It was not a great hope, but it was already better than those with no aptitude for cultivation at all.

If others were disappointed by it, then let them be disappointed. What of it?

What is the disappointment of others to me? What matters is that I keep hope in my own heart!

Five hundred years of living had taught him one truth. What makes a life exciting is the pursuit of one's own dreams. There is no need to demand that others withhold their disappointment, or grant their approval.

Go your own way, and let others be disappointed, let them disapprove!

“Haa...” The academy elder let out a deep sigh, then called again. “Next. Gu Yue Fang Zheng.”

No one answered.

“Gu Yue Fang Zheng!” The elder shouted, and his voice went echoing and humming through the karst cavern.

“Ah? Here, I'm here!” Fang Zheng tore himself free of his shock and hurried forward, only to stagger underfoot. He went straight over in a tumble and fell with a splash, rolling, as luck would have it, right into the river.

At once the whole cavern burst out laughing.

“The Fang brothers. Nothing more than this.” The Gu Yue Clan leader gave a cold snort, and a certain distaste for Gu Yue Fang Zheng rose in him as well.

“Now I've really disgraced myself!” Fang Zheng thrashed hard a few times in the water, but the riverbed was slick and he could not get to his feet at all. All his effort only made him look clumsier. Burst after burst of laughter reached his ears, and his heart grew more flustered still.

But just then he felt a great strength seize hold of him. His head finally came clear of the water, and his body found its balance again.

He wiped his face in a sorry state and looked again, and saw that it was his brother Fang Yuan, gripping him by the collar and hauling him up.

“Brother...” He opened his mouth to speak, and got a throatful of water for it, which set off a violent fit of coughing.

“Haha, the Fang brothers, a matched pair of wretches!” Someone on the bank jeered aloud.

The laughter grew louder still, and the academy elder did not step forward to stop it. He frowned deeply, his heart full of disappointment.

Fang Zheng was at a complete loss, and then his brother's voice came at his ear. “Go on. The road ahead will be exciting.”

Fang Zheng's mouth fell open in surprise. Fang Yuan had his back to the crowd just then, so no one on the bank could see clearly. But Fang Zheng felt his calm distinctly, and saw that as he spoke the corners of his mouth even curved faintly upward, letting slip a deep, savouring trace of a smile.

“He's plainly only a Grade C aptitude. Why is my brother still so calm?” Fang Zheng's heart filled with doubt in spite of himself.

Fang Yuan said nothing further. He patted Fang Zheng on the back, turned, and walked away.

Fang Zheng, his expression blank, walked toward the flower field.

“I'd never have thought my brother could be this calm. If it were me, I'm afraid...” His head was down and he walked on without thinking, not knowing that he was in the middle of staging a miracle.

By the time he came to himself with a start, he was standing deep in the flower field, at a distance no one before him had reached.

Forty-three steps!

“Heavens, a Grade A aptitude!!!” The academy elder cried out, all composure gone.

“Grade A? Truly Grade A?!”

“Three years! At last the Gu Yue Clan has produced another genius of Grade A aptitude!”

The elders watching in secret cried out at the same moment, their dignity abandoned entirely.

“Mm. The Fang lineage split off from our Chi lineage to begin with, so this Gu Yue Fang Zheng will be taken in by my Chi lineage.” Gu Yue Chi Lian announced it on the spot.

“How could that be? What virtue, what ability do you have, Chi Lian, you old devil? Ruining the young is the one talent you do possess. Better to hand the child to me, Gu Yue Mo Chen, to raise.” Gu Yue Mo Chen roared it out at once, as though he had touched a live wire.

“None of you will contend for him. Whoever raises this child, no one will do it as well as this clan leader raising him in person. Anyone who objects, objects to me, Gu Yue Bo!” The Gu Yue Clan leader's eyes were bloodshot, his earlier disappointment and dejection swept clean away, and the whole man had gone into a frenzy.

## Open questions for you

1. **精彩 across title, L46 and L60.** *Exciting* in all three, or *remarkable* in all three? I lean to *exciting*.
2. **L20, 轩然大波.** I dropped the literal wave for *uproar*, on your standing instruction against repeated wave imagery and because the primeval sea is only sixteen lines above. Confirm, or restore the wave.
3. **L32, the ellipsis paragraph.** Presented as `* * *` in the clean text purely so the break is visible. We never agreed a house style for this. Options are a bare ellipsis, a rule, or white space.
4. **L58, 难兄难弟.** English cannot hold both the complimentary and the contemptuous reading at once. I went with *a matched pair of wretches*. Alternatives: *two of a kind*, *a fine pair of brothers* played as sarcasm, or *brothers in misfortune*.
5. **L73, 触电一般.** The simile is an electric shock, which is anachronistic for the setting but consistent with a narrator who elsewhere uses percentages. Keep *as though he had touched a live wire*, or naturalise it?
6. **L74, 栽培.** Literally the gardener's verb for raising a plant, and the boy is standing in a flower field as it is said. I used *raise* twice for plainness. *Cultivate* would preserve the horticultural pun at some cost in naturalness.
