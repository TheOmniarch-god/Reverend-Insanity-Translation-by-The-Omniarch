# Line 26

1. Original

正举步维艰之时，脚畔的花海中忽然浮起一蓬光点，光点很稀薄，呈现素白之色。

2. Literal breakdown

• 正……之时 — Just as; at the moment when.
• 举步维艰 — Found it difficult to take a step.
• 脚畔的花海中 — Among the flowers beside his feet.
• 忽然浮起 — Suddenly rose or floated up.
• 一蓬光点 — A cluster of points of light.
• 很稀薄 — Sparse; thinly distributed.
• 素白之色 — Plain, pure white.

3. Translation judgment

The lights rise from the flowers beside his feet, not from the river. 一蓬 suggests a cluster, while 稀薄 qualifies it as sparse rather than dense.

Their identity is explained later. Preserve the current reveal order instead of naming them Hope Gu here. “Pure-white” describes their color, not a moral or spiritual purity.

4. Proposed rendering

As he struggled to take another step, a sparse cluster of pure-white lights suddenly rose from the flowers beside his feet.

# Line 27

1. Original

光辉向陈博汇集过去，并投入到他的体内。

2. Literal breakdown

• 光辉 — The light or radiance; here, referring back to the points of light.
• 向陈博 — Toward Chen Bo.
• 汇集过去 — Gathered or converged upon him.
• 并 — And.
• 投入到他的体内 — Entered into his body.

3. Translation judgment

The preceding sentence establishes individual points of light, so “the lights” maintains that reference naturally.

体内 means inside his body. No particular entry point or destination within it is given here. Do not insert pores, the chest, the abdomen, or the aperture into this sentence.

4. Proposed rendering

The lights converged on Chen Bo and entered his body.

他咬牙用力向前走，硬生生的挤进去。走了三步之后，前方的压力又大增，一如之前如墙壁一样，不能再进分毫。

2. Literal breakdown

• 咬牙 — Clenched his teeth.

• 一如之前如墙壁一样 — Like a wall, just as before.

The steps belong to his advance through the flower field, not his earlier river crossing.

4. Proposed rendering

Gritting his teeth, he strained forward, forcing his way through the resistance. After three steps, the pressure ahead increased sharply again. It was like a wall once more; he could not advance any farther.

The passage distinguishes the general expectation from the Gu Yue clan’s higher proportion.
