# Chapter 4 — reconstructed review, Lines 26–75

Fresh reconstruction, not a verbatim recovery of the original pre-review messages.

# Line 26

1. Original

正举步维艰之时，脚畔的花海中忽然浮起一蓬光点，光点很稀薄，呈现素白之色。

2. Literal breakdown

• 正……之时 — Just as; at the moment when.
• 举步维艰 — Found it difficult to take a step.
• 脚畔的花海中 — Among the flowers beside his feet.
• 忽然浮起 — Suddenly rose or floated up.
• 一蓬光点 — A cluster of points of light.
• 很稀薄 — Sparse; thinly distributed.
• 素白之色 — Plain, pure white.

3. Translation judgment

The lights rise from the flowers beside his feet, not from the river. 一蓬 suggests a cluster, while 稀薄 qualifies it as sparse rather than dense.

Their identity is explained later. Preserve the current reveal order instead of naming them Hope Gu here. “Pure-white” describes their color, not a moral or spiritual purity.

4. Proposed rendering

As he struggled to take another step, a sparse cluster of pure-white lights suddenly rose from the flowers beside his feet.
