## Chapter 2: Reversing Time, Five Hundred Years of Enlightenment

Reverend Insanity (蛊真人), Chapter 2: Master Literary Edition
**Original Work:** 《蛊真人》 by Gu Zhen Ren (蛊真人)
**Edition:** Uncensored ZhiXuan Base (知轩藏书 / 网友精校恢复版)
**Chapter Title:** 第二节：逆光阴五百年觉悟 (Chapter 2: Reversing Time, Five Hundred Years of Enlightenment)
---
* **River of Time (光阴之河):** The cosmic river flowing through the world.
* **Spring Autumn Cicada (春秋蝉):** Ranked seventh among the Ten Mystical Gu.
* **Refine (炼 / 炼制):** Exclusively “refine” (never “forge”).
* **Demonic Path (魔道) / Righteous Path (正道):** Foundational ideological polarity.
* **The Great Dao of Eternal Life (长生大道):** Fang Yuan’s ultimate teleological pursuit.
* **Aperture Opening Ceremony (开窍大典):** The pivotal clan ritual.
* **Qing Mao Mountain (青茅山):** Standardized geographic designation.
---
In legends, there exists a River of Time in this world, supporting the flow of all existence. And by utilizing the power of the Spring Autumn Cicada, one can travel upstream against the current, returning to the past. Regarding this rumor, the world was divided in opinion. Many did not believe it, while some half-believed and half-doubted. Almost no one was truly certain. Because every time the Spring Autumn Cicada was used, one had to pay with their life, sacrificing their entire body and all of their cultivation base as the driving power. This price was truly too steep, and what made it even harder to accept was that even after paying with one’s life, the outcome remained unknown.
Even if someone obtained the Spring Autumn Cicada, they would never dare use it casually on a whim. What if the rumor was false, nothing more than a hoax? If Fang Yuan had not been driven to a dead end, he would not have used it so readily. However, now, Fang Yuan believed completely and thoroughly. Because the ironclad truth lay before his eyes, beyond all dispute. He had indeed been reborn!
“It is only a pity for such a good Gu. Back then, it took monumental effort, the slaughter of hundreds of thousands of people, stirring the wrath of heaven and the resentment of men, enduring endless tribulations before I finally refined it…” Fang Yuan sighed inwardly. Though he had been reborn, the Spring Autumn Cicada had not traveled back with him.
Humans are the spirit of all living things; Gu are the essence of heaven and earth. Gu were bizarre and varied, countless in number. Some Gu would completely dissipate after being used once or twice. Other Gu, as long as they were not used excessively, could be used repeatedly. Perhaps the Spring Autumn Cicada was simply that kind of expendable Gu that could only be used once.
“However, even if it is gone, I can simply refine another one. If I could refine it in my past life, could I not in this life?” Pity aside, a surge of ambitious pride could not help rising in Fang Yuan’s heart.
To be able to be reborn, the loss of the Spring Autumn Cicada was entirely acceptable. Moreover, he still carried a priceless treasure; he was far from empty-handed. This treasure was his five hundred years of memories and experience. Within his memory lay countless secret troves, currently unopened by anyone. There lay major historical events one by one, allowing him to easily grasp the pulse of history. There lay countless figures: some were hidden experts who cultivate in deep seclusion, some were extraordinary prodigies, and some were not even born yet. There also lay the grueling experience of bitter cultivation over these five hundred years, and a rich reservoir of battle experience.
With these, he undoubtedly held the overall situation and the initiative. As long as he played his cards well, roaming the mortal realm and recreating his former glory as a demonic overlord and ruthless hegemon would be no problem at all, and he could even take a step further, charging towards an even higher realm!
“Then how should I proceed…?” Fang Yuan was extremely rational. He quickly gathered his emotions and turned toward the night rain outside the window, falling into deep thought.
Thinking like this, he felt a thousand loose ends and ten thousand tangled threads. After pondering for a moment, his brow furrowed deeper and deeper. A span of five hundred years was truly rather long. Not to mention those memories that were already blurred and could no longer be recalled, even among the secret treasure grounds and immortal opportunities he did remember, although numerous, most were either separated by hundreds of thousands of miles, or required specific times to open.
“The most crucial factor is still cultivation base. Right now, my primeval sea is not yet open; I have not even stepped onto the cultivation path of a Gu Master. I am fundamentally a mortal! I must cultivate as quickly as possible and raise my cultivation base, moving ahead of history to seize the initiative as much as possible and grab enough benefits.”
Moreover, without sufficient cultivation, he could not make full use of many hidden troves even if he obtained them. Instead, they would become dangerous liabilities, merely possessing such treasures would make him a target. The first difficult problem placed before Fang Yuan was his cultivation base. He had to raise his cultivation base as quickly as possible; if he was as slow as in his past life, even the daylilies would have gone cold.
“To raise my cultivation base quickly, I must rely on the resources of the clan. In my current situation, I fundamentally lack the capability to traverse the peril-laden mountain ranges; even an ordinary wild boar could take my life. Only if I reach the cultivation base of a Rank 3 Gu Master will I have the baseline ability to protect myself and traverse the mountains and rivers of this world.”
Through the eyes of a demonic overlord tempered by five hundred years, this Qing Mao Mountain was truly too small, and Gu Yue Village was more like a cage. However, while a cage imprisons freedom, a sturdy cell often represents a kind of safety.
“Hmph. For now, I’ll make my moves within this cage. As long as I advance to Rank 3 Gu Master, I will leave this impoverished mountain backwater. Fortunately, tomorrow is the Aperture Opening Ceremony, and shortly after, I can officially begin cultivating as a Gu Master.”
At the thought of the Aperture Opening Ceremony, memories long buried in dust surfaced within Fang Yuan’s heart.
“Aptitude, is it…?” Looking outside the window, he could not help letting out three cold chuckles.
Just at this moment, the room door was gently pushed open, and a youth walked in.
“Brother, why are you standing by the window letting the rain fall on you?”
The youth was thin in build, slightly shorter than Fang Yuan, his face bearing an uncanny resemblance to his own. Fang Yuan turned his head to look at this youth, a trace of complexity flashing across his face.
“So it is you, my twin brother.” He raised his brows slightly, his expression returning to his habitual coldness.
Fang Zheng lowered his head, looking down at his toes, his trademark habit. “Seeing that brother’s window wasn’t closed, I wanted to slip in quietly and shut it. Tomorrow is the Aperture Opening Ceremony. Brother, if Uncle and Aunt find out you’re still up this late, I’m afraid they’ll worry.” He was not surprised by Fang Yuan’s coldness, because from childhood to now, his brother had always been like this. Sometimes he would think that perhaps geniuses were just different from ordinary mortals. Although he shared a nearly identical face with his brother, he felt as ordinary as an ant.
Born from the same womb at the same time, why was heaven so unfair? It had given his brother diamond-like talent, while he was ordinary like a roadside stone. Everyone around him, whenever mentioning him, would always say: “This is Fang Yuan’s brother.” His uncle and aunt also often lectured him: “You must learn from your brother.” Sometimes, even looking at his own face in the mirror filled him with a certain disgust! These thoughts had existed for many years, accumulating deep inside his heart day by day. They weighed on his chest like a boulder. Over the years, Fang Zheng’s head had hung lower and lower, and he had grown increasingly silent.
“Worry…?” Thinking of his uncle and aunt, Fang Yuan scoffed inwardly.
He remembered very clearly that the parents of this body had both fallen during a clan mission. When he was merely three years old, he and his brother had become orphans. Under the pretext of raising them, his uncle and aunt had openly and legally usurped their parents’ inheritance, and treated the two brothers harshly. Originally, as a transmigrator, he had planned to bide his time in obscurity. But the hardship of survival had forced Fang Yuan to choose displaying talent different from normal people. The so-called “genius” was actually nothing more than the rationality of a mature soul, paired with a few Tang poems and Song verses from Earth that echoed through generations.
Just testing his hands like this, he was amazed at as a heavenly being, receiving widespread attention. Under external pressure, it also made the young Fang Yuan have to choose a cold expression to disguise and protect himself, reducing the chance of exposing his secrets. Over time, coldness instead became his habitual expression. Just like this, his uncle and aunt could no longer treat him and his brother harshly. As his age grew and his future looked brighter, his treatment also increased along with it.
However, this was not love, but an investment.
The laughable thing was that this brother of his had never seen through this truth. Not only was he blinded by their uncle and aunt, but he had even harboured a buried resentment toward Fang Yuan. Don’t look at him being so well-behaved and honest now; in Fang Yuan’s memories, once he tested with Grade A aptitude and was heavily nurtured by the clan, all of his hidden hatred and jealousy had been released, and he had spared no effort in targeting, making things difficult for, and suppressing his own elder brother.
And as for his own aptitude…?
Heh. At best, merely a Grade C.
Fate always loved to play jokes.
Born as twins, the elder brother’s aptitude was only Grade C, yet monopolized the name of genius for over a decade. The younger brother had been unknown, yet possessed Grade A natural talent. The result of the Aperture Opening Ceremony had left the entire clan stunned. It also completely reversed the two brothers’ situation and treatment. The younger brother was like a crouching dragon ascending the heavens; the elder brother was like a fledgling phoenix crashing to the earth. After that, came repeated difficulties from his brother, cold eyes from his uncle and aunt, and contempt from the clansmen.
Did he hate them?
In his past life, Fang Yuan had hated. He hated his own insufficient aptitude, hated the clan’s heartlessness, and hated fate’s unfairness. But now, as he reexamined that part of his life with five hundred years of experience behind him, he felt not a ripple of hatred.
What was there to resent?
Thinking from their perspective, he could also understand his brother, his uncle and aunt, as well as those righteous powerhouses who besieged him five hundred years later. The strong devour the weak; survival of the fittest, that was the very nature of this world. Moreover, everyone has their own ambitions, all fighting for that sliver of heavenly opportunity. Between each other suppressing and killing, what was there not to understand? Five hundred years of experience had long made him see through all of this; in his heart, there remained only the Great Dao of Eternal Life. If someone stood in the way of this pursuit, no matter who, it was nothing more than: either you die or I live.
The wild ambition in his heart was too immense. Stepping onto this path, one was destined to make enemies of the entire world, destined to walk alone, and destined to face relentless tribulations.
This was the enlightenment distilled from five hundred years of life.
“Vengeance is not my intention, and the demonic path also never has the word compromise.” Thinking to this point, Fang Yuan could not help laughing. Turning his head toward this brother of his, taking a faint glance, he said: “You can go.”
Fang Zheng felt a sudden tremor in his heart, feeling that his brother’s gaze was as sharp as an icy blade, seeming to pierce into the deepest part of his heart. Under that gaze, he felt as if he were standing naked in the snow, every secret laid bare.
“Then… see you tomorrow, brother.” Daring not speak more right now, Fang Zheng slowly closed the room door, murmuring compliance as he retreated.


## Chapter 4: Gu Yue Fang Yuan!

The morning sun rose, the clouds aglow with brilliant light. The mountain mist was thin, and sunlight pierced it easily like a sharp sword. More than a hundred fifteen-year-old youths were now gathered before the Clan Leader’s Pavilion. The Clan Leader’s Pavilion stood at the very heart of the village, five stories high, with projected eaves and upturned corners. It was heavily guarded. A square lay before it, and inside were enshrined the spirit tablets of the Gu Yue ancestors. Each successive clan leader also lived there. Whenever an important ceremony or a major unforeseen event arose, the clan elders would be convened there to deliberate. It was the center of power for the entire village.
“Very good. You’ve all arrived on time. Today is the Aperture Opening Ceremony, a major turning point in your lives. Enough preliminaries. Follow me.” Leading them was the Academy Elder. His hair and beard were white, but he remained full of vigor as he led the youths into the Clan Leader’s Pavilion.
However, they did not go upstairs. Instead, they passed through an entrance in the ground-floor hall and headed down. They followed the stone steps down into an underground cavern. The youths exclaimed in wonder. The underground cavern was magnificent. Its stalactites radiated seven colours, red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, and violet, illuminating their faces with rainbow brilliance. Standing amid the crowd, Fang Yuan quietly examined everything, thinking inwardly: “Several hundred years ago, the Gu Yue clan migrated from Central Continent to Southern Border and settled on Qing Mao Mountain. They chose this place for a spirit spring in the underground cavern. That spring produces large quantities of primeval stones, it could be called the very foundation of Gu Yue Village.”
As they walked several hundred steps farther, their surroundings grew darker, and they began to hear the faint sound of water. Rounding a bend, they came upon an underground river just over ten metres wide. Here, the coloured light of the stalactites had vanished entirely. Yet in the darkness, the river gave off a faint blue glow, like the river of stars in the night sky. The river flowed from the cavern’s dark depths, its water so clear that they could see fish swimming within it, aquatic plants, and even the sand and stones on the riverbed.
On the opposite bank lay a field of flowers.
These were moon orchids, deliberately cultivated by the Gu Yue clan. Their crescent-shaped petals had a delicate blue-pink hue. Their stems resembled jade, and their centers shone with the soft luster of pearls catching the light. At first glance, against the dark backdrop, the flower field beside the river resembled a vast blue-green carpet studded with countless pearls.
“Moon orchids are food for many kinds of Gu. This flower field could be called the clan’s largest growing site.” Fang Yuan understood this perfectly. “So beautiful.” “It really is lovely.”
It was an eye-opening sight for the youths. Their eyes shone, each of them both excited and nervous.
“All right,” the elder said. “Listen as I call your names. When your name is called, cross the river to the opposite bank. Walk as far as you can, the farther, the better, of course. Has everyone understood?” “Understood,” the youths answered. Their families or seniors had already explained it before they came: the farther they walked, the better their aptitude, and the greater their future achievements would be.
“Gu Yue Chen Bo.” Holding the list, the elder called the first name.
Though wide, the river reached only to his knees. His expression solemn, Chen Bo stepped into the flower field on the opposite bank. He immediately felt an invisible pressure resisting his advance, as though a wall stood before him. As he struggled to take another step, a sparse cluster of pure-white lights suddenly rose from the flowers beside his feet. The lights converged on Chen Bo and entered his body. Chen Bo immediately felt the pressure ease considerably. The invisible wall suddenly seemed to soften. Gritting his teeth, he strained forward, forcing his way through the resistance. After three steps, the pressure ahead increased sharply again. It was like a wall once more; he could not advance any farther.
The elder sighed at the sight. As he recorded the result, he announced, “Gu Yue Chen Bo: three steps. No aptitude to become a Gu Master. Next, Gu Yue Zao Xie.” Chen Bo’s face immediately turned pale. Teeth clenched, he crossed back through the river to where he had stood before. Without aptitude, he could only live as a mortal, occupying the very bottom of the clan’s hierarchy. He swayed, nearly collapsing. The blow was devastating, as though it had crushed every hope he held for his life.
Many looked at him with pity, but even more had turned their attention to the second youth, who had reached the opposite bank. Unfortunately, this youth managed only four steps. He, too, lacked aptitude. Not everyone possessed the aptitude to cultivate. Generally, five people out of ten being able to cultivate was already considered good. In the Gu Yue clan, the proportion was somewhat higher: six out of ten. This was because the Gu Yue ancestor, the first clan leader, had been a renowned, legendary expert. His cultivation had caused genes carrying power to lie hidden within his bloodline. As his descendants, members of the Gu Yue clan generally possessed higher aptitude.
The two youths had proved, one after the other, to have no aptitude. The expressions of the other clan elders observing discreetly darkened. Even the seasoned, composed Gu Yue Clan Leader frowned slightly. Just then, the Academy Elder called out the third name: “Gu Yue Mo Bei.”
“Here!” A horse-faced youth in hemp clothing answered with a short shout and stepped out from the crowd. He was tall and broad, far sturdier than others his age, with a hint of fierceness about him.
In a few strides, he crossed the river and stepped onto the opposite bank. Ten steps, twenty steps, thirty steps, faint lights continued to enter his body. He reached thirty-six steps before he could go no farther. The youths across the river stared, their eyes wide and mouths agape. The Academy Elder cried out in delight, “Excellent! Gu Yue Mo Bei, Grade B aptitude! Come here and let me examine your primeval sea.” Gu Yue Mo Bei returned to the Academy Elder’s side. The elder rested a hand on his shoulder, closed his eyes, and concentrated as he examined him. Then he withdrew his hand, nodded, and wrote on the paper: “Gu Yue Mo Bei. Primeval sea: sixty-six percent. Worth substantial investment in his development.”
Aptitude was divided into four grades, from highest to lowest: A, B, C, and D. With about three years of training, a youth with Grade D aptitude could become a seasoned Rank 1 Gu Master, forming part of the clan’s foundation. After two years of training, most youths with Grade C aptitude could become Rank 2 Gu Masters, forming the backbone of the clan. A youth with Grade B aptitude warranted special care. Usually groomed as a future clan elder, such a youth could become a Rank 3 Gu Master in six or seven years.
As for Grade A aptitude, the emergence of even a single such talent was a stroke of good fortune for the entire clan. With attentive care and priority access to resources, such a youth could become a Rank 4 Gu Master in around ten years. At that point, they could compete to become clan leader! In other words, provided Gu Yue Mo Bei fulfilled his potential, he would become an elder of the Gu Yue clan. No wonder the Academy Elder laughed in delight. The elders observing discreetly all breathed a sigh of relief, then turned to one of their colleagues with envy.
Gu Yue Mo Chen had the same horse-like face. Already smiling, he cast a challenging glance at his longtime rival. “Well, Gu Yue Chi Lian? My grandson isn’t bad, is he?” The red-haired elder, Gu Yue Chi Lian, gave a cold snort but made no reply, his expression sullen and grim. An hour later, half the youths had entered the flower field. Quite a few had shown Grade C or Grade D aptitude, though nearly half of those tested had none at all.
“Alas, our bloodline grows ever more diluted, and few Rank 4 experts have emerged in recent years to strengthen it. The fourth clan leader was our only Rank 5 expert, but he perished alongside the Flower-Wine Monk and left no descendants. The aptitude of our younger generations keeps declining.” The clan leader sighed deeply.
Just then, the Academy Elder called, “Gu Yue Chi Cheng.”
At the name, the elders turned to look at Gu Yue Chi Lian. This was his grandson. Gu Yue Chi Cheng was short and slight, his face covered in pockmarks. With his fists clenched and sweat covering his face, he looked especially nervous. He stepped onto the opposite bank, and points of light entered his body one after another. He continued for thirty-six steps before stopping.
“Another Grade B!” the Academy Elder exclaimed.
A stir ran through the youths as they turned envious eyes toward Gu Yue Chi Cheng.
“Thirty-six steps! Thirty-six!” Gu Yue Chi Lian shouted with a triumphant laugh, glaring challengingly at Gu Yue Mo Chen.
This time, it was Gu Yue Mo Chen’s turn to look livid.
“Gu Yue Chi Cheng…” Amid the crowd, Fang Yuan thoughtfully rubbed his chin.
Fang Yuan remembered that Chi Cheng had been severely punished by the clan for cheating during the Aperture Opening Ceremony. His aptitude was actually only Grade C, but his grandfather, Gu Yue Chi Lian, had arranged the deception that made it appear to be Grade B. If Fang Yuan wanted to cheat, he had dozens of methods in mind, some more sophisticated than the one used for Gu Yue Chi Cheng. If he appeared to possess Grade B or even Grade A aptitude, the clan would certainly devote substantial resources to his development.
Firstly, too little time had passed since his rebirth. Given his circumstances, preparing such a deception would have been difficult. Secondly, even if he succeeded, he could not conceal his subsequent cultivation speed. The deception would still come to light. Gu Yue Chi Cheng, however, was different. His grandfather, Gu Yue Chi Lian, was one of the clan’s two most powerful elders and could cover for him.
“Gu Yue Chi Lian and Gu Yue Mo Chen have long been enemies. They head the clan’s two largest factions. To undermine his rival, Chi Lian needs his grandson to have outstanding aptitude. It was his protection behind the scenes that allowed Chi Cheng to conceal the truth for a time. As I remember it, the deception would never have come to light without that unexpected incident.”
A sharp gleam flickered in Fang Yuan’s eyes as he considered how to exploit this knowledge for the greatest possible benefit. Exposing the deception now would earn him a small reward from the clan, but it would also antagonize the influential, high-ranking Gu Yue Chi Lian. That was out of the question. Blackmail was no option for now, either. He was too weak; attempting it would only bring disaster upon himself. As he considered this, he suddenly heard the Academy Elder call his name:
“Gu Yue Fang Yuan!”


## Chapter 5: Ren Zu’s Three Gu, Hope Opens the Aperture

His surroundings fell quiet in an instant. Countless eyes turned toward him.
“This is getting more and more interesting.” Fang Yuan smiled inwardly. Under everyone’s watchful eyes, he waded across the river and stepped onto the opposite bank.
Immediately, he felt a layer of pressure bearing against him.
The pressure came from the spirit spring deep within the flower field. The primeval qi it produced was so concentrated and abundant that it generated this pressure. But soon, a cluster of lights rose from the flowers beside Fang Yuan’s feet. The lights drifted around him, enveloping his entire body, before every last one entered it.
“These are Hope Gu,” Fang Yuan murmured inwardly. The elder in charge had not explained, but Fang Yuan knew perfectly well.
Every point of light was an individual Gu.
Their name was Hope.
One of the oldest legends told of Hope Gu.
According to legend, when this world had just formed, it was a wilderness where wild beasts roamed unchecked. The first human came into being, and his name was Ren Zu. He lived a primitive existence, eating raw flesh and drinking blood. Life was exceedingly difficult. In particular, there was a group of wild beasts called Predicaments. They found Ren Zu’s scent especially enticing and wanted to devour him. Ren Zu had neither a body as tough as mountain rock nor the sharp teeth and claws of wild beasts. How could he contend with this pack of beasts called Predicaments? His food supply was unreliable, and he spent his days hiding from place to place. At the lower end of nature’s food chain, he was almost at the point where he could no longer survive.
At this time, three Gu sought out Ren Zu of their own accord and said, “As long as you sustain us with your life, we will help you overcome your difficulties.” With nowhere else to turn, Ren Zu had no choice but to agree to the three Gu’s terms. First, he sustained the largest of the three Gu with his youth. That Gu gave him strength. With strength, Ren Zu’s life began to improve. He gained a reliable source of food and the strength to protect himself. He took eagerly to fighting and defeated many Predicaments. But he soon suffered for it. Eventually, he discovered that strength was not all-powerful: it required rest and recovery and could not be squandered at will.
Moreover, his strength alone was far too little against all the Predicament beasts together. Once his suffering had eased, Ren Zu reflected on what he had endured. He decided to sustain the most beautiful of the three Gu with his middle years, when he was in the prime of his strength.
Then the second Gu gave him wisdom.
With wisdom, Ren Zu learned to think and reflect on his actions, and began accumulating experience. He discovered that wisdom was often more effective than strength. With both, he was able, for a time, to overcome much that had previously been beyond him and kill more Predicaments. He ate their flesh and drank their blood, tenaciously surviving in this way. But the good times did not last. Ren Zu grew old, older and older. This was because he had offered his youth and middle years to Strength Gu and Wisdom Gu.
Once a person grows old, their muscles waste away and their mind no longer works as quickly.
“Human, what can you still give us? You have nothing left to offer us.” Once Strength Gu and Wisdom Gu realised this, they both abandoned him without compassion.
Ren Zu had lost strength and wisdom. The Predicaments found him again and surrounded him. He was old and could no longer run. All his teeth had fallen out; he could not even chew wild fruit or edible greens. He collapsed helplessly to the ground, Predicaments crowding all around him. His heart was filled with despair. Just then, the third Gu said, “Human, sustain me, and I can free you from these Predicaments.”
Ren Zu said through his tears, “Gu, what could I possibly have left? Look, Strength Gu and Wisdom Gu have both abandoned me. All I have left is my old age. Compared with youth and middle age, it may be worth little, but if I offer even that to you, my life will end immediately. Though the Predicaments surround me, I will not die just yet. I want to live a little longer, even if only for a single second. So go. I can no longer sustain you.” But the Gu said, “Of the three of us, I ask for the least. Human, all you need to do is give me your heart.”
“Then I’ll give you my heart,” Ren Zu said. “But, Gu, what can you give me? In this desperate situation, even if Strength Gu and Wisdom Gu returned to my side, they could not change anything.”
Beside Strength Gu, this Gu had the frailest body: nothing more than a tiny point of light. Beside Wisdom Gu, it was the dimmest, giving off only a faint white glow, without any splendor or brilliance. But when the human gave his heart to the Gu, it suddenly blazed with boundless light. Amid that light, the Predicaments cried out in terror: “It’s Hope Gu! Retreat! We Predicaments fear hope most of all!”
The Predicament beasts immediately retreated in panic.
Ren Zu stared, eyes wide and mouth agape. From that moment on, he understood: when facing predicaments, give your heart to hope. Meanwhile, the Hope Gu had gathered into a stream of light and entered Fang Yuan’s body. Under the external pressure, they quickly gathered in Fang Yuan’s abdomen, clustering of their own accord roughly ten centimeters below his navel. Fang Yuan immediately felt the pressure ease slightly. He stepped forward and continued on. With every step he took, more Hope Gu rose from the flower field, entered his body, and joined the mass of light.
The mass of light grew larger and brighter.
But across the river, the elder in charge frowned.
“There seem to be rather few Hope Gu.” At the sight, the hearts of the many elders who had been discreetly watching Fang Yuan sank.
The Clan Leader frowned as well. These were certainly not the signs one would expect from Grade A aptitude!
Fang Yuan continued forward against the pressure.
“Fewer than ten steps means no aptitude for cultivation. Ten to twenty steps means Grade D; twenty to thirty, Grade C; thirty to forty, Grade B; forty to fifty, Grade A. So far, I have taken twenty-three steps.” “Twenty-four, twenty-five, twenty-six… twenty-seven.” Fang Yuan counted silently. At his twenty-seventh step, he seemed to hear a boom: the mass of light in his abdomen, between his kidneys, had reached its limit and suddenly burst.
The burst occurred entirely within Fang Yuan’s body; no one else could detect it. Only he experienced its mysterious, earth-shaking wonder in that instant. In an instant, the fine hairs all over his body stood on end and his pores closed tightly. His mind drew taut like a bowstring suddenly pulled back. Immediately afterward, his mind went blank and his whole body relaxed, as though he had fallen into the clouds. The tension within him eased, his body hair settled, and every pore opened.
All at once, a light sheen of perspiration covered his body.
Though it takes some time to describe, the entire process was exceedingly brief. It came swiftly and ended just as abruptly.
Fang Yuan was dazed for only an instant before collecting himself.
He quietly concentrated and examined within his body. An aperture had appeared out of nowhere in his abdomen, below his navel and between his kidneys.
His aperture had opened successfully!
This was the hope of immortality!
